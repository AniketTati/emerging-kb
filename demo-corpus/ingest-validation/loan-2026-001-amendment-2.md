# Amendment No. 2 to Term Loan Agreement

This Amendment No. 2 (the "Second Amendment") further modifies the term loan
facility extended by **HDFC** to **Acme Manufacturing**. It supersedes
Amendment No. 1 dated 1 March 2026.

## 1. Revised Interest

Effective 1 June 2026, the post-amendment rate is **9.4% per annum**. This is
the current rate in force for the facility.

## 2. Status

This Second Amendment is the current, live version of the loan facility. The
original agreement and Amendment No. 1 are superseded.

The principal of ₹2.2 crore and the 60-month tenure remain unchanged.
