# Term Loan Agreement

This Term Loan Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 15 January 2026
between **Acme Manufacturing Pvt Ltd** ("the Borrower"), a company incorporated
in Pune, and **HDFC Bank** ("the Lender").

## 1. Facility

The Lender agrees to extend to the Borrower a term loan with a principal amount
of **₹2.2 crore** (Rupees Two Crore Twenty Lakh only). The facility is secured
against the Borrower's plant and machinery.

## 2. Interest

The all-in interest rate is **8.5% per annum**, benchmarked to 1Y MCLR plus a
spread of 200 basis points. Interest accrues monthly on the outstanding
principal.

## 3. Repayment

The equated monthly installment (EMI) is **₹4,52,000**, payable on the 5th of
each month. The loan tenure is **60 months** from the disbursement date.

## 4. Parties

Borrower: Acme Manufacturing Pvt Ltd, Pune.
Lender: HDFC Bank, Mumbai branch.

This Agreement is the original facility and may be amended by written addendum.
