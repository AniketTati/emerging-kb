# Amendment No. 1 to Term Loan Agreement

This Amendment No. 1 (the "First Amendment") modifies the Term Loan Agreement
dated 15 January 2026 between **Acme Manufacturing Pvt Ltd** ("the Borrower")
and **HDFC Bank** ("the Lender").

## 1. Revised Interest

Effective 1 March 2026, the interest rate is revised to **9.0% per annum**. The
revision reflects a 50 basis point increase in the underlying 1Y MCLR
benchmark.

## 2. Unchanged Terms

The principal amount of ₹2.2 crore, the EMI of ₹4,52,000, and the 60-month
tenure of the original agreement remain unchanged. All other terms and
conditions of the original Term Loan Agreement continue in full force.

This First Amendment supersedes Section 2 (Interest) of the original Agreement.
