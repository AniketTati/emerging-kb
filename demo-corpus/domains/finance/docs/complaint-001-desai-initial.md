## Nature of Complaint

**Category:** Wrongful debit / wire transfer dispute
**Sub-category:** Cross-border outward remittance

**Brief description (verbatim from filing):**

"I initiated an outward wire transfer of USD 142,000 on 6 February 2025 at 14:18 IST through your NetBanking corporate portal (transaction reference HDFC-WIR-2025-02-184228), beneficiary: my US-based investment manager Hudson Bay Holdings LLC, NY, USA. The transaction was confirmed on screen and an SMS confirmation was sent. However:

(1) The same transaction was duplicated on 7 February 2025 at 09:42 IST without my authorisation. A second USD 142,000 was debited from my account (reference HDFC-WIR-2025-02-184229);

(2) I received no SMS or email about the second transaction. The duplicate was discovered when I logged in on 11 Feb 2025 to verify my balance;

(3) My total loss is USD 142,000 (~INR 1.18 crore at current rates). The duplicate transfer has not been recalled despite my immediate call to RM Mr. Raj Mehta on 11 February 2025 evening.

I demand: (i) immediate recall of the duplicate transfer; (ii) full refund of the USD 142,000 + applicable interest; (iii) written explanation of how a duplicate transaction was processed without authorisation; (iv) escalation to RBI Banking Ombudsman if not resolved within statutory 30 days."
