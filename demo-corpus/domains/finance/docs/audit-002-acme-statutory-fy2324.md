## Report on the Audit of the Standalone Financial Statements

### Opinion

We have audited the standalone financial statements of **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("the Company"), which comprise the Balance Sheet as at 31 March 2024, the Statement of Profit and Loss (including Other Comprehensive Income), the Statement of Cash Flows, and the Statement of Changes in Equity for the year then ended, and notes to the financial statements, including a summary of significant accounting policies and other explanatory information.

In our opinion and to the best of our information and according to the explanations given to us, the aforesaid standalone financial statements give the information required by the Companies Act, 2013 ("the Act") in the manner so required and **give a true and fair view** in conformity with the Indian Accounting Standards prescribed under Section 133 of the Act read with the Companies (Indian Accounting Standards) Rules, 2015, as amended ("Ind AS"), and other accounting principles generally accepted in India, of the state of affairs of the Company as at 31 March 2024, and its profit (including other comprehensive income), changes in equity, and its cash flows for the year then ended.

### Basis for Opinion

We conducted our audit of the standalone financial statements in accordance with the Standards on Auditing (SAs) issued by ICAI. We are independent of the Company in accordance with the Code of Ethics issued by the ICAI together with the ethical requirements that are relevant to our audit of the standalone financial statements under the provisions of the Act and the Rules thereunder, and we have fulfilled our other ethical responsibilities in accordance with these requirements. We believe that the audit evidence we have obtained is sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for our opinion.

### Key Audit Matters

| Matter | Why considered | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue recognition — multi-year SaaS contracts | Significant judgment on performance obligation timing; material to financials | Tested 24 of the largest contracts (representing 78% of Q4 revenue); reviewed POC schedules + customer correspondence; concurred with Company's revenue cut-off |
| Capitalisation of internally generated software | Material balance; significant management judgment on development-vs-research phase classification | Reviewed 8 capex projects (representing 84% of capitalised software); tested key supporting documentation; concurred with capitalisation conclusions |
| Loan covenant compliance — HDFC term loan | Material covenants; breach could materially affect financial reporting | Reviewed quarterly compliance certificates issued to HDFC; verified arithmetic + supporting calculations; concurred with conclusion that all covenants met during FY 2023-24 |
| Trade receivables credit-loss provisioning | Customer concentration + post-COVID volatility | Reviewed customer-by-customer aging + provisioning matrix; tested top 10 receivables (representing 64% of total trade receivables); concurred |

### Emphasis of Matter

We draw attention to **Note 18 to the financial statements**, which describes a potential contingent liability arising from a litigation initiated by **Nimbus Technologies Pte Ltd (Singapore)** in March 2024 regarding alleged unpaid licence fees of INR 4.2 crore. The Company's legal counsel has advised that the claim is without merit; however, the outcome is not currently determinable. No provision has been made in the financial statements. Our opinion is not modified in respect of this matter.

### Other Matters

The financial statements of the Company for the year ended 31 March 2023 were audited by another firm of Chartered Accountants who, by their report dated 18 August 2023, expressed an unmodified opinion on those statements.

## Report on Other Legal and Regulatory Requirements

### Internal Financial Controls Over Financial Reporting (IFCFR)

In our opinion, the Company has, in all material respects, an adequate internal financial controls system over financial reporting and such controls were operating effectively as at 31 March 2024. **One material weakness** was noted in IFCFR — relating to the cross-border wire-transfer authorisation process — which has been remediated by management subsequent to year-end and is no longer a material weakness as of the report date.

### CARO 2020 Report

A detailed report under the Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020 ("CARO 2020"), as required by sub-section (11) of section 143 of the Act, is annexed to this report (Annex-A). Key findings:

- No material discrepancies on fixed-asset verification.
- The Company has been generally regular in depositing statutory dues including PF, ESI, GST, TDS, income tax, customs, professional tax. There are NO undisputed amounts in arrears for more than 6 months as on the balance sheet date.
- The Company has not made any preferential allotment of shares during the year.
- Loans from banks + financial institutions (HDFC term loan) have been used for the purposes for which they were obtained. No instances of wilful default.
- The Company has not raised any monies through Initial Public Offering, Further Public Offering, debt instruments, or private placement of fully or partially convertible debentures during the year.
- The Company has not granted any loans that fall within the scope of CARO 2020 Clause 3(iii).
- The Company has not been declared a wilful defaulter by any bank, financial institution, or government authority.
- Cash losses: None during current or preceding financial year.
- Resignation of statutory auditors: NIL during the year.
