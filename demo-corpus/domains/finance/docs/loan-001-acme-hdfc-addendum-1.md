# LOAN ADDENDUM #1 — INTEREST RATE REVISION

**Reference:** HDFC Term Loan Agreement HDFC-CORP-TL-2023-08412 dated 8 November 2023.

**Parties:** Acme Corp Pvt Ltd (Borrower) and HDFC Bank Ltd (Lender).

**Effective Date:** 12 June 2024.

---

## Background

The original Term Loan Agreement (Section 3 — Interest Rate) provides for the interest rate to be benchmarked to **HDFC MCLR plus 125 bps spread**. Pursuant to the first Interest Reset Date (Section 3, original Agreement), the prevailing MCLR + spread is to be applied.

As of the Interest Reset Date (12 June 2024):
- 1-year HDFC MCLR: 8.15 % (up from 7.60 %)
- Spread: 125 bps
- New all-in rate: **9.40 %**

## Amendment

Section 3 of the original Loan Agreement is hereby amended as follows.

### Revised Section 3 — Interest Rate

**Effective from 12 June 2024:**

> "Annual interest rate: **9.40 % per annum**, calculated on the outstanding daily balance, payable monthly.
>
> The interest rate is benchmarked to the HDFC MCLR plus a spread of 125 bps. The rate is subject to revision on each Interest Reset Date (every 12 months from drawdown).
>
> As of the date of this Addendum:
> - 1-year MCLR: 8.15 %
> - Spread: 125 bps
> - All-in rate: 9.40 %"

## EMI Impact

EMI on the outstanding principal (approximately INR 76.4 crore as of the Reset Date) recalculated:
- Pre-amendment EMI: INR 1.27 crore/month (at 8.85 %).
- Post-amendment EMI: **INR 1.33 crore/month** (at 9.40 %).
- Δ: +INR 6.0 lakh/month additional debt service.

## Acknowledgement of Borrower's Right

Per Section 3 of the original Agreement, the Borrower acknowledges that the rate revision is consistent with the benchmark-linked structure and is not subject to negotiation. The Borrower's right is to either:
(i) Accept the revised rate (defaults to acceptance unless prepayment is exercised);
(ii) Prepay the loan in full subject to the prepayment provisions of Section 8 (currently 1 % penalty post 3-year anniversary).

The Borrower has chosen to accept the revised rate.

## Other Terms

All other provisions of the original Loan Agreement remain in full force and effect.
