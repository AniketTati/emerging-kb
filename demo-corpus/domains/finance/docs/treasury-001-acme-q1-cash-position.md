## Executive Summary

As of 31 March 2025 (Q4 FY 2024-25 close), Acme's consolidated cash + cash-equivalent position is **INR 28.42 crore** in the HDFC operating account, plus **INR 8.42 crore** in money-market mutual funds + **INR 12.40 crore** in tax-free bonds — totalling **INR 49.24 crore** of consolidated liquidity.

For Q1 FY 2025-26 (Apr-Jun 2025), the Company expects:
- Net operating cash inflow: INR 12-14 crore
- One-time IT capex spend: INR 4.2 crore (Q1)
- Loan EMI servicing (HDFC term loan): INR 3.99 crore (Q1)
- Tax payments (TDS + GST + Income Tax advance): INR 8.4 crore (Q1)

**Projected end-Q1 cash position: ~INR 65 crore** (assuming flat working-capital).

This memo proposes a deployment plan for INR 30 crore of excess cash into yield-enhancing instruments while maintaining adequate liquidity for operating needs.

---

## Risk Considerations

1. **Counterparty:** All proposed instruments are with AAA-rated counterparties or sovereign (T-bills). No concentration breach.

2. **Liquidity:** T-bills + bank FDs are not instantly liquid but can be redeemed early at modest cost. Maintaining INR 8-10 crore in liquid MFs ensures operating liquidity.

3. **Interest-rate risk:** Short-tenor instruments mitigate duration risk. No fixed-rate exposure beyond 91 days.

4. **FX risk:** None — all deployments are INR-denominated. (Separate memo treasury-002 covers FX hedging policy for USD payables.)
