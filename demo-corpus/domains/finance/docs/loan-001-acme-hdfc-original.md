# TERM LOAN AGREEMENT

**HDFC Bank Ltd** (the "Bank")
HDFC Bank House, Senapati Bapat Marg, Mumbai 400013

**Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** (the "Borrower")
Plot 47, Sector 18, Gurugram, Haryana 122015

**Loan Reference:** HDFC-CORP-TL-2023-08412
**Date:** 8 November 2023

---

## 1. Facility

A term loan facility of **INR 80,00,00,000 (Indian Rupees Eighty Crore)** is hereby granted to the Borrower by the Bank for the purpose of:
- Working-capital expansion (40%);
- Capital expenditure on data centres (40%);
- Acquisition financing — earmarked (20%).

## 2. Tenure

- **Total tenure:** 7 years from drawdown.
- **Moratorium:** 12 months (no principal repayment); interest serviced monthly.
- **Principal repayment:** 72 months in equal monthly instalments (EMIs) from month 13.

## 3. Interest Rate

**Annual interest rate: 8.85 % per annum**, calculated on the outstanding daily balance, payable monthly.

The interest rate is benchmarked to the **HDFC MCLR (Marginal Cost of Funds Lending Rate)** plus a spread of **125 bps**. As of the date of this Agreement:
- 1-year MCLR: 7.60 %
- Spread: 125 bps
- All-in rate: 8.85 %

The rate is subject to revision on each Interest Reset Date (every 12 months from drawdown).

## 4. Drawdown

- Drawdown date: 30 November 2023.
- Full amount drawn in single tranche.

## 5. Security / Collateral

(i) First charge on Borrower's office building at Plot 47, Sector 18, Gurugram (Valuation report dated 1 Oct 2023; market value INR 145 crore).
(ii) Second charge on movable fixed assets at the Bangalore data centre.
(iii) Personal guarantee of two named promoters (separate guarantee agreements).

## 6. Covenants (Selected)

(i) **Financial covenants:**
   - Debt-to-equity ratio: ≤ 2.5x.
   - Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR): ≥ 1.5x.
   - Interest coverage ratio: ≥ 3.0x.

(ii) **Operational covenants:**
   - No new debt exceeding INR 20 crore without Bank's consent.
   - No change in ownership > 25 % without Bank's consent.

(iii) **Reporting:**
   - Quarterly financial statements within 30 days of quarter-end.
   - Annual audited statements within 90 days of FY-end.
   - Compliance certificate quarterly.

## 7. Events of Default

Standard list: payment default, covenant breach, insolvency, material adverse change, cross-default, fraud, change of control.

## 8. Prepayment

Prepayment allowed at 6-month intervals subject to **2 % prepayment penalty** for the first 3 years; **1 %** thereafter.

## 9. Late Payment

Penal interest of **2 % per annum** over the contracted rate on overdue amounts.

## 10. Governing Law

Indian law. Mumbai courts have exclusive jurisdiction.
