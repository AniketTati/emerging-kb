## Complaint Description (verbatim, translated from Tamil + English mix)

"I am 66 years old. My husband expired in October 2024. I have my pension + small savings in this AXIS Bank account. In January 2025 I visited the branch to break my Fixed Deposit of INR 18 lakh (matured) and asked to open a new FD with the same amount.

The branch manager (Mr. Kartik Murthy) told me there is a 'better option' — a 'special savings plan' that would give me 'guaranteed returns of 8% per year for 10 years, much better than FD which gives only 6%'. He showed me documents. I trusted him because I have banked with AXIS for 30 years.

He made me sign many papers. I did not read all of them carefully — I was still grieving for my husband. He told me it is a 'one-time premium' of INR 18 lakh and I will get back INR 32 lakh after 10 years guaranteed.

Three weeks ago (April 2025) my son visited me and asked to see the bond / receipt. He examined the papers and told me this is NOT a savings plan — it is a **Life Insurance Policy with a 10-year premium-payment term**. I have to pay INR 18 lakh PER YEAR for 10 years, totalling INR 1.8 crore. I cannot afford this. I have only INR 8 lakh in my account now.

The 'guaranteed return' is also NOT 8%; my son says the policy fine print says the guaranteed component is only 3-4% and the rest is variable.

I am 66 years old. I have no other source of income except pension. I cannot pay INR 18 lakh every year. If I miss any premium the policy will lapse and I will lose everything.

I want:
(i) Full refund of my INR 18 lakh premium — this is mis-selling;
(ii) Cancellation of the policy with no penalty (it has been more than 30-day free-look period because I did not understand what I had bought);
(iii) Action against Mr. Kartik Murthy for mis-selling to a vulnerable senior citizen who had just lost her husband;
(iv) Refund of any future-dated debits + closure of any auto-debit mandate."

## Initial Findings (preliminary, full investigation in progress)

1. **Policy:** Max Life Smart Wealth Plan (ULIP, 10-year premium payment term, 20-year policy term). Annual premium: INR 18 lakh. First premium paid: 28 January 2025.

2. **Customer's age at sale:** 66 years. Maturity age: 76 (with premium-payment until 76). High concentration of premium relative to declared assets.

3. **Free-look window:** Per IRDAI norms, customer had a 30-day free-look period from policy issuance (28 Jan 2025 → free-look ended 27 Feb 2025). Customer raised complaint 22 April 2025 — outside the formal free-look.

4. **Suitability assessment (regulatory requirement):** Per IRDAI's Sec 71(2)(c) read with the Banks-as-Corporate-Agents Master Direction, customer suitability is required before sale. The Bank's records show a "suitability questionnaire" was filled — but customer's son alleges:
   - The form was filled by the branch officer, not the customer;
   - Customer's actual financial profile (single pension income, recently widowed, no dependents requiring 10-year coverage) would NOT qualify her for a 10-year PPT ULIP under standard suitability.
   - The form on record indicates "income INR 24 lakh per annum" which is materially overstated (her actual pension is INR 6 lakh per annum).

5. **Branch officer status:** Mr. Kartik Murthy is suspended pending investigation. His sales record shows 4 other elderly customers (65+) sold the same product in the same quarter — review opened.

6. **Insurance partner cooperation:** Max Life has agreed (pending formal sign-off) to refund the INR 18 lakh premium without penalty under "mis-selling" provisions of the IRDAI-Bancassurance protocol, recognising the seriousness of the case + the cooperation of AXIS. Max Life will retain a small admin fee of INR 18,000.

---

## Provisional Action (within 5 working days of complaint)

(i) Auto-debit mandate cancelled effective 22 April 2025 (no further debits will be processed).

(ii) Policy refund initiation: Max Life will refund INR 17,82,000 (premium less admin fee) to customer's AXIS account within 15 working days.

(iii) Goodwill: AXIS Bank will additionally credit INR 18,000 (covering the Max Life admin fee) so that the customer is restored to a fully whole position. Total restoration: INR 18,00,000.

(iv) Free-look extension: Even though the formal free-look expired, the Bank + insurer have agreed to treat this as a "free-look at customer's option" + waive any IRDAI penalty in light of the senior-citizen + bereavement context.

(v) Disciplinary review of Mr. Kartik Murthy + his 4 other senior-citizen sales — final action pending.

(vi) Bank-wide training refresher on senior-citizen suitability + mis-selling — to be conducted by Q1 FY 2025-26 (already on the compliance calendar).
