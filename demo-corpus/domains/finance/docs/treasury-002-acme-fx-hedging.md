## Purpose

This memo documents Acme Corp's revised FX hedging policy (effective 1 March 2025) and the implementation plan for hedging USD-denominated payables (primarily to Nimbus Technologies Pte Ltd, Singapore + Hudson Bay Holdings, USA + smaller UK contractor payments).

## Policy

Acme's revised FX hedging policy provides:

(i) **Hedging horizon:** Hedge committed USD/GBP payables that fall due within the next **12 months**, at the time the underlying commitment becomes a firm obligation (e.g., signed PO, signed contract amendment).

(ii) **Hedging ratio:** Hedge **60-80%** of forecast USD payables 0-12 months out. Within this range, the Treasurer has discretion based on:
- Spot vs forward differential ("forward premium / discount")
- Market volatility (use upper-end of range when implied volatility > 12%)
- Forecast confidence (use lower-end when forecast is uncertain)

(iii) **Hedging instruments:** Plain-vanilla forward contracts with INR/USD authorised dealers (HDFC Bank as primary; ICICI Bank as secondary for diversification). Options + structured products NOT permitted unless specifically Board-approved per transaction.

(iv) **Counterparty diversification:** No single bank may hold more than **70%** of outstanding hedging contracts.

(v) **Mark-to-market accounting:** Per Ind AS 109, hedge accounting will be applied where the relationship meets effectiveness criteria. Auditors (KPMG India) have reviewed + concurred with the proposed accounting treatment.

(vi) **Reporting:** Treasurer will report monthly to CFO + quarterly to Audit + Risk Committee on:
- Outstanding hedges (notional + MTM)
- Realised gains/losses
- Hedging ratio actual vs target
- Counterparty exposure

(vii) **Stop-loss:** No formal stop-loss limit on forward MTM (since hedges are matched to underlying commitments — MTM gain/loss should reverse on physical settlement). However, any monthly MTM loss > INR 50 lakh on net hedge book triggers a special CFO review.

## Risk Considerations

(i) **Forecast risk:** Actual USD payables may differ from forecast (PO timing). Hedge ratio is set on a conservative forecast.

(ii) **Counterparty risk:** HDFC + ICICI are both AAA-rated banks; counterparty default risk is negligible.

(iii) **Settlement risk:** Standard for Indian banking system.

(iv) **Regulatory:** All forwards complied with FEMA Master Direction on Risk Management; supporting documentation (purchase orders + budgets) is on file.

(v) **Documentation:** Hedge documentation including hedge designation, effectiveness testing, and ISDA Master Agreement (with both HDFC + ICICI) is in place.
