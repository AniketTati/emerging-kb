## Opinion

We have audited the standalone financial statements of NorthWind Capital India Pvt Ltd ("the Company"), being an RBI-registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), for the year ended 31 March 2024.

In our opinion, the financial statements give the information required by the Companies Act 2013 and the relevant RBI Master Directions, and give a **true and fair view** in conformity with Ind AS, of the state of affairs as at 31 March 2024, the profit for the year, and the cash flows for the year then ended.

## Basis for Opinion

We conducted our audit in accordance with the Standards on Auditing (SAs) issued by the ICAI. We are independent and have complied with the Code of Ethics. We obtained sufficient + appropriate audit evidence.

## Key Audit Matters

| Matter | Description |
|---|---|
| **Loan-book credit-loss provisioning under Ind AS 109** | The Company's loan book (INR 2,840 cr) is the largest asset on the balance sheet. Credit-loss provisioning under the Expected Credit Loss (ECL) model involves significant judgment regarding probability of default + loss given default + macroeconomic adjustments. We tested the staging classification, the PD models (developed in conjunction with the parent), and the macroeconomic overlay. Our procedures included a sample of 84 loans (~32% by value). We concurred with the Company's provisioning of INR 64 cr (2.3% of gross loan book). |
| **Related-party transactions with parent + group** | Significant volume of related-party transactions with parent (NorthWind Capital Inc, USA) including ECB borrowings + management-fee transfers. We reviewed the Transfer Pricing Study + the ECB documentation; tested arm's-length compliance. No material exceptions. |
| **FEMA + RBI NBFC compliance** | First full year of NBFC operations. We reviewed RBI returns + FEMA filings. No material non-compliance noted. |

## Material Uncertainty — Group Litigation Disclosure

We draw attention to **Note 24** to the financial statements, which discloses ongoing securities class-action litigation in the United States against the parent company (NorthWind Capital Inc) — *In re NorthWind Capital Securities Litigation* (S.D.N.Y. Case No. 24-cv-1842). The Indian subsidiary (this Company) is not a named defendant, but a material adverse outcome at the parent level could affect the Indian operations through capital + funding constraints.

The Company has disclosed this risk in the financial statements; our opinion is not modified in respect of this matter.
