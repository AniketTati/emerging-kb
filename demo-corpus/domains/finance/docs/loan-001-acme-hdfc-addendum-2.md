# LOAN ADDENDUM #2 — COLLATERAL SUBSTITUTION

**Reference:** HDFC Term Loan Agreement HDFC-CORP-TL-2023-08412 dated 8 November 2023, as amended by Addendum #1 dated 12 June 2024.

**Parties:** Acme Corp Pvt Ltd + HDFC Bank Ltd.

**Effective Date:** 22 March 2025.

---

## Background

Per Section 5 of the original Loan Agreement, the collateral comprised:
(i) First charge on the office building at Plot 47, Sector 18, Gurugram (originally valued INR 145 crore).
(ii) Second charge on movable fixed assets at the Bangalore data centre.

The Borrower has indicated that the Gurugram property is being considered for **redevelopment / sale** in connection with an unrelated corporate transaction. The Borrower has requested a **substitution of the primary collateral** with an alternative asset of equal or greater value.

## Substituted Collateral

(i) **First charge** is hereby released from the Gurugram property (Plot 47, Sector 18) effective the Effective Date.

(ii) **First charge** is hereby established on:
   - The newly-purchased data centre property at **Whitefield Tech Park, Bangalore, KA 560066** (Property ID BG-WTP-2024-22).
   - Valuation: INR 168 crore (per dated 14 March 2025 valuation report by Cushman & Wakefield).

(iii) **Second charge** on the movable fixed assets at the Bangalore data centre is **maintained unchanged**.

(iv) Personal guarantees of named promoters are **maintained unchanged**.

## Lien Filings

- Registration of the new charge with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) — to be completed within 30 days.
- Mortgage deed for the new property — registered with the appropriate Sub-Registrar Office.

## Interest Rate

The interest rate remains as fixed by Addendum #1 (**9.40 %**), subject to future Interest Reset Dates.

## Other Provisions

All other provisions of the original Loan Agreement (as amended by Addendum #1) remain unchanged.

## Conditions Precedent

This Addendum is conditional upon:
(i) Completion of the new mortgage registration within 30 days.
(ii) ROC charge filing within 30 days.
(iii) Independent valuation of the new collateral (completed).

Failure to satisfy any condition precedent → the original Section 5 collateral structure is restored.
