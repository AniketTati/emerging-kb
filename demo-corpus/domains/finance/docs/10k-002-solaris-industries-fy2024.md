## Chairman's Letter

Dear Shareholders,

Fiscal year 2024-25 was a year of disciplined growth and operational transformation for Solaris Industries. We delivered total revenue of INR 8,420 crore, an increase of 18.4% over the previous fiscal year, and PAT of INR 1,184 crore, an increase of 22.8%. Our return on capital employed expanded to 22.4%, the highest in the past five years.

Key strategic milestones:

(i) Commissioning of the Pune-3 solar PV manufacturing facility (2.4 GW annual capacity);
(ii) Acquisition of Marigold Pharma's solar EPC division for INR 482 crore (closed January 2025);
(iii) Listing of our renewable-energy InvIT on the NSE in March 2025 (raised INR 2,840 crore);
(iv) Establishment of our Singapore subsidiary to serve Southeast Asian markets.

Looking ahead to FY 2025-26, our focus areas are: (a) doubling solar EPC orderbook; (b) commissioning the Tamil Nadu lithium-ion cell facility (Phase 1: 4 GWh by Q3 FY 2025-26); (c) further deleveraging the balance sheet through InvIT cash flows.

*Suresh K. Iyer*

### Risk Management

Material risks identified and mitigated during FY 2024-25:

(i) **Polysilicon price volatility** — managed through 12-month forward contracts covering 60% of expected consumption;
(ii) **Foreign exchange exposure (USD raw material imports)** — hedged via forward + options strategies covering 70% of expected USD requirement;
(iii) **Commodity / supply concentration** — Chinese supplier dependency reduced from 84% (FY 2022-23) to 48% (FY 2024-25); ongoing qualification of Vietnam + Malaysia suppliers;
(iv) **Customer credit risk** — average DSO 64 days (vs 72 days prior year), enhanced collections through working-capital factoring with multiple banks.

## Management Discussion + Analysis

### Industry Overview

India's renewable energy sector continues to expand rapidly. Installed solar capacity reached 84.2 GW at end of FY 2024-25 (vs 73.4 GW prior year), representing 18.4% of total installed power capacity. Government target of 280 GW solar by 2030 implies a CAGR of 20%+ over the next 5 years. The Production-Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme for solar manufacturing has been a significant tailwind for domestic players such as Solaris.

### Segment Performance + Outlook

**Solar Manufacturing:** EBITDA margin expanded to 24.8% (from 22.4% prior year) on the back of: (a) PLI incentive accruals; (b) operating leverage from the Pune-3 facility ramp-up; (c) higher average selling prices in H2.

**Solar EPC + O&M:** Orderbook at fiscal year-end was INR 12,420 crore, providing approximately 4.4× revenue visibility. The Marigold EPC acquisition added INR 1,842 crore to the orderbook.

**Energy Storage:** First-year segment performance was below internal targets due to delayed commissioning of the Tamil Nadu cell facility. Production is now expected to ramp from Q2 FY 2025-26. The InvIT IPO was successful, raising INR 2,840 crore at upper-band pricing.

### Capital Structure + Liquidity

Net debt declined to INR 3,420 crore (vs INR 4,182 crore prior year) on the back of InvIT proceeds + strong operating cash flow. Net-debt-to-EBITDA ratio improved to 1.82× (from 2.95× prior year). The Company's credit ratings:
