## Areas Under Discussion (NOT FINALISED)

1. **Capital structure:**
   - Whether to refinance the HDFC term loan (currently 9.40% post-Addendum #1) ahead of its 2030 maturity, or
   - Hold + reduce via accelerated EMI payments
   - Estimated savings if refinanced (if rates fall to 8.50%): INR 50-60 lakh per year
   - Open question: Does the Addendum #1 + Addendum #2 collateral substitution package make refinancing attractive vs holding?

2. **Working capital line:**
   - Current INR 18 cr LC line at HDFC is being moderately used (peak draw INR 8 cr in Q4)
   - Possible expansion to INR 25 cr to support Bangalore data centre Phase-2 capex
   - Quote being obtained from HDFC + ICICI

3. **Dividend policy:**
   - FY 2024-25 dividend pending Board approval — current draft is 8% of FY 2024-25 PAT (~INR 2.3 cr)
   - Comparable to FY 2023-24 (7%)

4. **Treasury counterparty additions:**
   - Currently HDFC primary + ICICI secondary
   - Possible addition of Kotak / Axis to further diversify deposits + hedging counterparties
   - No firm decision yet
