# CORPORATE LOAN — WORKING CAPITAL LINE

**Lender:** Kotak Mahindra Bank Ltd
**Borrower:** Nimbus Finance Pvt Ltd (subsidiary of Nimbus Technologies Pte Ltd, Singapore)
**Loan Reference:** KOTAK-WCL-2025-22941
**Date:** 28 February 2025

> **Cross-domain note:** Nimbus Finance is the Indian subsidiary of Nimbus Technologies Pte Ltd which appears in the Legal corpus (MSA-002 + 2 SOWs with Acme Corp). Indian operations are subject to RBI ECB norms.

## 1. Facility

A working-capital line of **INR 38,00,00,000 (Indian Rupees Thirty-Eight Crore)** revolving, for:
- Inventory + receivables financing.
- Cross-border parent-subsidiary remittances (within limits).
- General working capital.

## 2. Tenure

- Line validity: 12 months from drawdown, renewable on annual review.
- Working capital interest: monthly billing.

## 3. Interest Rate

**Annual rate: 9.65 %** (Kotak's working-capital prime rate for this risk band).

## 4. Security

(i) Hypothecation of all current assets (inventory + receivables).
(ii) Personal guarantee of Indian directors.
(iii) Corporate guarantee of parent company Nimbus Technologies Pte Ltd.
(iv) **No mortgage on property** (working capital, not term loan).

## 5. Sublimits

| Purpose | Sublimit |
|---|---|
| Pre-shipment finance | INR 8 crore |
| Post-shipment finance | INR 12 crore |
| LC issuance | INR 8 crore |
| Bank guarantees | INR 6 crore |
| Cash credit + others | INR 4 crore |
| **Total** | **38 crore** |

## 6. Cross-Border Considerations

(i) RBI ECB approval not required (revolving working capital).
(ii) FEMA compliance for cross-border services payments.
(iii) Transfer pricing documentation required for related-party transactions with the Singapore parent.

## 7. Covenants

Standard liquidity ratio + current asset coverage thresholds.
