## Complaint Description (verbatim)

"I took an ICICI personal loan (IL-2022-44218) in April 2022 for INR 5,00,000 at 11.50% p.a. fixed for 36 months. My EMI was fixed at INR 16,840 per month, debited on the 2nd of every month.

In November 2023, the loan EMI was suddenly increased to **INR 28,420 per month** — a near doubling. I received no prior intimation. I noticed the change only when I saw my November 2023 statement.

I have raised this with ICICI customer care multiple times (1860-120-7777, calls dated 5 Dec 2023, 18 Dec 2023, 4 Jan 2024) and was each time told 'it is being looked into'.

In March 2024, I received a letter from ICICI stating the EMI revision was due to 'rate reset under benchmark linkage'. However, my loan agreement (which I have re-read carefully) says the rate is **FIXED for the original 36-month tenure**, not floating. The loan agreement does NOT have a rate-reset clause.

It has been **13 months** since the EMI was increased without explanation. I have been paying the increased EMI under protest. I want:
(i) Confirmation in writing of why a fixed-rate loan was reset;
(ii) Refund of the excess EMI paid (~INR 11,580 × 13 months = INR 1,50,540);
(iii) Restoration of original EMI INR 16,840 for the remaining tenure;
(iv) Compensation for the prolonged service failure to address my complaints from December 2023."

## Internal Review — Initial Findings

Investigation reference: ICICI-PL-DISP-2024-12-1842
Lead: Mr. Bhavesh Joshi, Loans Operations Cluster Head

**Findings (interim):**

1. **Loan agreement (signed 8 April 2022):** Section 3.2 of the personal-loan agreement signed by the customer clearly states: *"The rate of interest is fixed at 11.50% p.a. for the full tenure of the loan unless otherwise mutually agreed in writing."* The agreement does NOT contain a rate-reset clause.

2. **System record:** The Bank's loan-management system shows the EMI was algorithmically increased on 1 November 2023, allegedly due to "benchmark-linked rate reset" — this triggered an EMI recalculation to INR 28,420 (consistent with a rate change to ~16.5%).

3. **No customer intimation:** The Bank's records confirm that no rate-revision letter was dispatched to the customer prior to the EMI change. The March 2024 letter referenced by the customer was sent in response to her complaint and contains a generic explanation that does not match the contractual terms of HER specific loan.

4. **Other affected customers:** Operations team has identified that **148 personal loans** across the ICICI book were similarly affected by an incorrect classification of fixed-rate loans as floating during a 2023 system migration. This appears to be an operational error, not a deliberate policy.

5. **Aggregate impact (preliminary):** Estimated excess EMI debited across all 148 customers: ~INR 84.2 lakh. ICICI Banks's Operations + Risk + Legal teams have been jointly notified.

---

## Conflict Reference (cross-document)

Notable contradiction:

- **The loan agreement (April 2022):** Rate is FIXED at 11.50% for full 36-month tenure.
- **The March 2024 explanation letter:** Cites "benchmark-linked rate reset" — which would only apply to floating-rate loans.
- **System record:** Treats the loan as floating (reset rate to 16.5% in Nov 2023).

These three sources are mutually inconsistent. Per the bank's standard hierarchy ("contract terms prevail over system records unless customer separately consented to amendment"), **the loan agreement should govern**. The system record + March 2024 letter are incorrect.

## Action Taken (interim — to be finalised in resolution)

(i) Provisional refund: INR 1,50,540 (estimated excess EMI for 13 months) credited to customer's savings account on **20 December 2024** subject to final reconciliation.
(ii) EMI restored to INR 16,840 effective the next debit cycle (2 January 2025).
(iii) Goodwill compensation: INR 5,000 for the extended delay in addressing her earlier calls.
(iv) Customer informed she will receive a final closure letter once the bank-wide root-cause investigation completes.
