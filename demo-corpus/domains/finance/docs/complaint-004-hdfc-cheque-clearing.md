## Complaint Description

"We deposited a cheque for INR 42,82,000 drawn on Punjab National Bank (Connaught Place branch, New Delhi) at our HDFC Lower Parel branch on 22 January 2025 at 11:18 IST. The cheque was issued by our customer Orion Consulting Pvt Ltd against invoice INV-VRT-2024-12-184.

Per RBI's Cheque Truncation System (CTS-2010) norms, an inter-city cheque should clear within **T+1 to T+2 working days** for local/grid clearing. We expected the funds to be credited by 24 January 2025 at the latest.

As of today (28 January 2025), the cheque has NOT been credited to our account. The branch officer has informed us that the cheque is "still in transit" — which is unacceptable 7 working days after deposit.

We have:
- A major vendor payment of INR 38,40,000 due on 30 January 2025 (HDFC tower lease — internal!);
- Penalties accrue at INR 18,000 per day delay on this vendor payment;
- Our working capital is strained because of this delayed clearing.

Demand:
(i) Immediate credit of INR 42,82,000 (back-dated to T+2 of deposit = 24 January 2025);
(ii) Interest compensation @ 8% p.a. from 24 January 2025 till actual credit;
(iii) Bank to bear any vendor-payment penalty if not credited by 29 January 2025 close-of-business;
(iv) Written explanation of why CTS clearing took 7+ working days when the regulatory norm is T+2."

---

## Internal Review

Investigation reference: HDFC-CTS-DISP-2025-01-848
Lead: Ms. Anita Naik, Cluster Operations Head (Mumbai)

**Findings:**

1. **Cheque receipt confirmed:** HDFC's records show the cheque was received at Lower Parel branch on 22 January 2025 at 11:18 IST and processed into the CTS pipeline at 11:42 IST.

2. **CTS image:** Image was captured, sent to clearing house at 16:42 IST same day (within branch SLA).

3. **Trail at PNB end:** The Bank's investigation revealed that the drawee bank (PNB) had **flagged the cheque for additional scrutiny** at 23 January 2025 due to a near-match on a CTS-fraud watchlist. The flag was a false positive (a different cheque series).

4. **PNB internal delay:** PNB took **5 working days** (vs the standard 1 day) to confirm the false positive and release the cheque. The cheque was actually cleared by PNB on 28 January 2025 morning.

5. **Funds received:** HDFC received the credit advice from PNB at 13:18 IST on 28 January 2025. The funds will be credited to Vertex Industries' account on **29 January 2025 morning** in the next clearing cycle.

6. **Root cause:** Delay is attributable to the drawee bank (PNB), not HDFC. However:
   - HDFC should have proactively communicated the PNB flag to the customer when it was noticed (24-25 January). It did not.
   - The branch officer's response ("still in transit") was unhelpful and inaccurate.

---

## Action Taken

(i) **Cheque credit:** INR 42,82,000 credited to Vertex Industries' account on 29 January 2025 at 10:18 IST.

(ii) **Interest compensation:** HDFC will credit interest @ 8% p.a. for 5 working days (24-28 Jan) on INR 42,82,000 = INR 5,861. This will be paid as a goodwill gesture even though the delay was at the drawee bank end. Credit reference: HDFC-COMP-2025-01-848 dated 29 January 2025.

(iii) **Vendor penalty:** HDFC declines to bear the vendor-penalty risk as the delay was not caused by HDFC. However, HDFC has offered to provide a written acknowledgement of the cheque-clearing delay (signed by the Branch Head) that Vertex can use in its negotiation with its vendor.

(iv) **Service improvement:** HDFC's Treasury operations team is reviewing whether early-warning notifications to corporate customers can be implemented when a deposited cheque is flagged by the drawee bank.

(v) **Branch coaching:** The Lower Parel branch officer who provided the unhelpful response has been counselled. The branch head has provided a personal apology.
