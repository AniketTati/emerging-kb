## PART I

### Item 1. Business

NorthWind Capital Inc. ("NorthWind", "the Company") is a publicly traded specialty finance and asset management holding company incorporated in Delaware and headquartered in New York. The Company operates primarily through two reportable segments:

1. **Asset Management:** Management of approximately USD 18.4 billion in assets across hedge fund, private credit, and structured product strategies. Net management + performance fees in FY 2024 were USD 482.4 million.

2. **Direct Lending:** A balance-sheet lending business that originates senior secured loans to middle-market corporate borrowers in the United States, India, and Singapore. Loan book at fiscal year-end was approximately USD 2.84 billion, with weighted-average yield 8.92% and weighted-average tenor 4.3 years.

The Company has subsidiaries in India (NorthWind Capital India Pvt Ltd, regulated as an NBFC by the RBI), Singapore (NorthWind Asia Pte Ltd, regulated by MAS), and the United Kingdom (NorthWind UK Ltd, regulated by the FCA).

### Item 1A. Risk Factors

Material risks include, but are not limited to:

(i) **Credit risk:** The Company's direct-lending book has growing exposure to Indian middle-market corporates. While each loan is collateralised, a regional economic downturn in India could increase non-performing assets above the 1.8% level reported as of year-end 2024.

(ii) **Counterparty concentration:** Approximately 18.4% of FY 2024 net asset management fees were generated from a single sovereign wealth fund client. Loss of this client would materially affect FY 2025 results.

(iii) **Cross-border regulatory complexity:** As a US-headquartered firm with regulated subsidiaries in 3 foreign jurisdictions, the Company is subject to overlapping and at times conflicting regulatory regimes. Recent RBI directives on offshore borrowings by Indian NBFCs (effective January 2025) may affect the cost of capital for the India subsidiary.

(iv) **Litigation exposure:** As of fiscal year-end, the Company was party to 8 active commercial litigations, of which 2 are class-action proceedings filed by minority shareholders. Aggregate claimed damages are USD 184.2 million; the Company believes claims are without merit but cannot rule out unfavourable outcomes.

(v) **Operational + cyber risk:** A material cybersecurity incident at the Company's NewYork data center in Q3 2024 (later disclosed publicly in November 2024) is described under Item 1C (Cybersecurity). The Company has since remediated identified vulnerabilities; however, residual reputational and regulatory risk remains.

### Item 1C. Cybersecurity

In August 2024, the Company detected unauthorised access to a limited-scope file repository at its New York data center. Personally identifiable information (names + email addresses) of approximately 8,400 current and former clients may have been exposed. No financial account information was accessed. The Company notified affected individuals and filed required regulatory notices. Total remediation + notification cost was USD 4.2 million.

### Item 2. Properties

The Company leases office space at 1450 Park Avenue, New York (~84,000 sq ft, lease through Dec 2032); office space in Mumbai BKC (~22,000 sq ft, lease through Mar 2030); and smaller offices in Singapore (~12,400 sq ft) and London (~8,400 sq ft).

### Item 3. Legal Proceedings

In addition to the 8 active commercial litigations referenced under Item 1A, the Company is named as a defendant in *In re NorthWind Capital Securities Litigation*, Case No. 24-cv-1842 (S.D.N.Y.), a putative class action alleging misstatements regarding the August 2024 cybersecurity incident. Discovery is ongoing.

The Company is also a third-party witness (not a defendant) in connection with an SEC investigation of two former portfolio managers who departed the Company in 2023. The Company is cooperating fully.

### Item 7. MD&A

#### Overview

Fiscal year 2024 was a record year for NorthWind, with total revenue growing 20.5% year-over-year and net income growing 29.8%. Growth was driven by a 13.6% increase in AUM (primarily through net inflows into the private credit fund family) and a 30.1% expansion of the balance-sheet loan book, particularly in India.

#### Segment results

**Asset Management:** Net management + performance fees of USD 482.4 million were 18.4% above FY 2023. Performance was driven by the NorthWind Special Situations Fund III (up 24.8% gross) and the NorthWind Asia Credit Fund (up 18.2% gross).

**Direct Lending:** Net interest income of USD 184.2 million was 22.4% above FY 2023. The India sub originated USD 884.2 million of new loans in FY 2024, including a notable USD 80 million senior secured term loan facility with HDFC Bank India serving as facility agent for the Indian leg (cross-border ECB). Borrower: Acme Corp Pvt Ltd. (See related disclosures.)

#### Capital + liquidity

The Company ended FY 2024 with USD 484.2 million of cash + cash equivalents and USD 1.2 billion of undrawn revolver capacity. Total debt was USD 1.84 billion, with weighted-average maturity 6.4 years and weighted-average cost 5.42%.
