## Chairperson's Statement

Dear Shareholders,

Fiscal year 2024-25 was a defining year of strategic transformation for Marigold Pharma. We completed three milestone transactions:

(i) Divestment of our solar EPC division to Solaris Industries Limited for INR 482 crore (closed January 2025), enabling focus on core pharmaceutical operations;
(ii) Acquisition of Mehrotra Biologics Pvt Ltd, a Hyderabad-based biosimilars manufacturer, for INR 1,184 crore;
(iii) FDA approval (May 2025) for our first US-market biosimilar — Adalimumab biosimilar "Adalsol", expected to contribute USD 84-118 million revenue in FY 2025-26.

Total revenue grew 14.2% to INR 6,240 crore. EBITDA margin contracted 180 bps to 22.4% due to acquisition-related charges and R&D ramp-up. We expect margins to recover from H2 FY 2025-26 as biosimilar volumes scale.

*Dr. Jayanti R. Iyer*
Chairperson + Whole-time Director

### Risk Factors

(i) **Product liability:** As of fiscal year-end, the Company was named as a defendant in 14 active product-liability matters across US (8), Canada (3), India (2), and UK (1). Aggregate claimed damages are USD 184.2 million; the Company has insurance coverage of USD 200 million per occurrence (deductible USD 5M per claim). Management does not expect material adverse impact.

(ii) **Regulatory inspections + warning letters:** During FY 2024-25, the Company received one Form 483 observation from the US FDA following an inspection at the Andheri facility (March 2025). All 6 observations were addressed in writing within 30 days; no warning letter has been issued. The Company is also subject to routine inspections by India's CDSCO + Maharashtra FDA, EMA (Europe), MHRA (UK), and TGA (Australia).

(iii) **Patent expirations + biosimilar competition:** Two key US generic products (representing ~12% of US segment revenue) face new generic entrants in FY 2025-26.

(iv) **Currency:** Approximately 56% of revenue is in USD/EUR; only ~40% of cost is in USD/EUR. Net long USD position is hedged ~70% via forwards.

(v) **Litigation matters of note:**
   - *In re Marigold Adalimumab IP Litigation*, multi-district litigation in US courts. The Company is a defendant in a patent-infringement suit by AbbVie related to formulation IP. The Company believes its patents-around design is valid and infringement claims are without merit. Trial expected Q3 FY 2025-26.
   - *Marigold Pharma vs. Solaris Industries* (Bombay High Court). A post-closing earn-out dispute related to the January 2025 solar EPC divestment. Disputed amount: INR 42 crore. Management expects favourable resolution but cannot guarantee outcome.

## Management Discussion + Analysis

### Industry Overview

The Indian pharmaceuticals industry continues robust growth (CAGR ~11% over the past 5 years), driven by domestic chronic-disease prevalence + export demand for generics. The biosimilars sub-segment is projected to grow at 24%+ CAGR through 2030 as more biologics lose exclusivity. India's CDSCO has approved 22 biosimilars to date; Marigold accounts for 4 of these.

### Segment Performance

**Branded formulations (India):** Strong 16.4% growth, driven by chronic-care segments (cardiology, diabetes, oncology). Top 10 brands account for 64% of segment revenue. Re-launch of the heritage brand "Crocin-MX" (analgesic) contributed materially.

**Generic formulations (US + EU):** Growth of 11.8%. Headwinds from patent expirations on competitor brands, partially offset by 4 new generic launches (FY 2024-25). 8 ANDA filings pending US FDA approval.

**API + intermediates:** Modest 8.4% growth. Margin pressure from Chinese over-supply on certain key starting materials.

### Capital Structure + Outlook

Net debt rose to INR 1,184 crore (from INR 482 crore prior year) on the back of the Mehrotra Biologics acquisition. Net-debt-to-EBITDA at 0.85× remains comfortable. The Company expects net debt to peak in Q2 FY 2025-26 and decline thereafter.

Credit ratings (CRISIL): AA / A1+ / Stable.
