# EQUIPMENT FINANCING AGREEMENT

**Lender:** HDFC Bank Ltd
**Borrower:** Apollo Hospitals Pvt Ltd (Pune unit)
**Loan Reference:** HDFC-EFA-2024-08841
**Date:** 18 December 2024

## 1. Facility

A term loan of **INR 22,40,00,000 (Indian Rupees Twenty-Two Crore Forty Lakh)** for the purchase of a high-end CT-scanner unit (Siemens SOMATOM Force, 384-slice).

> Cross-domain: Apollo Hospitals Pune is also referenced in the healthcare corpus (see Arjun Singh case + others).

## 2. Tenure

- Total: 5 years.
- No moratorium.
- 60 monthly instalments from month 1.

## 3. Interest Rate

**Annual rate: 9.85 %** (fixed-rate equipment financing).

## 4. Security

(i) Hypothecation of the CT scanner equipment.
(ii) Corporate guarantee from Apollo Hospitals Pvt Ltd parent.

## 5. Asset Insurance

- Equipment insured for INR 24 crore (vs INR 22.4 crore facility) — additional buffer for transit + commissioning.
- Annual premium ~INR 28 lakh.

## 6. Drawdown

In two tranches:
- Tranche 1 (INR 18 crore) on 28 Dec 2024 (equipment delivery + commissioning advance).
- Tranche 2 (INR 4.4 crore) on 15 Mar 2025 (final acceptance test passing).

## 7. End-Use Compliance

The equipment must be installed and operational at the Apollo Pune hospital. Annual utilisation report (scans performed) submitted to the Bank.
