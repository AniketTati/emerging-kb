## Complaint Description (verbatim)

"On 2 April 2025 at approximately 22:18 IST I noticed five (5) transactions on my Kotak White Reserve credit card that I did not authorise. The transactions are:

1. INR 84,000 — 'Hudson Online UK' — 02 Apr 2025 21:42 IST
2. INR 76,500 — 'Hudson Online UK' — 02 Apr 2025 21:46 IST
3. INR 92,300 — 'BetFair UK' — 02 Apr 2025 21:54 IST
4. INR 1,18,400 — 'Steam Wallet Gift Card' — 02 Apr 2025 22:08 IST
5. INR 87,200 — 'Steam Wallet Gift Card' — 02 Apr 2025 22:14 IST

Total: INR 4,58,400

I was at home in Mumbai during this entire period. I have NEVER used this card outside India. I have never gambled online. I have never bought a Steam gift card.

I attempted to call Kotak's 24/7 fraud helpline (1860-266-2666) at 22:22 IST. After waiting 18 minutes I was disconnected. I tried again at 22:48 IST and after a 12-minute wait, was able to block the card. By that time the damage was done.

I demand:
(i) Immediate refund of INR 4,58,400 as I am a victim of fraud and these are NOT authorised transactions;
(ii) Confirmation that I have ZERO liability under RBI's Limited Liability for Customers in Unauthorised Electronic Banking Transactions (Circular dated 6 July 2017);
(iii) Replacement card with new number issued ASAP — I am traveling on 12 April and need a working card;
(iv) Written explanation of how these transactions were authorised when I have OTP-based 2-factor authentication enabled."

## Bank Initial Assessment

**Investigation reference:** KOTAK-FRD-2025-04-1182

Initial findings (preliminary, subject to confirmation):

1. **OTP records:** No OTP was generated or sent for any of the 5 disputed transactions. This is inconsistent with the customer's claim of having 2FA enabled — the bank's records confirm that 2FA WAS enabled on her card, but the disputed merchants used a "merchant-initiated" flow that bypassed OTP.

2. **Merchant categorisation:** Hudson Online UK, BetFair UK, and Steam Wallet are all online merchants known to Kotak's fraud-monitoring system. The combination + timing + amount is consistent with a "card-testing followed by quick burn" fraud pattern that the Bank's analytics flag.

3. **Geo-IP analysis:** All 5 transactions originated from IP addresses in Bulgaria + Romania. Customer's NetBanking + app activity in the prior 30 days is consistently from Mumbai IPs.

4. **CCTV (ATM):** Customer last used the physical card at a Kotak ATM in Andheri on 28 March 2025 (5 days before fraud). No suspicious context.

5. **Card-data compromise — likely vector:** A historical merchant where customer used the card may have suffered a data breach. The Bank is reviewing data-sharing partner alerts to identify the likely source.

## Bank Action (within 24 hours of complaint)

(i) Card blocked permanently (was already blocked by customer at 22:48 IST on 2 Apr 2025 via helpline).

(ii) Provisional credit of INR 4,58,400 to customer's account effected at 14:42 IST on 4 April 2025 (per RBI Circular on Limited Liability — Zero Liability applies when fraud is reported within 3 working days and customer has not contributed to the loss).

(iii) Replacement card issued: 5421-XXXX-XXXX-1842 (white Reserve), dispatched via priority courier on 4 April 2025 evening — expected delivery 7 April 2025.

(iv) Charge-back initiated with VISA + acquiring banks for all 5 transactions. Expected timeline: 45-60 days for cross-border merchant chargebacks.

(v) FIR support: Bank offered to assist customer in filing a cybercrime FIR with Mumbai Cyber Police; customer has agreed and the Bank will provide transaction records + supporting evidence.
