# TERM LOAN AGREEMENT (USD-denominated)

**Lender:** HDFC Bank Ltd
**Borrower:** NorthWind Capital Ltd
**Loan Reference:** HDFC-CORP-TL-2024-12892
**Date:** 30 September 2024

## 1. Facility

A term-loan facility of **USD 80,000,000 (US Dollars Eighty Million)** is granted to NorthWind Capital Ltd for:
- Working capital expansion (50%)
- Acquisition financing (40%)
- General corporate purposes (10%)

## 2. Tenure

- Total: 5 years from drawdown date.
- Moratorium: 6 months (interest serviced).
- Principal repayment: 54 monthly instalments from month 7.

## 3. Interest Rate

USD SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate) + 350 bps. As of drawdown:
- 3-month SOFR: 4.85 %
- Spread: 350 bps
- All-in rate: **8.35 %**

Subject to quarterly reset against the prevailing SOFR.

## 4. Security

(i) Charge on receivables (≥INR 480 crore as of 30 June 2024).
(ii) Pledge on key intangible assets (software trademarks).
(iii) Corporate guarantee from holding company.

## 5. Covenants

- Net debt to EBITDA: ≤ 3.0x.
- Interest coverage: ≥ 4.0x.
- Free cash flow: positive in 3 of past 4 quarters.

## 6. Drawdown

Single tranche on **3 October 2024**.

## 7. Governing Law

Indian law; cross-border foreign-exchange controls per FEMA.

## 8. Cross-Border Features

- Settlement in USD via the Bank's New York correspondent.
- ECB (External Commercial Borrowing) approval from RBI obtained (Approval No. RBI/ECB/2024/0188).
- AS-94 hedging recommended; not mandated.
