# TREASURY MEMORANDUM — COUNTERPARTY CREDIT LIMITS POLICY (REVISED)

**To:** Board (Finance + Risk Committee), CEO, CFO
**From:** Mr. Vikas Acharya, Treasurer
**Date:** 20 January 2025
**Effective from:** 1 February 2025
**Replaces:** Treasury counterparty memo dated 18 March 2024
**Classification:** Internal — Restricted

---

## Purpose

This memo establishes the revised counterparty credit-limit framework for Acme Corp's:

(i) Cash + cash-equivalent deposits with banks + AMCs;
(ii) Derivative + hedging counterparties;
(iii) Trade finance + working-capital counterparties.

Limits ensure no single counterparty failure (default, fraud, or major operational disruption) could expose more than **10% of Acme's consolidated cash position** to loss.

---

## Excluded Counterparties

The following are **explicitly EXCLUDED**:

(i) Cooperative banks (Multi-state or otherwise) — operational + governance concerns
(ii) NBFCs (regardless of rating) — except in specific trade-finance transactions approved on case-by-case basis
(iii) Microfinance institutions
(iv) Any bank or AMC that has been under RBI direction (e.g., PCA) within the prior 24 months
(v) Crypto exchanges + crypto-payment processors

## Concentration Reporting

The Treasurer will report monthly to the CFO + quarterly to the Board's Finance + Risk Committee:

- Outstanding exposure to each counterparty by category
- Aggregate exposure as % of Acme's total cash + hedge position
- Single-counterparty concentration vs limits
- Any breach (or near-breach within 90% of limit) — IMMEDIATE escalation

Limit breaches will require immediate Treasurer corrective action + CFO sign-off. Any sustained breach > 14 days requires Board notification.

---

## Limit Revision Triggers

The limits set out above will be **automatically revised** in the following situations:

(i) A rating downgrade of more than 1 notch by any of CRISIL, ICRA, India Ratings, or international agency → exposure to that counterparty must be reduced by 50% within 30 days OR completely run off within 90 days, whichever the CFO determines.

(ii) RBI imposes Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) on any bank in our universe → all deposits with that bank must be withdrawn within 7 days.

(iii) Any material adverse news event (e.g., fraud disclosure, governance scandal, central bank investigation) → CFO will make a same-day decision.

(iv) Annual review in January each year (next: January 2026).
