### Risk + Watch Items

(i) **Sector regulatory risk:** Healthcare sector subject to NPPA price-control updates + Maharashtra FDA inspections. Apollo Pune is generally compliant.

(ii) **Litigation exposure (industry-wide):** Possible product liability + medical malpractice suits. No material open litigation at Apollo Pune currently.

(iii) **AMC contract renewal:** Siemens Healthineers AMC for CT-scanner is annual — renewal due Nov 2025. Risk if Siemens revises pricing materially. RM aware.

(iv) **Cross-domain PII:** Apollo Pune handles patient records (Aadhaar/PAN/medical history). These are HIPAA-equivalent sensitive and NOT visible to HDFC. HDFC sees only the corporate banking layer.
