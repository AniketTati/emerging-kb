# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **11 March 2025** between **Helios Partners LLP** ("Helios") and **Vertex Industries Inc.** ("Vertex").

## 1. Purpose

Advisory engagement in connection with a potential **acquisition** by Vertex of a UK-based company in the document-intelligence sector. Sensitivity is high; deal-team containment is required.

## 2. Confidential Information

All non-public information disclosed by either Party, including target company identification, valuation models, due-diligence findings, transaction structures, and the existence of this engagement itself.

## 3. Heightened Obligations

3.1 Need-to-know basis strictly limited to the named deal team.

3.2 No electronic transmission outside the agreed virtual data room and counsel email channels.

3.3 The existence of this engagement, the identity of the target, and the fact that discussions are taking place shall all be treated as Confidential Information of the highest sensitivity.

## 4. Term and Perpetual Confidentiality

4.1 The active engagement term is **eighteen (18) months** from the Effective Date.

4.2 **Notwithstanding the term**, with respect to any information designated by the Disclosing Party as "Highly Sensitive" — including the identity of the target and the existence of the transaction — the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement shall be **perpetual** and shall survive any termination of this Agreement indefinitely.

> *(NOTE: The perpetual confidentiality undertaking in Section 4.2 is unusual in commercial NDAs and reflects the sensitivity of M&A target identification in the relevant industry. This Agreement should not be used as a template for ordinary engagements.)*

## 5. Equitable Remedies

5.1 The Parties acknowledge that monetary damages are likely inadequate; each Party is entitled to seek injunctive relief.

## 6. Governing Law

6.1 The Agreement is governed by the **laws of England and Wales**. Exclusive jurisdiction of the High Court in London.
