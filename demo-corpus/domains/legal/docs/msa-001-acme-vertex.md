# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Master Services Agreement** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of **March 15, 2024** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Acme Corp Pvt Ltd**, a private limited company organized under the laws of India, with its principal place of business at Plot 47, Sector 18, Gurugram, Haryana 122015, India ("**Customer**" or "**Acme**"); and

**Vertex Industries Inc.**, a corporation organized under the laws of the State of Delaware, with its principal place of business at 1010 Vertex Way, Palo Alto, CA 94306, USA ("**Vendor**" or "**Vertex**").

Acme and Vertex are each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties."

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Services**" means the document-intelligence platform services described in any Statement of Work executed under this Agreement.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.

1.3 "**Subscription Term**" means the period commencing on the Effective Date and continuing for an initial term of three (3) years, automatically renewing for successive one (1) year periods unless terminated under Section 9.

1.4 "**Fees**" means the amounts payable by Customer to Vendor as set forth in the applicable Statement of Work.

---

## 2. Services

2.1 Vendor shall provide the Services in accordance with the terms of this Agreement and any Statement of Work signed by both Parties.

2.2 Vendor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure the Services are available **99.5%** of each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.

2.3 Production incidents classified as **Severity-1** (system unavailable) shall be acknowledged within one (1) hour and resolved within **twenty-four (24) hours**. Severity-2 incidents shall be resolved within seventy-two (72) hours.

---

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Customer shall pay all Fees within **thirty (30) days** of receipt of a valid invoice. Payment terms are **NET-30** from invoice date.

3.2 Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

3.3 All Fees are exclusive of taxes; Customer is responsible for all applicable sales, use, GST, and withholding taxes.

3.4 Vendor may increase Fees upon renewal with at least sixty (60) days' written notice; in no event shall any annual increase exceed five percent (5%).

---

## 4. Term and Termination

4.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for the Subscription Term unless earlier terminated as set forth herein.

4.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach by the other Party that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice.

4.3 Upon termination, Customer shall pay all Fees accrued through the effective date of termination.

---

## 5. Limitation of Liability and Indemnification

5.1 EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF SECTION 7 (CONFIDENTIALITY) OR LIABILITY UNDER SECTION 5.3 (IP INDEMNIFICATION), EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR IN CONNECTION WITH THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE GREATER OF (i) **ONE MILLION U.S. DOLLARS ($1,000,000)** OR (ii) THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER TO VENDOR IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

5.2 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUES, OR LOST DATA.

5.3 **Indemnification.** Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer from and against any third-party claims alleging that Customer's authorized use of the Services infringes the third party's intellectual property rights, subject to the cap in Section 5.1.

---

## 6. Warranties

6.1 Each Party warrants that it has the corporate authority to enter into this Agreement.

6.2 Vendor warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the documentation for the duration of the Subscription Term.

6.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY PROVIDED HEREIN, VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION ANY WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party may disclose to the other Party certain Confidential Information. The receiving Party shall maintain the Confidential Information in confidence using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, but in no event less than reasonable care.

7.2 The confidentiality obligations under this Section shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of **five (5) years**.

---

## 8. Data Protection

8.1 Vendor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Data against accidental loss, destruction, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or access.

8.2 Vendor is certified to SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 standards.

---

## 9. Governing Law and Disputes

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the **State of Delaware, USA**, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

9.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules.

9.3 The seat of arbitration shall be Wilmington, Delaware. The language of arbitration shall be English.

---
