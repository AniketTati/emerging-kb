# SOFTWARE LICENSE AMENDMENT — WITH MFN PRICING

This **Software License Amendment** is entered into as of **April 18, 2025** between **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Licensor") and **Orion Consulting Group LLC** ("Licensee"), and amends the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated September 12, 2024 (the "**MSA**").

This Amendment governs Licensee's use of Acme's proprietary analytics tooling ("**Acme Insights Engine**" or the "**Software**").

---

## 1. License Grant

1.1 Subject to payment of the Fees, Licensor grants Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide license to install and use the Software for Licensee's internal business purposes.

1.2 The license is limited to **50 named users** and **5 production environments**.

---

## 2. Fees

2.1 Annual license fee: **USD 145,000**.

2.2 Renewal: 4% annual escalation, unless re-negotiated.

2.3 Payment terms: NET-30.

---

## 3. **MOST-FAVORED-NATION (MFN) PRICING — RARE CLAUSE**

3.1 **Most-Favored-Nation.** Licensor represents and warrants to Licensee that, throughout the term of this Amendment, the per-user license fee charged to Licensee shall not exceed the lowest per-user fee charged by Licensor to any other commercial customer of the Software for substantially similar usage, deployment scale, and contract terms.

3.2 **Quarterly Certification.** Within fifteen (15) days of the end of each calendar quarter, Licensor shall deliver to Licensee a signed certification (signed by the Licensor's CFO) attesting to compliance with the MFN obligation in Section 3.1.

3.3 **True-Up.** If Licensor offers any other commercial customer a lower per-user fee for substantially similar usage during the term, Licensor shall, within thirty (30) days, refund the difference to Licensee retroactively to the date such lower pricing first became effective.

3.4 **Audit.** Licensee may, no more than once per contract year and upon thirty (30) days' notice, audit Licensor's commercial agreements (under reasonable confidentiality protections) to verify compliance with the MFN obligation in this Section 3.

> *Counsel note (Mehta & Associates LLP, on behalf of Licensor): The MFN clause in this Amendment was negotiated specifically by Orion at the request of its General Counsel and is non-standard for our other commercial customers. We have advised Licensor that this clause materially constrains commercial flexibility. Recommend tracking MFN-eligible customers separately.*

---

## 4. Maintenance and Support

4.1 Standard 99.5% uptime; 24×5 support; quarterly version updates included.

## 5. IP and Restrictions

5.1 All right, title, and interest in the Software remains with Licensor.

5.2 Licensee shall not reverse engineer, decompile, sublicense, or distribute the Software.

## 6. Term and Termination

6.1 Initial term: three (3) years from the Effective Date.

6.2 Termination for material breach uncured for 30 days.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 Aggregate cap: 12 months' fees paid in the period preceding the claim.

## 8. Governing Law

State of New York, USA.
