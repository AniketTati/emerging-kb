# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

**This Lease Agreement** ("Lease") is entered into as of **February 1, 2024** by and between:

**Lakeview Properties Ltd**, a company incorporated in India with its registered office at Cyber Hub, DLF Phase 2, Gurugram, Haryana 122002 ("**Lessor**"); and

**Acme Corp Pvt Ltd**, a private limited company incorporated in India ("**Lessee**" or "**Tenant**").

---

## 1. Premises

1.1 The Lessor leases to the Lessee the office space located at:

> Plot 47, Sector 18, 8th Floor (entire floor), Gurugram, Haryana 122015, India
> Carpet area: **18,500 sq ft**
> Super built-up area: 24,000 sq ft

## 2. Term

2.1 **Lease term:** Five (5) years commencing on the Effective Date (Feb 1, 2024) and ending on January 31, 2029.

2.2 **Renewal option:** Lessee has one (1) option to renew for an additional three (3) years on the same terms, exercisable by written notice not less than nine (9) months prior to the expiration of the initial term.

## 3. Rent

3.1 Monthly rent: **INR 27,75,000** (Indian Rupees Twenty-Seven Lakh Seventy-Five Thousand), payable in advance on the first business day of each month.

3.2 **Escalation:** 7.5% escalation every twelve (12) months, effective on the anniversary of the Effective Date.

3.3 **Security deposit:** INR 1,66,50,000 (six months' rent), refundable upon termination minus any deductions for damages or unpaid rent.

## 4. Permitted Use

4.1 The Premises shall be used solely for general commercial office purposes consistent with Acme's information-technology services business.

## 5. Maintenance and Utilities

5.1 Lessor is responsible for structural maintenance, common-area upkeep, and building utilities up to the meter.

5.2 Lessee is responsible for internal repairs, electricity beyond the meter, and IT/network infrastructure.

5.3 Property taxes and municipal levies are payable by the Lessor.

## 6. Insurance

6.1 Lessee shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than INR 5 crore.

6.2 Lessor shall maintain building insurance.

## 7. Subletting and Assignment

7.1 Lessee may not sublet or assign without Lessor's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 8. Termination

8.1 Either Party may terminate this Lease for material breach uncured for 30 days after written notice.

8.2 Lessee may exercise an early-exit option after the third anniversary, on six (6) months' written notice, subject to forfeit of two (2) months' rent.

## 9. Governing Law

9.1 Indian law. Jurisdiction: courts of Gurugram, Haryana.
