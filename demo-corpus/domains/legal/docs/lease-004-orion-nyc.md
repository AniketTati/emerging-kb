# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is entered into as of **December 15, 2023** between **200 Park Avenue Tenant LLC** (Landlord) and **Orion Consulting Group LLC** (Tenant).

## 1. Premises
Suite 1850, 200 Park Avenue, New York, NY 10166, USA.
Rentable area: **9,400 sq ft**.

## 2. Term
**Seven (7) years** from January 1, 2024 through December 31, 2030.

## 3. Base Rent
- Year 1: USD 95.00 per sq ft per annum = USD 893,000 per annum, payable monthly in advance.
- Annual escalation: 3% straight-line.
- All rent exclusive of Operating Expenses, Real Estate Taxes, and Utilities (each per the Loadshare provisions in Sched. B).

## 4. Security Deposit
Letter of credit equal to twelve (12) months' base rent.

## 5. Use
General executive and administrative office, including consulting services.

## 6. Tenant Improvement Allowance
USD 60.00 per sq ft, payable upon completion of build-out in accordance with Landlord-approved plans.

## 7. Surrender
At expiration or termination, Tenant shall surrender the Premises in good order and condition, ordinary wear-and-tear excepted.

## 8. Subletting and Assignment
Permitted to affiliates without consent; to non-affiliates only with Landlord's prior written consent.

## 9. Indemnification
Standard mutual indemnification provisions.

## 10. Governing Law
Laws of the State of New York. Jurisdiction: New York County, NY.
