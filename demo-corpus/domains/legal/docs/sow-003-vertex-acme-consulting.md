# STATEMENT OF WORK — VERTEX CONSULTING ENGAGEMENT

Under the Vendor / SaaS Procurement Agreement between **Vertex Industries Inc.** (Vendor) and **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** (Customer) dated March 20, 2024.

**SOW Effective Date:** May 5, 2025.

## 1. Scope

Vertex shall provide consulting services to Customer in connection with the deployment of the Enterprise Plus tier features:
- (a) Workshop & training sessions with Customer's product, ops, and ML teams.
- (b) Solution-architecture review of Customer's integration patterns.
- (c) Build of 3 custom extraction templates for Customer's domain-specific document types.

## 2. Deliverables

1. **D1 (week 1):** Discovery workshop output + scoping document.
2. **D2 (week 3):** Architecture-review report.
3. **D3 (week 6):** 3 custom extraction templates with documentation.
4. **D4 (week 8):** Hand-off, knowledge-transfer, and final report.

## 3. Fees

3.1 Fixed-fee: **USD 95,000**, milestone-billed equally (4 milestones × 25%).

3.2 Travel/expenses: pre-approved, reimbursed at cost up to USD 12,000.

3.3 Payment terms per Vendor Agreement (NET-60).

## 4. Term

4.1 8 weeks from the SOW Effective Date.
