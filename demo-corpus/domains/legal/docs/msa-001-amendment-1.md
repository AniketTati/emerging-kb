# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Amendment No. 1** ("Amendment No. 1") is entered into as of **November 20, 2024** (the "Amendment Effective Date") by and between **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Acme") and **Vertex Industries Inc.** ("Vertex"), and amends that certain Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated March 15, 2024 (the "**Original Agreement**").

Capitalized terms not defined herein have the meanings ascribed to them in the Original Agreement.

---

## Recitals

WHEREAS, the Parties entered into the Original Agreement on March 15, 2024;

WHEREAS, in connection with Acme's expanded usage of the Services and increased volume commitments, the Parties wish to revise the payment terms and add a volume-discount provision;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. Amendment to Section 3 (Fees and Payment)

1.1 Section 3.1 of the Original Agreement is hereby **deleted in its entirety** and replaced with the following:

> **3.1** Customer shall pay all Fees within **forty-five (45) days** of receipt of a valid invoice. Payment terms are **NET-45** from invoice date.

1.2 A new Section 3.5 is added:

> **3.5 Volume Discount.** If Customer's annual Fees exceed five hundred thousand U.S. dollars ($500,000) in any contract year, Customer shall be entitled to a five percent (5%) credit applied to the following quarter's invoice.

---

## 2. Amendment to Section 2.3 (Service Levels)

2.1 Section 2.3 is hereby amended to add the following sentence at the end:

> Severity-1 incidents that remain unresolved beyond twenty-four (24) hours shall result in service credits equal to ten percent (10%) of the monthly Fee for each additional twelve (12) hour period of unresolved downtime, up to a maximum of fifty percent (50%) of the monthly Fee.

---

## 3. No Other Changes

3.1 Except as expressly modified by this Amendment No. 1, all other terms and conditions of the Original Agreement remain in full force and effect.

3.2 In the event of any inconsistency between this Amendment No. 1 and the Original Agreement, this Amendment No. 1 shall control.
