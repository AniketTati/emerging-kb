# SIDE LETTER TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Side Letter** ("Side Letter") is entered into as of **September 10, 2025** (the "Effective Date") between **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Acme") and **Vertex Industries Inc.** ("Vertex").

Reference is made to the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated March 15, 2024, as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated November 20, 2024 and Amendment No. 2 dated August 1, 2025 (collectively, the "**MSA**").

---

## Background

Following the August 1, 2025 amendment that extended the Agreement to a five-year term, and in light of Acme's commitment to expanded production usage of the Services across multiple regulated jurisdictions, the Parties have agreed to elevate certain liability and indemnification protections beyond the limits set forth in Section 5 of the MSA.

This Side Letter is executed by the Chief Executive Officers of both Parties and shall be deemed part of the MSA for all purposes.

---

## 1. Increased Liability Cap

1.1 Solely for purposes of indemnification under Section 5.3 of the MSA (IP indemnification), and notwithstanding the cap in Section 5.1:

> Vertex's total aggregate liability for IP indemnification claims arising out of the Services shall be capped at **five million U.S. dollars ($5,000,000)** in the aggregate, per contract year.

1.2 All other limitations in Section 5.1 remain in full force and effect.

---

## 2. Mutual Audit Right

2.1 Upon thirty (30) days' written notice, either Party may, no more than once per calendar year, audit the other Party's compliance with Section 7 (Confidentiality) and Section 8 (Data Protection) of the MSA, at the requesting Party's cost.

---

## 3. Confidentiality

3.1 The existence and terms of this Side Letter shall be treated as Confidential Information of both Parties under Section 7 of the MSA.

---
