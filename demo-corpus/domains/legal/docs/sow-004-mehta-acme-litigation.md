# STATEMENT OF WORK — LITIGATION DEFENSE ENGAGEMENT

Under the Counsel Engagement Letter between **Mehta & Associates LLP** and **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** dated February 15, 2024.

**Matter:** Defence of Acme Corp in **Sterling Vendor Solutions vs. Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** (Bombay High Court, suit no. 2025/3417/CIV), filed June 4, 2025. The plaintiff alleges breach of a vendor agreement and seeks damages of INR 4.20 crore.

**SOW Effective Date:** June 12, 2025.

## 1. Scope

1.1 Lead counsel for Acme's defence in the Sterling Vendor Solutions litigation, including:
- (a) Filing the written statement and any counter-claims;
- (b) Discovery, evidence assembly, and witness preparation;
- (c) Settlement negotiations as authorised by Client;
- (d) Trial conduct, including all interlocutory applications.

1.2 The lead partner shall be **Aisha Mehta, Esq.** The matter shall be staffed with one senior associate and two associates.

## 2. Billing

2.1 Hourly billing per the Engagement Letter rates (Partner INR 32,000, SA INR 19,000, Associate INR 11,000).

2.2 **Cap on Fees** for this matter through the trial-court phase: **INR 60,00,000** (Indian Rupees Sixty Lakh). Fees beyond the cap require Client's prior written approval.

2.3 Disbursements (court fees, expert witness fees) reimbursed at cost.

## 3. Reporting

3.1 Monthly status reports to Client; quarterly review meetings with Client's GC.

## 4. Term

4.1 Through final disposition at the Bombay High Court (estimated 18-24 months). Appeals require separate engagement.
