# VENDOR AGREEMENT

This Vendor Agreement is entered into as of **22 January 2025** between **Nimbus Technologies Pte Ltd** ("Vendor") and **Orion Consulting Group LLC** ("Customer").

## 1. Services
Cloud-infrastructure managed services for Orion's analytics platform: VPC provisioning, container orchestration, observability stack, and 24×7 NOC monitoring.

## 2. Fees
- Year 1: **SGD 420,000**, paid in 12 equal monthly installments in advance.
- Payment terms: NET-45.
- GST as applicable.

## 3. Service Levels
- Production uptime: 99.9% per month.
- Critical alerts: 1-hour response; 4-hour resolution target.
- Service credits per Annex A.

## 4. Data Residency
All Customer Data shall reside in Singapore. Cross-border replication only with Customer's prior written consent.

## 5. Confidentiality
Mutual. Survives termination for five (5) years.

## 6. Liability Cap
Aggregate liability capped at SGD 1,000,000 or the Fees paid in the preceding 12 months, whichever is greater.

## 7. Term
Initial term: two (2) years. Renewal options TBD on commercial terms 90 days before expiration.

## 8. Governing Law
Laws of Singapore. SIAC arbitration in Singapore.
