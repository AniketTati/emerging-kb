# UNILATERAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**This Unilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement** ("Agreement") is entered into as of **October 22, 2024** by **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Discloser") in favour of **Vertex Industries Inc.** ("Recipient").

This Agreement supplements (and does not supersede) the Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement between the Parties dated January 8, 2024.

## 1. Purpose

Discloser will disclose certain proprietary technical materials, source code, and customer datasets to Recipient solely for the purpose of evaluating Recipient's document-intelligence platform against Acme's production workloads (the "Evaluation").

## 2. Confidential Information

"**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient in connection with the Evaluation, including without limitation:

- (a) Acme's source code, architecture diagrams, and technical specifications;
- (b) Customer data, transaction histories, and analytics extracts;
- (c) Pricing, cost data, and contractual terms with third parties;
- (d) Acme's product roadmap, business strategy, and financial projections.

## 3. Obligations of Recipient

3.1 Recipient shall:
- (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;
- (b) use Confidential Information solely for the Evaluation;
- (c) **not retain any copies** of Confidential Information for longer than necessary to complete the Evaluation; and
- (d) destroy or return all Confidential Information within thirty (30) days of completion of the Evaluation.

3.2 Recipient shall **not** disclose Confidential Information to any third party (including affiliates) without Discloser's prior written consent.

## 4. Term

4.1 This Agreement shall be effective as of October 22, 2024 and shall continue in force for a period of **five (5) years**.

4.2 The confidentiality obligations herein shall survive termination of this Agreement indefinitely with respect to any information meeting the definition of a "trade secret" under applicable law.

## 5. No Reverse Engineering

5.1 Recipient shall not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or attempt to derive the source code of any software included in the Confidential Information.

## 6. Equitable Remedies

6.1 The Parties acknowledge that monetary damages may be inadequate to remedy a breach of this Agreement and that Discloser shall be entitled to seek injunctive and other equitable relief, in addition to any other available remedies.

## 7. Governing Law

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, USA.
