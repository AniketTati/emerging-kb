Dear Ms. Joshi,

We are delighted to offer you the position of **Director of Product** at Vertex Industries Inc. ("Vertex"). The terms of your employment are set forth below.

---

## 1. Position

1.1 **Title.** Director of Product, reporting to the Chief Product Officer.

1.2 **Work Location.** Vertex's Mumbai office (lease pending) and remote, with quarterly travel to Palo Alto, CA.

1.3 **Start Date.** December 15, 2024.

---

## 2. Compensation

2.1 **Base Salary.** **USD 195,000** per annum.

2.2 **Sign-on Bonus.** USD 25,000, payable in your first paycheck. Repayable on a pro-rata basis if you voluntarily resign within twelve (12) months.

2.3 **Performance Bonus.** Target 20% of base, subject to company and individual performance.

2.4 **Stock Options.** 15,000 ISOs of Vertex Industries Inc. at fair market value as of the grant date, vesting over four (4) years with one-year cliff.

---

## 3. Confidentiality and IP Assignment

3.1 You acknowledge that you will have access to highly proprietary information, including source code, model architectures, and customer data.

3.2 You agree to keep all such information confidential during and after your employment.

3.3 You assign to Vertex all right, title, and interest in any work product created during your employment that relates to Vertex's business.

---

## 4. IP Indemnification

4.1 You represent and warrant that no inventions, software, or other intellectual property you contribute to Vertex's products will infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.

4.2 **You agree to indemnify and hold Vertex harmless** from any claims arising from a breach of this representation, **without limit as to the amount** of such indemnification.

> **COUNSEL NOTE (Stark Levin & Co):** Section 4.2 imposes unlimited personal indemnification on the employee for IP infringement claims. This is a material departure from market norms, which typically cap an employee's IP indemnification obligation at twelve (12) months of base salary or impose no personal indemnification at all. We have advised the company that this provision may be unenforceable in certain jurisdictions and may make recruiting more difficult.

---

## 5. Non-Solicitation

5.1 During employment and for eighteen (18) months thereafter, you shall not solicit Vertex employees or customers.

---

## 6. Non-Competition

5.1 During employment and for twelve (12) months thereafter, you shall not engage with any business that competes with Vertex in the document-intelligence sector. Enforceability is subject to applicable law (including California Business & Professions Code §16600).

---

## 7. Termination

7.1 At-will employment. Either Party may terminate at any time, with or without cause, subject to applicable law.

7.2 Vertex shall provide written notice and any required statutory benefits upon termination.

---
