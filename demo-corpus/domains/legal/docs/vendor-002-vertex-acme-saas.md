# VENDOR / SAAS PROCUREMENT AGREEMENT

This Vendor Agreement is entered into as of **March 20, 2024** between **Vertex Industries Inc.** (as "Vendor") and **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** (as "Customer"). This Agreement operates alongside the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated March 15, 2024 (the "MSA"); to the extent of inconsistency the MSA controls.

## 1. Subscription

1.1 Customer subscribes to Vertex's **Enterprise Tier** SaaS platform for an initial term of three (3) years.

1.2 Subscription includes:
- (a) Document-intelligence platform access for up to 250 named users;
- (b) 100 million document-pages per annum quota;
- (c) Dedicated tenant on `ap-south-1`;
- (d) 24×7 Severity-1 support.

## 2. Annual Subscription Fees

2.1 Year 1: **USD 750,000**.

2.2 Years 2-3: subject to up to 5% annual increase per Section 3.4 of the MSA.

2.3 Payment terms as per MSA (currently NET-60 per Amendment No. 2).

## 3. Usage Overages

3.1 Document-page overage: USD 0.012 per page above the annual quota, billed monthly in arrears.

## 4. Acceptable Use

4.1 Customer agrees not to use the platform for unlawful purposes, to reverse-engineer the platform, or to benchmark against competitors' products without Vertex's prior written consent.

## 5. Security

5.1 Vertex maintains SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certification; annual reports shared with Customer under NDA.

## 6. Term and Termination

6.1 Three (3) years; auto-renewal subject to MSA termination rights.

## 7. Governing Law

7.1 As per MSA: laws of the State of Delaware, USA.
