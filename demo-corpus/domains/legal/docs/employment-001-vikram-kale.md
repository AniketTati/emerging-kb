## 1. Position

1.1 **Title.** Senior Software Engineer (Grade SSE-2), reporting to the Director of Engineering — Platform Infrastructure.

1.2 **Work Location.** Bangalore, Karnataka. Hybrid work arrangement; minimum two (2) days per week in office.

1.3 **Start Date.** April 1, 2025, or such other date as mutually agreed in writing.

---

## 2. Compensation

2.1 **Base Salary.** Annual cost-to-company (CTC) of **INR 38,40,000** (Indian Rupees Thirty-Eight Lakh Forty Thousand), payable in twelve equal monthly installments.

2.2 **Variable Pay.** Annual performance bonus targeted at 15% of base salary, payable upon achievement of mutually-agreed objectives.

2.3 **Joining Bonus.** A one-time joining bonus of **INR 4,00,000**, payable in the first month's salary. The joining bonus is subject to recovery, on a pro-rata basis, if you voluntarily resign or are terminated for cause within twelve (12) months of joining.

2.4 **Equity.** You will be granted **5,000 RSUs** of Acme Corp Pvt Ltd, vesting over four (4) years with a one-year cliff, in accordance with the Company's Equity Incentive Plan.

---

## 3. Benefits

3.1 Health insurance for self and dependents (sum insured INR 10,00,000 per family per annum).

3.2 Group term life insurance of INR 1,00,00,000.

3.3 24 days of annual paid leave + 8 statutory holidays.

3.4 Provident Fund and Gratuity per applicable law.

## 4. Probation

4.1 You shall be on probation for a period of six (6) months from the Start Date. The Company may extend the probation period by up to three (3) additional months.

4.2 During probation, either Party may terminate this employment with fifteen (15) days' notice.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 You acknowledge that during the course of your employment you will have access to Acme's Confidential Information.

5.2 You agree to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence both during your employment and after termination thereof, indefinitely with respect to trade secrets.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 All inventions, software, designs, and other intellectual property created by you during the course of your employment, whether on Acme's premises or elsewhere, that relate to Acme's business or use Acme's resources, shall be the sole and exclusive property of Acme.

6.2 You hereby assign to Acme all right, title, and interest in such Work Product.

## 7. Non-Competition

7.1 You agree that, **during your employment and for a period of thirty-six (36) months following termination thereof**, you shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in or accept employment with any business that competes with Acme in the document-intelligence sector in India, the United States, or Singapore.

> **NOTE (counsel's comment):** The 36-month post-termination non-compete period exceeds the customary 6 to 12 months typically seen for similar roles. This provision reflects the seniority and access associated with this position. Enforceability is subject to applicable law, including section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, and will require judicial assessment of reasonableness.

## 8. Non-Solicitation

8.1 During your employment and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, you shall not directly or indirectly solicit any employee, customer, or business partner of Acme.

9.2 The Company may terminate this employment immediately for cause, including without limitation: gross misconduct, willful breach of duty, fraud, conviction of a criminal offense, or material breach of this Agreement.
