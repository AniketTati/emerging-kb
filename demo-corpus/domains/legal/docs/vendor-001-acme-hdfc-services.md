# VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **July 12, 2024** between **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** (as "Vendor") and **HDFC Bank Ltd** (as "Customer").

> *Cross-domain note: HDFC Bank also appears as the counterparty bank in the Finance corpus (loan agreements + bank statements). Identity resolution must collapse these references to the same canonical entity.*

## 1. Services

1.1 Vendor shall provide enterprise document-intelligence services to Customer, including:
- (a) document classification and routing automation;
- (b) entity extraction from KYC documents and account statements;
- (c) loan-application processing assistance for the Customer's retail banking operations.

1.2 Specific deliverables, SLAs, and pricing shall be set forth in Statements of Work signed under this Agreement.

## 2. Fees

2.1 Annual fee: **INR 4,80,00,000** (Indian Rupees Four Crore Eighty Lakh) for Year 1.

2.2 Payment terms: **NET-45** from invoice date.

## 3. Data Protection

3.1 Vendor shall comply with all applicable data-protection laws in India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

3.2 Customer Data shall be stored within India only (data localisation).

3.3 Vendor shall implement encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3+).

3.4 Vendor shall not subprocess Customer Data without Customer's prior written consent.

## 4. Service Levels

4.1 Vendor shall maintain platform availability of **99.9%** of each calendar month.

4.2 Severity-1 incident response: 30 minutes; resolution target: 4 hours.

4.3 Service credits apply per the schedule in Annex A.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Confidentiality obligations survive termination for seven (7) years.

## 6. Liability Cap

6.1 Aggregate liability capped at INR 25,00,00,000 (Twenty-Five Crore) per contract year.

## 7. Term and Termination

7.1 Initial term: three (3) years.

7.2 Customer may terminate for convenience on 180 days' written notice.

## 8. Governing Law

8.1 Indian law. Mumbai courts.
