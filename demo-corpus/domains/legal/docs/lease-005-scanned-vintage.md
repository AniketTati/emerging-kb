# COMMERCIAL LEASE DEED

[Stamp paper, Government of Maharashtra, INR 100/-]

This LEASE DEED is made and executed on this **15th day of June, 2008** at Mumbai, by and between:

**Patel Industries Private Limited**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act 1956, having its registered office at 21, Veer Nariman Road, Fort, Mumbai - 400001 ("**LESSOR**");

AND

**Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** (formerly Acme Soft Solutions Pvt Ltd), a private limited company incorporated under the Companies Act 1956, with its principal office at A-15, MIDC, Andheri East, Mumbai - 400093 ("**LESSEE**").

---

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of office premises at:

> 4th Floor, Patel Chambers, 21 Veer Nariman Road, Fort, Mumbai - 400001
> admeasuring 2,400 sq ft (built-up)

AND WHEREAS the Lessee is desirous of taking the said premises on lease for its business operations;

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

## 1. TERM
The Lease shall be for a period of **THREE (3) YEARS** commencing 1st July 2008, with renewal option subject to mutual agreement.

## 2. RENT
Monthly rent of **INR 1,80,000** (Rupees One Lakh Eighty Thousand only), payable in advance by the 5th of each month.

## 3. DEPOSIT
Security deposit of INR 10,80,000 (six months' rent), refundable on vacating the premises after deductions if any.

## 4. ESCALATION
Rent shall escalate by 15% upon renewal (every 3 years).

## 5. USE
The premises shall be used solely for software development and administrative offices.

## 6. MAINTENANCE
The Lessor shall maintain the structure and common areas. The Lessee shall maintain the interior.

## 7. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Lease with three (3) months' written notice after the expiry of the initial term.

## 8. GOVERNING LAW
This Deed is governed by the laws of India. Bombay courts shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
