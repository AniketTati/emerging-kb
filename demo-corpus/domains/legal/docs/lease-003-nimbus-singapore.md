# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is entered into as of **1 April 2024** between **Prudential Tower (Cecil) Pte Ltd** (Landlord) and **Nimbus Technologies Pte Ltd** (Tenant).

## 1. Demised Premises
Unit #15-00, Prudential Tower, 30 Cecil Street, Singapore 049712.
Net floor area: **6,750 sq ft**.

## 2. Term
**Three (3) years** from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2027.

## 3. Rent and Service Charge
- Monthly rent: **SGD 47,250** (S$7 per sq ft).
- Service charge: SGD 6,750 per month.
- GST (8%): applicable to both rent and service charge.

## 4. Deposit
Three (3) months' gross rent payable on or before Possession Date.

## 5. Use
General office use (cloud-infrastructure software development).

## 6. Reinstatement
Tenant shall reinstate the Premises to bare-shell condition at termination, ordinary wear-and-tear excepted.

## 7. Insurance
Tenant to maintain public liability of S$5,000,000 and to insure its own contents.

## 8. Renewal Option
One (1) option to renew for three (3) years at then-prevailing market rent (subject to a 5% floor and 15% ceiling on rental increase).

## 9. Governing Law
Laws of Singapore. Singapore courts have exclusive jurisdiction.
