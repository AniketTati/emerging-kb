# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **August 30, 2024** by **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Acme") and **Orion Consulting Group LLC** ("Orion").

## 1. Purpose
Strategic consulting engagement evaluation, including review of Acme's competitive positioning, market expansion options, and operating model.

## 2. Confidential Information
All non-public information disclosed by either Party in connection with the Purpose, including strategy decks, financial models, customer lists, pricing data, and operational metrics.

## 3. Obligations
3.1 Receiving Party shall: (a) hold Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use it solely for the Purpose; (c) not disclose to third parties without prior written consent.

3.2 Standard need-to-know exception applies to employees, advisors, and contractors bound by equivalent obligations.

## 4. Term
**Three (3) years** from the Effective Date. Confidentiality survives an additional **five (5) years**.

## 5. Exclusions
Standard carve-outs for publicly known information, independently developed information, and information required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice).

## 6. Governing Law
Laws of the **State of New York, USA**. Disputes submitted to the state and federal courts located in New York County.
