# UNILATERAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (RFP RESPONSE)

This Unilateral NDA ("Agreement") is entered into as of **July 4, 2024** by **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Discloser") in favour of **Orion Consulting Group LLC** ("Recipient"), in connection with Orion's submission of a Request for Proposal response to Acme.

## 1. Purpose

Acme is conducting an RFP process for strategic advisory services and may disclose to Orion certain non-public information about Acme's business, growth plans, and competitive positioning to enable Orion to prepare its proposal.

## 2. Confidential Information

Includes: Acme's RFP brief, evaluation criteria, internal cost models, vendor short-lists, and any information shared during clarification calls.

## 3. Restricted Use

3.1 Recipient may use Confidential Information solely to prepare and submit its RFP response.

3.2 Recipient shall not use Confidential Information to inform consulting engagements with Acme's competitors.

3.3 Standard need-to-know exception applies for proposal team only.

## 4. Term

4.1 Two (2) years from the Effective Date; confidentiality obligations survive an additional three (3) years.

## 5. Return / Destruction

5.1 Upon conclusion of the RFP process (selection or rejection), Orion shall return or destroy all Confidential Information within thirty (30) days of written request.

## 6. Governing Law

State of New York, USA. Exclusive jurisdiction in New York County.
