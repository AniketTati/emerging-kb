# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Master Services Agreement** is entered into as of **5 January 2025** by and between:

**Helios Partners LLP**, a limited liability partnership organised under the laws of England and Wales with its registered office at 1 Finsbury Avenue, London EC2M 2PP, United Kingdom ("**Helios**" or "**Advisor**"); and

**Acme Corp Pvt Ltd**, a private limited company incorporated in India ("**Client**" or "**Acme**").

---

## 1. Scope of Engagement

1.1 Helios shall provide advisory services to Acme in connection with corporate strategy, capital structure, and potential M&A opportunities in the South Asian region.

1.2 The specific scope, fees, and deliverables of each engagement shall be set out in separate Engagement Letters executed under this Agreement.

---

## 2. Fees

2.1 Helios shall be entitled to advisory fees as set out in each Engagement Letter, payable within thirty (30) days of invoice.

2.2 Success fees, where applicable, shall be calculated as a percentage of transaction value as agreed in writing on a deal-by-deal basis.

---

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

3.1 Helios shall promptly disclose to Acme any actual or potential conflicts of interest that arise during the engagement.

3.2 Helios may, with Acme's prior written consent, act for parties whose interests are or may become adverse to those of Acme.

---

## 4. Limitation of Liability

4.1 Helios's total liability shall be limited to the lesser of (i) fees paid by Acme in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim or (ii) ten million U.S. dollars (USD 10,000,000).

4.2 Subject to applicable law, neither Party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or special losses.

---

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party shall hold the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence for a period of seven (7) years from disclosure.

---

## 6. Term

6.1 This Agreement shall continue until terminated by either Party upon ninety (90) days' written notice.

---

## 7. Governing Law and Disputes

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the **laws of England and Wales**.

7.2 The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England.
