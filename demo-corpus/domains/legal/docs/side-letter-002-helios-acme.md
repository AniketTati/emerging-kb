# SIDE LETTER — INDEMNITY CARVE-OUT (HELIOS-ACME MSA)

This Side Letter is entered into as of **8 February 2025** between **Helios Partners LLP** ("Helios") and **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Client").

Reference is made to the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated 5 January 2025 (the "**MSA**").

## 1. Background

The Parties have agreed to an additional protection for Client in connection with regulatory advice provided by Helios in the context of capital-markets matters.

## 2. Indemnity Carve-out

2.1 The exclusions to Helios's liability in Section 4 of the MSA shall **not apply** to claims arising from Helios's gross negligence or wilful misconduct in connection with capital-markets advice.

2.2 In such cases, Helios's liability shall be capped at **USD 25,000,000** in the aggregate per matter (rather than the lower limit in Section 4.1 of the MSA).

## 3. Audit Right

3.1 Client may, upon 30 days' notice, audit Helios's compliance with applicable regulatory frameworks (including FCA rules where relevant) once per calendar year.

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 The existence of this Side Letter is itself Confidential Information.

## 5. Effect

5.1 Except as expressly modified, all other provisions of the MSA remain in full force and effect.
