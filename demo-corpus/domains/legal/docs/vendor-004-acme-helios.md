# VENDOR AGREEMENT

This Vendor Agreement is entered into as of **8 November 2024** between **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Vendor") and **Helios Partners LLP** ("Customer").

## 1. Services
Vendor shall provide its document-intelligence platform to Customer for use in Customer's deal-room and due-diligence workflows.

## 2. Fees
- Annual subscription: **USD 320,000**.
- Payment: NET-45, invoiced quarterly in advance.
- Usage overage: USD 0.010 per page above 30 million annual quota.

## 3. Acceptable Use
Platform shall be used solely for advisory engagements; not to be sub-licensed to Customer's own clients without written consent.

## 4. Service Levels
- 99.5% monthly uptime.
- 1-hour acknowledgement on Severity-1; 24-hour resolution target.

## 5. Confidentiality and Data Protection
- Heightened obligations given the M&A nature of Customer's use cases.
- Data residency: EU only.
- GDPR DPA executed concurrently as Schedule 1.

## 6. Liability Cap
USD 1,000,000 aggregate per contract year.

## 7. Term
Two (2) years. Renewal at then-prevailing rates with 90 days' notice.

## 8. Governing Law
Laws of England and Wales.
