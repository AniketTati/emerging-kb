# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is entered into as of **September 10, 2023** between **Lakeview Properties Ltd** (Lessor) and **Vertex Industries Inc.** (acting through its Indian subsidiary, Vertex India Pvt Ltd) (Lessee).

## 1. Premises
Office space at Lodha Park, Lower Parel, Mumbai 400013, 5th Floor (partial).
Carpet area: **8,200 sq ft**; super built-up: 11,400 sq ft.

## 2. Term
**Three (3) years** from the Effective Date through September 9, 2026. One renewal option for two (2) additional years on the same terms (subject to escalation).

## 3. Rent
- Monthly rent: **INR 19,80,000** (Indian Rupees Nineteen Lakh Eighty Thousand).
- Escalation: 5% every 12 months.
- Security deposit: INR 1,18,80,000 (six months' rent).

## 4. Permitted Use
General commercial office purposes — Vertex India product, sales, and operations teams.

## 5. Maintenance and Utilities
Standard division between Lessor (structural + common areas) and Lessee (interior + connectivity).

## 6. Subletting
Permitted to affiliates (including parent Vertex Industries Inc.) without consent; to non-affiliates only with Lessor's prior written consent.

## 7. Termination
Either Party may terminate for material breach uncured for 30 days. Lessee early-exit after 24 months on 90 days' notice (forfeit of 1.5 months' rent).

## 8. Governing Law
Indian law. Jurisdiction: Mumbai courts.
