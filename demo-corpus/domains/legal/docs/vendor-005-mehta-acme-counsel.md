# COUNSEL ENGAGEMENT LETTER

**Mehta & Associates LLP**
Express Towers, Nariman Point, Mumbai 400021, India

To: **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd**
Date: **February 15, 2024**

Re: **General Counsel Engagement**

Dear Robert,

We are pleased to confirm the engagement of Mehta & Associates LLP ("the Firm") to provide ongoing legal counsel to Acme Corp Pvt Ltd ("the Client").

## 1. Scope of Engagement

1.1 General corporate-commercial advice, contract review, regulatory advice (Indian law), and dispute support (other than litigation conduct, which is engaged separately under per-matter SOWs).

1.2 The Firm will act as Indian-law counsel of record on Client's commercial contracts with international counterparties.

## 2. Billing

2.1 Hourly rates (FY 2024-25):
- Partner: INR 32,000 per hour;
- Senior Associate: INR 19,000 per hour;
- Associate: INR 11,000 per hour.

2.2 Monthly retainer: **INR 6,00,000**, covering up to 25 partner-equivalent hours per month. Hours above retainer billed at the rates above.

2.3 Out-of-pocket expenses (e.g., court fees, stamp duty, third-party translation) reimbursed at cost.

## 3. Conflicts

3.1 The Firm shall promptly disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest. The Firm currently represents the following parties that have business relationships with Acme: [list to be maintained separately].

## 4. Confidentiality and Privilege

4.1 All communications between the Firm and the Client are protected by attorney-client privilege.

4.2 The Firm shall maintain client confidentiality indefinitely.

## 5. Termination

5.1 Either Party may terminate this engagement on 30 days' written notice; on termination, the Firm shall transfer all client files to the Client or its nominated successor counsel.

## 6. Governing Law

Indian law, Mumbai jurisdiction.

If this engagement is acceptable, please sign and return.

Sincerely,

**Aisha Mehta, Esq.**
Partner, Mehta & Associates LLP
