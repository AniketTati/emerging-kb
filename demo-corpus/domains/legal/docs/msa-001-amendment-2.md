# AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Amendment No. 2** ("Amendment No. 2") is entered into as of **August 1, 2025** (the "Amendment Effective Date") by and between **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Acme") and **Vertex Industries Inc.** ("Vertex"), and further amends that certain Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated March 15, 2024 (as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated November 20, 2024) (the "**Agreement**").

Capitalized terms not defined herein have the meanings ascribed to them in the Agreement.

---

## Recitals

WHEREAS, in connection with Acme's expanded multi-region deployment and Vertex's launch of the Enterprise tier (including dedicated tenancy options), the Parties wish to extend payment terms to align with Acme's expanded commercial activity and to address several operational provisions;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to extend the term of the Agreement and confirm certain SLA exclusions agreed in operational discussions;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. Amendment to Section 3 (Fees and Payment)

1.1 Section 3.1 (as amended by Amendment No. 1) is hereby **deleted in its entirety** and replaced with the following:

> **3.1** Customer shall pay all Fees within **sixty (60) days** of receipt of a valid invoice. Payment terms are **NET-60** from invoice date.

1.2 The volume discount provision in Section 3.5 (added by Amendment No. 1) is hereby **amended** as follows:

> **3.5 Volume Discount.** If Customer's annual Fees exceed seven hundred fifty thousand U.S. dollars ($750,000) in any contract year, Customer shall be entitled to an **eight percent (8%)** credit applied to the following quarter's invoice.

---

## 2. Amendment to Section 4 (Term and Termination)

2.1 Section 4.1 of the Agreement is hereby amended to extend the initial Subscription Term from three (3) years to **five (5) years**, with the new termination date being March 14, 2029.

2.2 Section 4.2 is hereby amended to add the following sentence at the end:

> Notwithstanding the foregoing, neither Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience during the initial Subscription Term.

---

## 3. New Section 8.3 — Customer Data Residency

3.1 A new Section 8.3 is added to the Agreement:

> **8.3 Data Residency.** Vendor shall store Customer Data in Vendor's `us-east-1` and `ap-south-1` regions only. Vendor shall not transfer Customer Data outside these regions without Customer's prior written consent.

---

## 4. No Other Changes

4.1 Except as expressly modified by this Amendment No. 2, all other terms and conditions of the Agreement remain in full force and effect.

4.2 In the event of any inconsistency between this Amendment No. 2 and any prior version of the Agreement (including Amendment No. 1), this Amendment No. 2 shall control.
