# STATEMENT OF WORK — STRATEGY CONSULTING

Under the MSA between **Orion Consulting Group LLC** ("Consultant") and **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Client") dated September 12, 2024.

**SOW Effective Date:** September 1, 2025.

## 1. Project: International Expansion Strategy

Orion shall develop a strategy roadmap for Acme's expansion into the EMEA market (UK + Singapore as priority territories, Germany + UAE as second wave).

## 2. Deliverables

1. **Phase 1 (weeks 1-4) — Market analysis:** Sizing, competitive landscape, regulatory profile per territory.
2. **Phase 2 (weeks 5-8) — Entry-model evaluation:** Direct sales vs. partner-led, with go-to-market sizing for each.
3. **Phase 3 (weeks 9-12) — Recommendation + 24-month roadmap:** Detailed roadmap with hiring plan, capital required, milestones.

## 3. Fees

3.1 Fixed-fee: **USD 285,000**, billed in three equal installments (USD 95,000 each, due at the end of each phase).

3.2 Travel reimbursed per the MSA (per-diem cap USD 250).

3.3 Payment terms: NET-30.

- Lead Partner: TBD.
- 2× Engagement Managers + 4× Consultants.
- Estimated effort: 1,800 hours total.

## 5. Term

12 weeks from SOW Effective Date.
