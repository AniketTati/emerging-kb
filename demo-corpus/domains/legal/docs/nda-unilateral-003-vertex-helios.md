# UNILATERAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (M&A DUE DILIGENCE)

This Unilateral NDA is entered into as of **February 18, 2025** by **Vertex Industries Inc.** ("Discloser") in favour of **Helios Partners LLP** ("Recipient") in connection with a potential capital-raising or strategic transaction.

## 1. Purpose

Vertex is exploring a Series C-extension capital raise and may disclose to Helios (acting as advisor) financial information, customer metrics, board materials, and strategic plans to evaluate transaction structure.

## 2. Confidential Information

All non-public information disclosed by Vertex or any of its subsidiaries to Helios in connection with the Purpose, including:
- Audited and unaudited financial statements;
- Customer concentration and ARR breakdowns;
- Board minutes and shareholder agreements;
- Cap table and equity history;
- Product roadmap and strategic plans.

## 3. Use, Care, and Non-Solicitation

3.1 Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for advising Vertex on the contemplated transaction.

3.2 Recipient shall not solicit any current Vertex employees identified in the Confidential Information for a period of eighteen (18) months from the Effective Date.

3.3 Standard need-to-know exception applies to named deal team only.

## 4. Term

4.1 The active engagement runs for twelve (12) months; confidentiality obligations survive an additional five (5) years (perpetual for any trade secrets).

## 5. Equitable Remedies and Indemnification

5.1 Vertex is entitled to seek injunctive relief for any breach.

5.2 Recipient indemnifies Vertex for any breach by its personnel.

## 6. Governing Law

Laws of the State of Delaware, USA.
