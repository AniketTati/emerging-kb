# UNILATERAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (ARCHITECTURE REVIEW)

This Unilateral NDA is entered into as of **25 April 2024** by **Nimbus Technologies Pte Ltd** ("Discloser") in favour of **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Recipient").

## 1. Purpose

Nimbus will disclose to Acme certain proprietary cloud architecture documentation, scaling models, and security control matrices, to enable Acme to evaluate Nimbus's suitability as a cloud infrastructure partner for Acme's production workloads.

## 2. Confidential Information

Includes Nimbus's internal architecture diagrams, infrastructure-as-code repositories (read-only access), SOC 2 audit details, customer reference architectures, and any data shared during evaluation calls.

## 3. Permitted Use

3.1 Acme may use Confidential Information solely to evaluate Nimbus's offerings.

3.2 Acme shall not share Confidential Information with any of Nimbus's competitors (a list to be provided by Nimbus on request).

3.3 Read-only repository access shall be revoked upon completion of the evaluation; no copies or forks may be retained.

## 4. Term

4.1 Three (3) years from the Effective Date.

## 5. Governing Law

5.1 Governed by the **laws of Singapore**; disputes referred to SIAC arbitration in Singapore.
