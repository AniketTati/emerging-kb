# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Master Services Agreement** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of **June 1, 2024** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Acme Corp Pvt Ltd**, a private limited company organized under the laws of India ("**Customer**"); and

**Nimbus Technologies Pte Ltd**, a company incorporated in Singapore with its registered office at 30 Cecil Street, #15-00, Prudential Tower, Singapore 049712 ("**Nimbus**").

---

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Services**" means cloud infrastructure, container orchestration, and disaster-recovery services provided by Nimbus pursuant to Statements of Work executed under this Agreement.

1.2 "**Production Environment**" means any Customer environment designated by Customer in writing as production-tier.

1.3 "**Subscription Term**" means an initial period of two (2) years from the Effective Date, automatically renewing for successive one (1) year periods.

---

## 2. Service Levels — Production Environment

2.1 **Uptime.** Nimbus shall maintain Production Environment availability of at least **99.95%** of each calendar month, measured as ((total minutes — downtime minutes) ÷ total minutes) × 100.

2.2 **Severity-1 Incident Response — Production Environment ONLY.** For Severity-1 incidents (defined as a complete service outage affecting Production), Nimbus shall:

> (a) acknowledge the incident within fifteen (15) minutes of Customer's notice, and
> (b) **deliver a remediation, workaround, or restoration of service within four (4) hours** of acknowledgment.

This four-hour Severity-1 remediation commitment is a material obligation of Nimbus under this Agreement.

2.3 **Severity-2 Incident Response.** Nimbus shall respond within one (1) hour and resolve within twenty-four (24) hours.

2.4 **Service Credits.** Failure to meet Section 2.2 (the 4-hour Production Severity-1 SLA) shall entitle Customer to a service credit of fifteen percent (15%) of the monthly Fees for each Severity-1 incident not remediated within the four-hour window.

---

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Customer shall pay all Fees within **forty-five (45) days** of receipt of a valid invoice. Payment terms are **NET-45** from invoice date.

3.2 All Fees are denominated in Singapore Dollars (SGD).

3.3 GST and other applicable taxes are the responsibility of Customer.

---

## 4. Data Residency

4.1 Nimbus shall store and process Customer Data exclusively in Singapore and, with Customer's prior written consent, in Mumbai (India). No transfer of Customer Data outside these regions is permitted under this Agreement.

---

## 5. Limitation of Liability

5.1 EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF SECTION 8 (CONFIDENTIALITY) OR LIABILITY UNDER SECTION 6 (IP INDEMNIFICATION), EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE GREATER OF (i) **TWO MILLION SINGAPORE DOLLARS (SGD 2,000,000)** OR (ii) THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE TO NIMBUS IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

5.2 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

---

## 6. IP Indemnification

6.1 Nimbus shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer from third-party claims that the Services infringe such third party's intellectual property rights, subject to the cap in Section 5.1.

---

## 7. Term and Termination

7.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice.

7.2 Customer may terminate for convenience upon ninety (90) days' written notice, subject to payment of accrued Fees through the effective date of termination.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 Each Party shall protect the other's Confidential Information using the same degree of care as it uses for its own. Confidentiality obligations survive termination for **three (3) years**.

---

## 9. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the **laws of Singapore**, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

9.2 Any dispute shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the **Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC)** under the SIAC Rules then in force.

9.3 The seat of arbitration shall be **Singapore**. The language of arbitration shall be **English**. The tribunal shall consist of one (1) arbitrator.

---

## 10. Miscellaneous

10.1 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all Statements of Work executed hereunder, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

10.2 **Amendments.** Amendments must be in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

10.3 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond its reasonable control.
