# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Master Services Agreement** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of **September 12, 2024** (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Acme Corp Pvt Ltd**, a private limited company organized under the laws of India ("**Customer**"); and

**Orion Consulting Group LLC**, a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Delaware, with its principal place of business at 200 Park Avenue, New York, NY 10166, USA ("**Orion**" or "**Consultant**").

---

## 1. Services

1.1 Orion shall provide strategy and management consulting services to Acme on a project-by-project basis, as set forth in Statements of Work signed under this Agreement.

1.2 Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope of work, deliverables, milestones, fees, and timeline.

---

## 2. Fees and Expenses

2.1 Fees shall be invoiced monthly in arrears.

2.2 Payment terms are **NET-30** from invoice date.

2.3 Reasonable, pre-approved travel expenses shall be reimbursed at cost; per-diem rates are capped at $250 per consultant per day.

---

## 3. Intellectual Property

3.1 All deliverables prepared specifically for Acme under a Statement of Work ("**Work Product**") shall be deemed "work made for hire" under U.S. copyright law and shall be the sole property of Acme upon payment in full.

3.2 Orion retains ownership of its pre-existing methodologies, frameworks, and tools ("**Background IP**") used in creating the Work Product, and grants Acme a perpetual, royalty-free license to use such Background IP solely as embedded in the Work Product.

---

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 Each Party shall protect the other's Confidential Information. Confidentiality obligations survive termination for five (5) years.

---

## 5. Limitation of Liability

5.1 Each Party's total liability shall not exceed the Fees paid by Acme to Orion in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

5.2 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages.

---

## 6. Term and Termination

6.1 This Agreement shall continue until terminated by either Party upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

6.2 Outstanding Statements of Work shall survive termination through completion unless separately terminated.

---

## 7. Governing Law

7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the **State of New York**, USA, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

7.2 Disputes shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New York County, New York.
