## 1. Introduction

You have asked us to provide a risk-assessment memorandum in connection with the proposed follow-on Master Services Agreement with Acme Corp Pvt Ltd, currently in draft form (the "**Follow-on MSA**").

## 2. Summary of Open Risks

2.1 **Term length.** The proposed seven-year term substantially exceeds the existing five-year arrangement. **[TBD: stress-test exit scenarios at year 3, 5, 7.]**

2.2 **NET-90 payment terms** materially extend the existing NET-60 arrangement. Working-capital impact estimated at USD 2-3M float on an annualized basis.

2.3 **Liability cap raised to USD 10M.** Customary for high-tier enterprise SaaS but materially above our other Tier-1 customer caps. [Risk to be discussed with insurance broker — current E&O coverage may need uplift.]

2.4 **Customer's request for a 5-year out-clause** is materially adverse to Vertex's revenue forecast. [Partner-level discussion required.]

## 3. Recommendation [TENTATIVE]

We tentatively recommend that Vertex:
- (a) Accept the NET-90 terms subject to a renegotiation trigger if Acme's credit profile deteriorates by more than one notch (S&P);
- (b) Push back on the 5-year out-clause; counter-propose a 5-year no-termination period followed by a 2-year early-termination window with a buyout fee;
- (c) Increase liability cap subject to procurement of additional E&O coverage.
