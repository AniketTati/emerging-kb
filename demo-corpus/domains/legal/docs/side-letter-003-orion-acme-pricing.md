# SIDE LETTER — PRICING ADJUSTMENT (ORION-ACME MSA)

This Side Letter is entered into as of **July 22, 2025** between **Orion Consulting Group LLC** ("Orion") and **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Client").

Reference is made to the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated September 12, 2024 (the "**MSA**").

## 1. Background

In recognition of Client's expanded use of Orion's consulting services across multiple strategic initiatives (including the international-expansion project under SOW dated 2025-09-01), the Parties have agreed to adjust certain pricing terms for the remainder of 2025 and calendar year 2026.

## 2. Per-Diem Cap Increase

2.1 The per-diem cap in Section 2.3 of the MSA shall be increased from **USD 250** to **USD 350** for Orion consultants travelling to Client locations within Asia-Pacific (specifically: India, Singapore, Japan, Korea).

2.2 This adjustment is effective from the Effective Date of this Side Letter through December 31, 2026, after which it shall revert to USD 250 unless re-negotiated.

## 3. Volume Discount

3.1 If Orion's annual fees from Client exceed **USD 1,000,000** in calendar year 2025 or 2026, Orion shall apply a **7% volume discount** to subsequent invoices for the remainder of that calendar year.

## 4. No Other Changes

4.1 Except as expressly modified, all other terms of the MSA remain unchanged.
