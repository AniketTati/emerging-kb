# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into as of **15 May 2024** by and between **Acme Corp Pvt Ltd** ("Acme") and **Nimbus Technologies Pte Ltd** ("Nimbus").

## 1. Purpose

The Parties intend to evaluate a potential cloud-infrastructure relationship for Acme's production workloads, including disaster-recovery and multi-region failover.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means proprietary non-public information of either Party, including technical architecture, customer data, financial information, and roadmaps.

## 3. Use and Protection

3.1 The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, shall not disclose to third parties, and shall employ commercially reasonable safeguards.

3.2 Disclosure to need-to-know employees and advisors is permitted provided they are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.

## 4. Term

4.1 This Agreement is effective for three (3) years from the Effective Date. Confidentiality obligations survive an additional five (5) years.

## 5. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **Singapore** and disputes shall be referred to **SIAC arbitration** in Singapore.
