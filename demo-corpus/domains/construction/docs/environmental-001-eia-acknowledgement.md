# KSPCB CONSENT TO OPERATE — ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE

**Reference:** KSPCB/CO/EE/2025/418
**Date Issued:** 4 March 2025
**Issuing Authority:** Karnataka State Pollution Control Board
**Project:** Acme Whitefield Datacentre Phase-2 (Survey No. 184/2A, Whitefield Main Road, Bangalore)
**Applicant:** Acme Corp Pvt Ltd

---

## 1. Background

Acme Corp applied for Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB) consent to operate the new datacentre under the Air Act 1981 + Water Act 1974 + Hazardous Waste Rules 2016, in accordance with the Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Notification 2006 requirements.

Project category (per EIA Notification): **B2 (small datacentres + auxiliary infrastructure)** — no EIA required, but state pollution control board consent mandatory.

---

## 2. Conditions of Consent

KSPCB has granted Consent to Operate subject to the following conditions:

### A. Air Emissions (DG Sets)

(i) DG sets shall comply with **CPCB 2014 Stage III norms** for stationary combustion engines
(ii) Each DG enclosure shall have **acoustic insulation per CPCB norms** ≤ 75 dB(A) at 7 m boundary
(iii) Emission monitoring (online) shall be installed + reports submitted **monthly**
(iv) Annual stack monitoring by KSPCB-approved third-party agency
(v) Fuel used: Diesel HSD-IS:1460 grade only (no use of furnace oil or other prohibited fuels)
(vi) DG operating hours: Limited to backup; no commercial use of DG power

### B. Water + Wastewater

(i) Domestic + cooling-tower wastewater: connected to Bangalore Water Supply + Sewerage Board (BWSSB) sewer
(ii) Cooling-tower blow-down treated in on-site sewage-treatment plant (STP) capacity 12 KLD
(iii) Recycled water used for landscaping + sanitary flushing
(iv) No discharge to surface drains permitted

### C. Hazardous Waste

(i) Used DG fuel filters + lubricants: hazardous waste category — to be collected by KSPCB-approved hazardous waste handler quarterly
(ii) Used UPS batteries: lead-acid recovered through KSPCB-approved e-waste handler
(iii) Annual hazardous-waste log submitted to KSPCB
(iv) No on-site storage of hazardous waste > 30 days

### D. Solid Waste

(i) Daily solid waste segregation: dry waste, wet waste, e-waste (per BBMP norms)
(ii) Wet waste composted on-site or handed to BBMP
(iii) Dry waste recyclable items handed to BBMP-empanelled recyclers
(iv) E-waste handled per E-Waste (Management) Rules 2016

### E. Air-Quality + Noise Monitoring

(i) **Boundary air-quality monitoring** quarterly (PM2.5, PM10, NOₓ, SO₂, CO) — third-party agency
(ii) **Boundary noise monitoring** quarterly (day + night levels) — third-party agency
(iii) Boundary noise day: ≤ 55 dB(A); night: ≤ 45 dB(A) — datacentre boundary is residential zone
(iv) Reports submitted within 30 days of monitoring

### F. Tree Cover + Greenery

(i) Pre-construction tree audit: 14 trees within plot boundary; 8 retained, 6 transplanted to KSFC nursery
(ii) Post-construction: minimum 30% green cover (built into landscape design per architectural plan)
(iii) 22 new trees to be planted at handover (committed by Acme + verified by KSPCB at first annual inspection)

### G. Energy Efficiency

(i) Datacentre PUE design ≤ 1.4 (specified in tender)
(ii) IGBC LEED Platinum certification target (separately tracked; certification issued 16 Feb 2026)
(iii) Annual energy-efficiency audit + report

---

## 3. Validity

This consent is valid for **5 years** from the date of issue (4 March 2025 - 3 March 2030). Renewal application to be submitted ≥ 90 days before expiry.

---

## 4. Reporting Requirements

Acme shall submit to KSPCB:

| Report | Frequency | Submission Format |
|---|---|---|
| DG operating hours + emission monitoring data | Monthly | Online portal |
| Boundary air + noise monitoring (third party) | Quarterly | PDF email |
| Hazardous waste handover records | Quarterly | Online portal |
| Solid waste audit + green cover audit | Annual | Site visit |
| Energy + water consumption audit | Annual | PDF report |

---

## 5. Penalties + Enforcement

Per Air Act 1981 + Water Act 1974, non-compliance may result in:
- Cancellation of consent
- Penalty up to INR 25 lakh
- Director liability for serious offences
- Closure orders

Routine inspections by KSPCB regional officer: quarterly. Special inspections triggered by complaints, incidents, or self-reported issues.
