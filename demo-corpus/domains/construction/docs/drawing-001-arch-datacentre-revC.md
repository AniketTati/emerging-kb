## Changes from Rev B (Owner-requested + Compliance)

### Change 1: NOC + Security Station Relocated

The Network Operations Centre + main security station are repositioned to **face the main entry** (north-east corner). This improves visitor wayfinding + security operations.

**Impact:**
- NOC moves to 184 sq.m at north-east corner (was central position in Rev B)
- A small office (previously NOC location) is repurposed as a meeting room
- No structural change; only partition + finish adjustments

### Change 2: Loading Dock Extension (+1.8m)

The loading dock is extended by 1.8m to accommodate larger forklifts requested by Acme's procurement team (per email from Mr. Sanjay Tripathi, Acme Procurement, dated 18 Feb 2025).

**Impact:**
- Loading dock area increases from 168 sq.m to 195 sq.m
- Rear setback reduces from 6m to 4.2m (still within BBMP's 4m minimum)
- Storage area immediately adjacent reduces by 27 sq.m
- BBMP confirmed acceptable in revised submission

### Change 3: Generator Room Ventilation (Compliance)

Per KPCB's conditional NOC (issued 4 March 2025), the generator room (320 sq.m) requires:
- Larger intake ventilation grilles (was 8 × 1m² grilles; now 12 × 1m² grilles)
- One additional emergency exhaust fan (50,000 CMH)
- Acoustic enclosure for each DG set (was 4 DG sets covered; now confirmed all 4 with NRC-rated acoustic panels)

**Impact:**
- Minor wall opening additions
- Slight increase in mechanical contractor scope (~INR 18 lakh)

### Change 4: Finishes Update

Anti-static vinyl floor spec upgraded:
- Was: standard ESD-rated vinyl (Acme: "good enough")
- Now: high-performance ESD-rated vinyl (Acme: "preferred for Tier IV")
- Cost premium: INR 22 lakh
- Approved by Acme per change-order CO-005 dated 12 March 2025
