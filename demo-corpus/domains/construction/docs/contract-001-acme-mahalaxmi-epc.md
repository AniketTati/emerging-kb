## 1. Scope of Work

Mahalaxmi shall design (in coordination with Deshpande Architects + Engineers LLP and Sundar Structural Consultants Pvt Ltd, who are Owner's consultants), procure, construct, test, commission, and hand over the Datacentre building per the drawings + specifications attached as Schedule 1 (Architectural Rev C dated 22 March 2025 + Structural Rev 2 dated 8 February 2025 + MEP coordination drawings).

The scope explicitly includes:
- All civil works (foundation, superstructure, finishes per BoQ)
- All MEP works (electrical, HVAC, plumbing, fire protection, BMS)
- Specialty datacentre items (UPS, DG, raised flooring, containment)
- External site works (boundary, paving, landscaping, rainwater harvesting)
- All statutory consents in the Contractor's name (BBMP construction work permit, etc.)
- Commissioning + handover documentation

The scope **excludes**:
- IT equipment (servers + storage + network) — to be procured by Acme separately
- Land acquisition + site clearance — Acme has clean title
- Owner's own consultants' fees — Acme pays direct

## 4. Liquidated Damages

If Mechanical Completion is not achieved by 28 February 2026 (subject to extension for Force Majeure + Owner-caused delays):

- **0.25% of Contract Value per week of delay**, capped at **5% of Contract Value (~INR 2.20 crore)**.

If the cap is reached without completion, Acme has the option to terminate the contract and engage an alternative contractor.

## 6. Performance Security

Mahalaxmi shall provide a **Bank Guarantee for 10% of the Contract Value (INR 4,41,01,150)** valid through the Defects Liability Period. The BG shall be from a Tier-1 Indian bank (HDFC / ICICI / SBI / Axis acceptable).

In addition, retention of 5% of each payment shall be held by Acme until 12 months post-Mechanical Completion (DLP end). Retention is released 50% on MC and 50% at DLP end.

---

## 7. Variations + Change Orders

Variations are governed by Clause 13 of the Contract. Acme has the right to instruct variations up to **+/- 15% of Contract Value** without renegotiation. Variations beyond that range require mutual agreement.

Change orders (CO) are documented as numbered amendments to the BoQ:
- CO-001 through CO-005: Rev A → Rev C drawing changes — already incorporated in Schedule 1
- CO-006 onwards: subject to mutual sign-off

## 8. Risk + Indemnity

| Risk | Allocated to |
|---|---|
| Site conditions (geological) | Contractor (geotech report acknowledged) |
| Permits + statutory approvals | Contractor |

| Labour disputes + strikes (non-political) | Contractor |
| Force Majeure | Both (per FIDIC clause 19) |
| Latent defects (post-DLP) | Contractor (5 years) |
| Cyber + IT (Owner's equipment) | Owner |

## 9. Safety + Compliance

Mahalaxmi shall:
(i) Comply with all applicable laws including Karnataka Factories Act 1948, Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation) Act 1996, Child Labour Prohibition Act 1986, Karnataka Pollution Control Board norms
(ii) Maintain Workmen Compensation insurance + Public Liability insurance + All-Risks Construction Insurance (combined cover ≥ INR 50 crore)
(iii) Appoint a Senior Safety Officer (qualified per ER&D Council guidelines) on site full-time
(iv) Maintain daily safety briefings + monthly safety committee meetings
(v) Report all accidents within 24 hours; investigate within 7 days

## 10. Sub-contracting

Mahalaxmi may sub-contract specialty work to:

## 11. Dispute Resolution

(i) **Pre-arbitration:** Disputes shall first be referred to senior management of both parties for resolution within 30 days.
(ii) **Arbitration:** Disputes not resolved within 30 days shall be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration + Conciliation Act 1996. Seat: Bangalore. Language: English.
(iii) **Governing law:** Indian law. Jurisdiction (for incidental matters): Bangalore courts.
