## Key Changes from Rev A

### Change 1: NICU Expansion (12 → 14 cot positions)

Post Apollo's medical board review (16 February 2025), the NICU is expanded from 12 to **14 cot positions** to meet projected demand. NICU area increases from 168 sq.m to 196 sq.m.

Impact:
- Doctors' lounge reduced from 28 sq.m to 18 sq.m
- Linen + supplies storage reduced from 32 sq.m to 24 sq.m
- Slightly tighter overall footprint but within structural envelope

### Change 2: Lactation Room Enlargement

Lactation room enlarged from 12 to 18 sq.m to comfortably accommodate 6 mothers (was 4).

### Change 3: Pharmacy Counter

Per request from Apollo's pharmacy lead (Dr. Anjana Kulkarni), the pharmacy dispensing window is converted to a full pharmacy counter (28 sq.m) with seating + waiting area for 8-10 patients. This expansion uses space from the consultation room area (one room consolidated).

### Change 4: PMC Approval Conditions

PMC approval received 18 March 2025 with the following conditions, all incorporated in this revision:
- Additional fire-rated door at corridor junction to surgical block
- One emergency exit + staircase widened from 1.2m to 1.5m
- Disabled-access toilet added on Ground Floor (1 nos)
- Two additional parking spaces required (will be on existing campus surface car park; no drawing change)
