# FACTORY INSPECTOR REPORT — Q2 FY 2025-26 (POST-INCIDENT)

**Inspection Date:** 22 June 2025 (special follow-up post-incident dated 22 May 2025)
**Site:** Acme Whitefield Datacentre Phase-2
**Inspector:** Mr. Mohan Rao, Factory Inspector, Directorate of Factories + Boilers, Karnataka
**Inspection Ref:** DFBK-INSP-2025-06-184
**Statutory Reference:** Karnataka Factories Act 1948 + Building and Other Construction Workers Act 1996 + Karnataka Labour Welfare Fund Act 1965

---

## 1. Inspection Purpose

Follow-up inspection following the worker fall-from-height incident reported on 22 May 2025 (incident reference Acme-WCD-INCD-2025-05-001 / Mr. Dinesh Kumar). The Factory Inspector wished to verify implementation of corrective actions recommended in the detailed investigation report (safety-001-incident-fall-investigation dated 1 June 2025).

---

## 2. Inspection Method

Three-day inspection (22-24 June 2025) including:
- Site walk-down at all work areas (Day 1)
- PPE verification + worker interviews (random sample of 22 workers, Day 1-2)
- Document review (PPE inventory, JSA logs, training records, safety committee minutes) (Day 2-3)
- Worker interviews: 8 (with no Mahalaxmi representative present, to ensure candid feedback)
- Site office records audit (Day 3)

---

## 3. Detailed Findings

### Corrective Action Implementation Status

| Action | Status | Inspector verification |
|---|---|---|
| CA-001: PPE inventory replenishment | ✅ Implemented | Visual count: 84 harnesses in stock (vs 24 required for daily use = 350% safety margin); buffer is robust |
| CA-002: Supervisor PPE-verification | ✅ Implemented | Spot-checked 12 of 14 supervisors; 12 had JSA log signed for current shift |
| CA-003: Worker training | ✅ Implemented | Training records show 100% of 142 workers re-trained on PPE within 30 days of incident |
| CA-004: Disciplinary action | ✅ Implemented | Mr. Rajesh Pal on PIP; HR records confirm |
| CA-005: Digital PPE tracking | 🟡 In progress | RFID procurement contract signed; deployment target 31 Aug 2025 (still on track) |
| CA-006: Safety culture review | ✅ Implemented | Workshops conducted; survey completed; KPIs added to dashboards |

---

### Worker Interviews — Themes

(i) Workers feel that PPE availability has improved markedly + supervisors are now visibly checking before height work.
(ii) Some workers expressed appreciation for the post-incident ex-gratia decision (INR 12 lakh to Mr. Kumar) — the financial protection is reassuring.
(iii) Workers noted that the "right to refuse work without PPE" message has been clearly communicated.
(iv) No worker raised any new safety concerns.
(v) One worker (anonymous, voluntary) commented that previously there was implicit pressure to continue work even when PPE was short — this pressure is no longer felt.

---

## 4. Inspector's Commendation

The Factory Inspector wishes to formally commend Mahalaxmi Infrastructure for:

(i) **Fast + transparent investigation** of the 22 May 2025 incident
(ii) **Thorough root-cause analysis** + acceptance of system responsibility (rather than blaming the worker)
(iii) **Generous compensation** to the affected worker (INR 12 lakh ex-gratia + medical + family support)
(iv) **Effective corrective action implementation** within 4 weeks of investigation
(v) **Worker-friendly culture changes** evident in interviews

This case is on the Inspector's record as a **"best practice incident management"** example.

---

## 5. Outstanding Recommendations

(i) **CA-005 (Digital PPE tracking):** Complete by 31 August 2025 as planned.
(ii) **Worker representation:** Consider formalising worker representation on the Safety Committee (currently advisory only).
(iii) **Annual safety culture review:** Plan for a similar workshop in Q2 FY 2026-27 to maintain momentum.
(iv) **Lessons-sharing:** Share the anonymised case study with Karnataka Construction Industry Safety Council (KCISC) — already initiated; commend.

---

## 6. Formal Statement

The site is **compliant with Karnataka Factories Act, BOCW Act, + other applicable labour + safety regulations**. The 22 May 2025 incident has been properly investigated + corrective actions implemented to the satisfaction of the Inspector. No further enforcement action is required at this time.

The next scheduled inspection is the routine annual inspection (target: October 2025).
