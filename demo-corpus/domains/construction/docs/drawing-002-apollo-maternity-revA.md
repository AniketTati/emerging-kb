## Project Summary

Expansion of the existing Apollo Hospitals Pune maternity ward by adding **18 new private rooms** + **2 birthing suites** + **expanded NICU (Neonatal ICU) with 12 cot positions** + ancillary spaces (consultation, nurses' station, mothers' lounge, family waiting area).

- **Plot:** Existing Apollo Hospitals campus, Pune Camp branch
- **Built-up area added:** 1,884 sq.m (G + 1 floor extension to existing maternity block)
- **Total estimated cost:** INR 38 crore (per Apollo's capex memo dated 4 Feb 2025)
- **Construction duration:** 12 months (target completion 28 Feb 2026)

## Special Requirements (Medical)

Per Apollo Hospitals' medical brief:

(i) **Infection control:** All air-handling units have HEPA filtration; positive-pressure rooms for NICU; separate flow for clean vs soiled materials
(ii) **Noise + privacy:** Acoustic separation between rooms (STC ≥ 50)
(iii) **Patient comfort:** Natural daylighting where possible; views to landscaped courtyards
(iv) **Family-friendly:** Sleeper sofa in every private room; visiting hours design accommodates 2 visitors per mother simultaneously
(v) **Code compliance:** Maharashtra Nursing Home Registration Act + Maharashtra Medical Council guidelines + NABH accreditation criteria
(vi) **Equipment integration:** Coordination with Siemens Healthineers (Apollo's medical-equipment partner) on:
   - Foetal monitors (one per private room)
   - Phototherapy units (NICU)
   - Ventilators (NICU + birthing suites)
   - Patient monitoring central console (nurses' station)

## Structural + MEP Notes

- **Structural:** New construction sits on existing foundation pad (already designed for 4-floor capacity in original 2018 construction). No new foundation work needed.
- **MEP:** Tap into existing chilled water + electrical mains; estimated additional 480 kVA load
- **Vertical transportation:** Existing 2 lifts adequate; one additional dumbwaiter for sterile supplies
- **Fire safety:** Sprinkler system extended; new fire-rated compartmentation between maternity wing and adjacent surgical block
