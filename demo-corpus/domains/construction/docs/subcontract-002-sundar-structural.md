# CONSULTANCY AGREEMENT — STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING SERVICES

**Reference:** Acme-SSC-CONS-2025-01-S
**Date:** 8 January 2025
**Effective from:** 12 January 2025

## Parties

**Client:** Acme Corp Pvt Ltd
**Consultant:** Sundar Structural Consultants Pvt Ltd (Engineer of Record)

## Scope

Sundar Structural Consultants Pvt Ltd shall provide:

(i) **Concept structural design** — feasibility studies, alternative-load-path analyses
(ii) **Detailed structural design** — calculations + drawings (Structural Drawing Set S-001 through S-008)
(iii) **Coordination with Architect** — review architectural drawings; raise structural concerns; issue load + clearance calculations
(iv) **Site visits + construction supervision** — minimum 12 site visits over project duration (1 per month + special events)
(v) **Quality assurance** — review concrete + steel test reports; certify completed structural elements
(vi) **Defect investigations** — respond to RFIs from contractor + Acme
(vii) **As-built drawing review + sign-off**
