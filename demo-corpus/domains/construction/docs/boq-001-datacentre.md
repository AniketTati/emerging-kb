(iii) BoQ excludes statutory fees (BBMP plan + KPCB + Factory NOC) — Acme to pay direct
(iv) BoQ excludes financing costs + insurance + contingencies above 5%
(v) Rates are based on Bangalore market rates as of February 2025; subject to material price escalation clause per contract clause 14
