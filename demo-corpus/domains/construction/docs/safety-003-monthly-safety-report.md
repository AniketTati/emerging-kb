## ⚠ INCIDENT REPORT — MAY 2025

**One Lost-Time Injury this month** — Mr. Dinesh Kumar fall-from-height on 22 May 2025. Reference: safety-001 chain (initial → investigation → corrective).

| Severity | Count |
|---|---|
| Lost-time injuries (LTI) | **1** (Mr. Kumar — wrist fracture + concussion) |
| Reportable injuries (non-LTI) | 0 |
| First-aid only | 4 |
| Near-misses | 18 |
| Fatalities | 0 |

**LTIFR (Lost-Time Injury Frequency Rate): ~28.8 per million man-hours** (calculated annualised). This is well above industry benchmark of <2.0 and a significant deterioration from April.

**Investigation:** Detailed investigation completed 1 June 2025; corrective actions completed by 18 June 2025. Root cause: PPE non-availability + supervision gap. See safety-001-incident-fall-investigation + corrective.

## Immediate Actions Taken Post-Incident

(i) Site East elevation (Grid 4-8) work halted 22-26 May for safety review
(ii) Full PPE audit conducted 23-24 May
(iii) Harness inventory replenished 26 May (60 new units)
(iv) All 142 workers re-briefed on PPE 23 May
(v) Acme Corp client informed via formal communication on 22 May evening
