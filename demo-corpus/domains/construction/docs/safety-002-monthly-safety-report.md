## Summary

April was a **safe month with zero lost-time injuries** + 14 near-misses captured + actioned. PPE compliance is generally good but improvement needed in eye + hand protection. Recommendation: continued vigilance on height work — site is entering structural phase from May 2025 which involves significant height work.
