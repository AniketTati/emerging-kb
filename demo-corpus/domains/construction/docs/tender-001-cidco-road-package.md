# CIDCO TENDER NOTICE — NAVI MUMBAI INFRASTRUCTURE PACKAGE

**Tender No.:** CIDCO/INFRA/2025/T-018
**Date of Notice:** 12 February 2025
**Issuing Authority:** City and Industrial Development Corporation of Maharashtra Ltd (CIDCO)
**Tender Type:** Open Tender (Two-stage: Technical + Financial bids)
**Mode:** e-Tendering via Maharashtra Government Procurement Portal

---

## 1. Tender Subject

Construction of internal access roads + storm-water drains + utility ducts at the CIDCO Navi Mumbai New Town Sector 47-49 (also known as Pushpak Greenfield Township).

**Scope summary:**
- Construction of 14.2 km of internal roads (RCC + bituminous pavement)
- 18.4 km of storm-water drains (precast RCC sections)
- 12.4 km of utility ducts (HDPE)
- Street lighting + signage
- Landscaping at intersections

**Estimated tender value:** INR 184 crore (excluding GST)
**Construction duration:** 24 months

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

### Financial Criteria

- Average Annual Turnover ≥ INR 150 crore (last 3 financial years)
- Net Worth ≥ INR 50 crore at last balance sheet date
- Solvency Certificate from a scheduled commercial bank ≥ INR 75 crore

### Technical Criteria

- Completed at least 2 similar projects in last 7 years, each:
  - Value ≥ INR 100 crore
  - Including at least 8 km of road + 12 km of drain construction
- Owned key equipment: paver, asphalt batching plant, vibratory roller, motor grader, RCC pipe layer
- Quality + Safety certifications: ISO 9001 + ISO 45001 (or equivalent)

### Procedural Criteria

- Mandatory Earnest Money Deposit (EMD): INR 1.84 crore (in form of FD or BG)
- Tender processing fee: INR 25,000 (non-refundable)
- e-Tender portal registration up-to-date
- PAN + GST registration + Maharashtra state work-completion certificate (if any)

Tender documents are downloadable from the Maharashtra Government Procurement Portal at maharashtra.gov.in/etender. The documents include:

(ii) Volume II: Special Conditions of Contract (SCC) — site-specific

(vii) Annexures: Approved drawings + clearances

---

## 5. Bid Evaluation Methodology

Evaluation is by the L1 method (lowest financial bid among technically qualified bidders), subject to:

(i) Technical evaluation: pass/fail (60% weight not used; instead pass criteria)
(ii) Financial bid: rank-ordered by total bid value
(iii) Award to lowest financial bidder, provided:
    - No abnormally low bid (more than 15% below CIDCO's confidential estimate)
    - All technical pass criteria met
    - All financial + legal documents are in order

If the lowest bid is abnormally low (>15% below estimate), CIDCO may seek bidders' justification or reject the bid.

---

## 6. Important Notes

(i) **Land acquisition + clearances:** All land for the project has been acquired by CIDCO; environmental clearance + tree-cutting NOC obtained
(ii) **Construction site access:** Available from 1 June 2025 onwards
(iii) **Force majeure:** Standard clauses including monsoon (Jun-Sep), labour strikes
(iv) **Mobilisation advance:** Up to 10% of contract value against BG, refunded against progress
(v) **Performance security:** 5% of contract value as BG for the duration of the contract + DLP
(vi) **Defect Liability Period (DLP):** 24 months from completion certificate
(vii) **Liquidated damages:** 0.5% per week of delay, capped at 10% of contract value
(viii) **Variations:** Up to ±15% of contract value at unit rates
