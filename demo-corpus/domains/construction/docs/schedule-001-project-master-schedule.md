## Critical Path Items

Critical activities (zero float) that drive overall delivery:

(i) Foundation excavation + casting (Apr - May 2025)
(ii) Plinth + ground floor structure (Jun - Jul 2025)
(iii) First + roof slab structure (Aug 2025)
(iv) MEP rough-in (Aug - Oct 2025)
(v) BMS + automation integration (Nov 2025 - Jan 2026)
(vi) Pre-commissioning + testing (Dec 2025 - Feb 2026)
(vii) Commissioning + handover (Feb 2026)

A delay in any of these activities directly delays the project completion. The structural sequence (#i to #iii) is on track. MEP rough-in is the next critical phase.

## Schedule Updates (Recent)

**5-day risk flag (October 2025):** MEP rough-in forecast 5 Nov 2025 vs target 30 Oct 2025. This is a 5-working-day variance which is within the 8% float carried in the schedule. Recovery plan: increase electrical crew + extend shifts during week 1-2 November. PM forecasts completion by 7 November (still within float).

**No baseline change yet.** If recovery does not materialize, a formal schedule change request will be issued.
