## 2. Incident Description (Preliminary)

At approximately 11:42 IST on 22 May 2025, Mr. Dinesh Kumar fell from a height of approximately **2.4 metres** while working on the brick masonry of the first-floor external wall of the datacentre building (East elevation, Grid line 6).

He was working from a tubular scaffold platform when, per witnesses' accounts (Mr. Mahesh Yadav + Mr. Rajeev Pal, both fellow masons), he lost balance while reaching for a fresh batch of mortar. He fell backwards, landing on the ground (which was concrete-paved, freshly poured 14 days prior).

He was found semi-conscious at approximately 11:43 IST. The site First Aid team (Mr. Bharat Kale, certified first aider) attended within 2 minutes.

## 6. Initial Root-Cause Hypothesis (PRELIMINARY)

**Initial assessment (within 24 hours):** The incident appears to be due to **worker error — failure to clip safety harness to the lanyard anchor point**. Mr. Kumar's harness was found on the ground unhooked, suggesting he had not properly attached it before commencing the high-altitude work.

**Note (to be reviewed):** Mr. Bhargava notes that this initial assessment is preliminary; a fuller investigation will be conducted by the safety committee + an independent investigator. The initial assessment is being treated as the "first hypothesis" only, and may be revised based on further evidence.

(ii) Toolbox briefing conducted with all 142 workers on site at 14:42 IST emphasising harness use
(iii) Inspector Mohan Rao (Factories Karnataka) notified immediately + on-site visit scheduled for 23 May 2025 morning
(iv) Family of Mr. Kumar contacted; spouse + son informed; flying down from UP via Mahalaxmi-sponsored ticket for 24 May arrival
