## 1. Scope of MEP Sub-contract

Phoenix shall provide design coordination, supply, installation, testing, and commissioning of all Mechanical-Electrical-Plumbing (MEP) systems for the Acme Whitefield Datacentre Phase-2, including:

(i) Electrical: HT switchgear (LV side), LT distribution panels, cabling, lighting, low-voltage power
(ii) HVAC: Chilled-water plant (chillers + cooling towers), CRAC units, ducting, VRF (offices)
(iii) Plumbing: Domestic water, drainage, DG fuel piping
(iv) Fire protection: Sprinkler + clean-agent suppression + VESDA detection
(v) BMS: Building Management System (Honeywell Niagara N4)
(vi) Earthing + lightning protection
(vii) Commissioning + 24×7 24-month operations support post-handover

The MEP scope does NOT include:
- UPS systems (Acme procures directly; Phoenix integrates)
- DG sets (Acme procures directly; Phoenix integrates)
- IT racks + cabling

## 5. Payment Terms

Phoenix is paid by Mahalaxmi against monthly running bills. Each bill is certified by the MEP Engineer (Phoenix in-house) + endorsed by Mahalaxmi's QS team + countersigned by Acme's MEP consultant.

Mahalaxmi shall pass through payments to Phoenix within **7 days of receipt from Acme**, less:
- 7.5% retention (released 50% at MC + 50% at DLP end)
- Any agreed deductions for non-conformance or delay

---

## 6. Insurance + Compliance

Phoenix shall maintain:
- Workmen Compensation insurance per Karnataka Workmen Compensation rules
- Public Liability insurance ≥ INR 10 crore per occurrence
- All-risks insurance covering Phoenix-installed equipment up to INR 25 crore
- Compliance with Karnataka Factories Act + Karnataka Pollution Control Board norms

---

## 7. Performance Security

Phoenix shall provide a BG for 5% of sub-contract value (INR 85,73,880) valid through DLP.

---

## 8. Back-to-back Pass-through

All Acme-imposed conditions (drawings, specifications, schedule, quality, safety) on Mahalaxmi flow through to Phoenix on a back-to-back basis.

---

## 9. Dispute Resolution

Disputes between Mahalaxmi + Phoenix → resolved between parties first; if escalated, arbitration in Bangalore.

Disputes that involve Acme as a third-party (e.g., variation orders driven by Acme) → resolved through Mahalaxmi as principal, with Phoenix as Mahalaxmi's downstream party.
