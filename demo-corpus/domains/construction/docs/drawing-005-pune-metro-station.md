## Project Summary

Elevated metro station design for Mira Road station (MM-08) on Metro Line-9. Station is part of the package awarded by MMRDA to a JV of L&T + Deshpande Architects (consortium ref MMRDA-LINE9-PKG-3 dated 12 January 2025).

- **Station type:** Elevated (15.4m above ground)
- **Platform configuration:** Side platforms (2 nos)
- **Built-up area:** 12,400 sq.m (station + ancillaries)
- **Passenger throughput design:** 24,000 PPHPD (peak hour peak direction)
- **Number of escalators:** 8
- **Number of lifts:** 6 (3 for passengers, 1 for staff, 2 for goods)
- **Construction duration:** 18 months (target ROM completion Q4 FY 2026-27)
- **Estimated cost (this station):** INR 184 crore

## Special Requirements

(i) **PwD accessibility:** Full compliance with Harmonised Guidelines + Standards for Universal Accessibility (2021). Tactile paving, audible signage, accessible toilets, lifts dedicated to PwD use.

(ii) **Surveillance + safety:** 184 CCTV cameras across the station, with central monitoring at MMRDA Operations Control Centre.

(iii) **Fire + emergency:** Compartmentation per NBC 2016 high-occupancy provisions. Emergency exits, smoke management, evacuation modeling completed (third-party verification).

(iv) **Noise + vibration:** Station design includes acoustic barriers to limit noise to ≤ 70 dB(A) at the residential boundary 18m away.

(v) **Climate:** Natural cross-ventilation prioritised; air-conditioning only for operations rooms + retail.

(vi) **Heritage + context:** Architectural design language consistent with the rest of Line-9 (jali-screen façade, terracotta-tone exterior cladding).
