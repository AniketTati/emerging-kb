# SAFETY INCIDENT — CORRECTIVE ACTION REPORT

**Incident reference:** Acme-WCD-INCD-2025-05-001 (Mr. Dinesh Kumar fall from height, 22 May 2025)
**Parent reports:** safety-001-incident-fall-initial (initial); safety-001-incident-fall-investigation (detailed)
**Report date:** 18 June 2025
**Prepared by:** Mr. Anand Krishnamurthy (Site Engineer Civil — Mahalaxmi)
**Reviewed by:** Mr. Vinay Pandey (Managing Director — Mahalaxmi)
**Approved by:** Mr. Mohan Rao (Factory Inspector, DFB Karnataka)

---

## 1. Summary

Following the detailed investigation report dated 1 June 2025, Mahalaxmi Infra has implemented the recommended corrective actions. This report documents the actions completed + the resulting status.

**Final root-cause classification (confirmed):** **PPE non-availability + supervision gap (system failure)** — NOT worker error. The initial worker-error hypothesis is conclusively rejected based on the detailed investigation.

---

## 2. Corrective Actions Completed

### CA-001: PPE Inventory Replenishment (Immediate)

| Action | Status |
|---|---|
| Replenished harness inventory: 60 new harnesses ordered | ✅ Complete (delivered 26 May 2025) |
| Buffer stock policy implemented: maintain 50% above expected daily usage | ✅ Effective 1 June 2025 |
| Daily PPE inventory check by Safety Officer | ✅ Effective 1 June 2025 (logged daily) |
| Automatic re-order trigger (procurement system): when inventory < 70% of daily usage | ✅ Implemented 12 June 2025 |

### CA-002: Supervisor PPE Verification (Process Change)

| Action | Status |
|---|---|
| Mandatory PPE-check station at scaffold base; supervisor signs JSA log | ✅ Effective 1 June 2025 |
| JSA template updated to include explicit PPE checklist + supervisor signature | ✅ Issued 2 June 2025 |
| Spot-check by Safety Officer at start of every shift | ✅ Effective 1 June 2025 |

### CA-003: Training + Briefings (Behaviour Change)

| Action | Status |
|---|---|
| All site supervisors retrained on PPE verification + escalation | ✅ Completed 8-12 June 2025 (14 supervisors trained) |
| All 142 site workers retrained on right to refuse work without PPE | ✅ Completed 14 June 2025 |
| New monthly safety committee meetings (incl. worker representatives) | ✅ Effective June 2025 |

### CA-004: Disciplinary Action (Accountability)

| Action | Status |
|---|---|
| Site Foreman (Mr. Rajesh Pal) — written warning issued | ✅ 8 June 2025; placed on PIP for 6 months |
| Safety Officer (Mr. Pradeep Bhargava) — Performance Improvement Plan | ✅ 8 June 2025; PIP for 6 months |
| Procurement HQ — process review + escalation pathway formalised | ✅ Completed 14 June 2025 |

### CA-005: Digital PPE Tracking (Technology)

| Action | Status |
|---|---|
| RFID tagging of all harnesses + critical PPE | ✅ Procurement contract awarded; deployment target 31 August 2025 |
| Digital JSA app for supervisors (Mahalaxmi-developed) | ✅ MVP deployed 15 June 2025; rolling out across sites |

### CA-006: Safety Culture Review (Systemic)

| Action | Status |
|---|---|
| Dr. Suresh Krishnan-led workshops (3 nos) | ✅ Conducted 8, 14, 18 June 2025 (84 participants total) |
| Safety culture survey administered | ✅ 92 responses collected (65% response rate); results being analysed |
| Safety performance KPI added to Mahalaxmi site-level dashboards | ✅ Effective 18 June 2025 |
| Safety bonus / penalty (5%) added to senior supervisor compensation | ✅ Approved by MD; effective Q3 FY 2025-26 |

## 5. Long-term Lessons + Industry Impact

(i) Mahalaxmi has shared anonymised key learnings with the Karnataka Construction Industry Safety Council (KCISC) for industry-wide awareness.

(ii) Acme Corp (Owner) has used this incident as a case study for their internal vendor-management framework; future contracts will include explicit PPE-provisioning + supervision audit requirements.

(iii) The digital PPE-tracking initiative is being scaled across all 8 Mahalaxmi project sites nationally (not just this site).

(iv) The Karnataka Factory Inspector has commended the **fast + transparent investigation + corrective action**. This is on record as a "best practice" incident-management example.
