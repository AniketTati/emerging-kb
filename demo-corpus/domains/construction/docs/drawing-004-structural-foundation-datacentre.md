## Soil Investigation Summary

Per Geotech Report dated 8 December 2024 (Geotech Sub-Surface Investigations Pvt Ltd):

| Layer | Depth (m) | Type | SBC (kN/m²) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 - 1.2 | Top soil + clayey silt | 80-120 | Remove + replace |
| 1.2 - 3.8 | Medium-dense weathered gneiss | 250-340 | Workable for shallow foundation |
| 3.8 - 8.0 | Hard weathered gneiss | 480-680 | Excellent founding stratum |
| 8.0+ | Sound rock | > 1,000 | Inaccessible at this depth |

**Adopted foundation:** Shallow isolated footings on hard weathered gneiss at depth 3.8 m. SBC adopted: 480 kN/m² (with FoS = 3 on UBC of 1,440 kN/m²). No piling required.

## Foundation Plan

The building is supported on **isolated RCC isolated footings** at column locations + **strip footings** under the internal load-bearing wall (Grid line D).

### Column Locations + Loads

Total of 36 columns in a 12 × 3 grid. Column reactions (factored, ULS):

| Column type | Count | Max ULS load (kN) | Footing size (m × m × m) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corner | 4 | 1,840 | 2.4 × 2.4 × 0.6 |
| Edge | 16 | 2,640 | 3.0 × 3.0 × 0.6 |
| Internal (typical) | 12 | 3,840 | 3.6 × 3.6 × 0.75 |
| Internal (heavy — under UPS + chiller pad) | 4 | 5,840 | 4.2 × 4.2 × 0.9 |

### Load-Bearing Wall Strip Footing

The internal load-bearing wall (Grid line D, 8.1m from East face) requires a strip footing:
- Width: 1.5 m
- Depth: 0.6 m
- Length: 36.8 m (full building width)
- Reinforcement: 12-mm dia bars at 200 mm c/c top + 8-mm dia distribution bars at 250 mm c/c bottom

### Material Specifications

| Element | Material | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Concrete | RCC | M30 (slab + walls), M35 (columns + foundations) |
| Reinforcement | TMT bars | Fe500D (per IS 1786:2008) |
| Cover | Concrete | 50 mm (foundation), 40 mm (columns), 25 mm (slabs) |
| Curing | Water + jute | 28 days minimum |

## Construction Notes

(i) **Excavation:** Excavate to 3.8m below FFL to reach weathered gneiss. Test pit verification required at every column location prior to footing casting.
(ii) **Dewatering:** Site is generally dry; standby sump pump for monsoon (June - September) excavation.
(iii) **Foundation casting sequence:** Cast footings first (target: 8 April - 28 May 2025); then plinth beam (target: 1 - 28 June 2025); then column lifts (target: 1 July - 30 August 2025).
(iv) **Quality control:** Cube tests every 50 m³ concrete; rebar testing per BIS norms. M30 mix design by IIT Bombay-approved batching plant.
(v) **Site safety:** Excavation > 1.5m deep requires shoring; per Factory Act + Karnataka safety protocols.
