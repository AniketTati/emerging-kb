# DM BEED — FOREST RIGHTS ACT CLAIM DISPOSAL SCHEDULE

**Order No.:** DM-BEED/2025/FRA/0042
**Date:** 25 July 2025

## Background

The Forest Rights Act (FRA) 2006 grants legal recognition to forest-dwelling Scheduled Tribes + traditional forest dwellers (OTFDs) for individual + community forest rights. In Beed district, 388 FRA claims are currently pending — some for over 24 months.

This order establishes a structured schedule for disposal of all pending claims.

## Order

### A. Categorization of Pending Claims (388 total)

| Category | Claims | % of total |
|---|---|---|
| Individual forest rights (IFR) | 312 | 80.4% |
| Community forest rights (CFR) | 64 | 16.5% |
| Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (OTFD) | 12 | 3.1% |
| **Total** | **388** | **100%** |

### B. Disposal Schedule

| Phase | Period | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | 1 August 2025 - 30 September 2025 | Disposal of 120 claims (low-complexity IFR cases) |
| Phase 2 | 1 October 2025 - 30 November 2025 | Disposal of 140 claims (medium-complexity) |
| Phase 3 | 1 December 2025 - 31 January 2026 | Disposal of 100 claims (complex IFR + simple CFR) |
| Phase 4 | 1 February 2026 - 31 March 2026 | Disposal of remaining 28 claims (complex CFR) |

### C. Process Requirements

(i) **Field verification by tehsildar + forest officer:** Mandatory site visit within 30 days of disposal hearing.

(ii) **Community consent:** For CFR claims, gram sabha resolution required.

(iii) **Documentation requirements:** Established possession evidence (≥3 generations for OTFD), occupational documents, etc.

(iv) **DLR Committee meeting:** Bi-weekly on Mondays + Fridays.

### D. Special Provisions

(i) Claims of widows, elderly, persons with disability: **fast-tracked** within 30 days of receipt.

(ii) Claims with documentation deficiencies: 30-day cure period; failure → rejection.

(iii) Disputed claims (multiple claimants): referred to State-level FRA committee for resolution.

### E. Reporting

District Tribal Welfare Officer to submit weekly status to DM.
