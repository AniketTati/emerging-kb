# CIDCO — TENDER BID DECISION (POST-RTI DISQUALIFICATION)

**Tender Reference:** CIDCO/T-2025/NV-RR-014 (original RFP dated 20 March 2025)
**Decision Date:** 12 June 2025
**Award to:** **Sterling Infra Pvt Ltd** (L2 bidder, after L1 disqualification)
**Awarded Value:** **INR 5.14 crore** (Sterling Infra Pvt Ltd's revised bid)

---

## 1. Original Bid Opening (29 April 2025)

The tender for road resurfacing of Navi Mumbai Sector 14 received 11 bids; after technical evaluation, 7 bidders were declared eligible. Financial bids:

| Rank | Bidder | Bid (INR) |
|---|---|---|
| L1 (lowest) | ABC Construction Pvt Ltd | 4.28 crore |
| L2 | Sterling Infra Pvt Ltd | 5.14 crore |
| L3 | Patel Roads Maharashtra | 5.28 crore |
| L4 | Konkan Infra Pvt Ltd | 5.42 crore |
| L5 | Maha Roads Pvt Ltd | 5.58 crore |
| L6 | Pune Civic Works | 5.84 crore |
| L7 | Bharat Highways | 6.12 crore |

Initial intent was to award to **L1 (ABC Construction Pvt Ltd) at INR 4.28 crore**.

## 2. RTI Complaint + Investigation

Following the announcement of the L1 award (3 May 2025), **Shri Ramesh Solanki** filed an RTI application (RTI/CIDCO/2025/0488, filed 5 May 2025) raising the following concerns:

- ABC Construction Pvt Ltd was previously blacklisted by the PWD Maharashtra in 2022 for sub-standard work on a different project (PWD blacklist ref: BL-2022-148). The blacklist was lifted in March 2024.
- However, ABC Construction's technical eligibility submission in this tender claimed "no blacklist in the past 5 years", which is **factually incorrect**.
- Multiple ongoing complaints about ABC Construction's work quality from other clients.

CIDCO's vigilance department investigated the RTI complaint (10 May - 5 June 2025). Findings:

(i) **ABC Construction did have a 2022 PWD blacklist** that is within the 5-year exclusion period (per the tender's eligibility criteria).

(ii) The "no blacklist" declaration in the bid was a **misrepresentation** by ABC Construction.

(iii) Per the tender's clause on misrepresentation, ABC Construction's bid is **invalid ab initio**.

## 3. Disqualification + Re-Award

The CIDCO Tender Evaluation Committee, in its meeting of 8 June 2025, has decided:

(i) **Disqualification of ABC Construction Pvt Ltd** from this tender. The L1 bid (INR 4.28 crore) is rejected.

(ii) The work is to be re-awarded to the next eligible bidder — **L2: Sterling Infra Pvt Ltd at INR 5.14 crore**.

(iii) The difference of INR 0.86 crore between the disqualified L1 and the awarded L2 represents the public cost of the misrepresentation. **CIDCO is initiating action against ABC Construction Pvt Ltd:**
   - **Fresh blacklisting**: 5 years from date of this decision (12 June 2025).
   - **Forfeiture of EMD** (INR 5.20 lakh).
   - **Reference to CBI/anti-corruption bureau** for potential investigation.

(iv) Sterling Infra Pvt Ltd has confirmed acceptance of the award and will commence work as per the original tender timeline (90 days from work commencement order).

(v) Shri Ramesh Solanki's RTI complaint is acknowledged + commendation will be entered into his RTI file (for transparency assistance).

## 4. Operative Award

**The operative award for tender CIDCO/T-2025/NV-RR-014 is:**

- **Contractor:** Sterling Infra Pvt Ltd
- **Awarded value:** **INR 5.14 crore** (exclusive of GST)
- **Work commencement order:** 20 June 2025
- **Completion target:** 18 September 2025 (90 days)
