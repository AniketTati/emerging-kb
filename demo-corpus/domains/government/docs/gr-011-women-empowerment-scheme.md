# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA — GAD
### GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION

**GR No.:** GAD-WOM-2025/CR-1024/WOM-4
**Date:** 18 May 2025

**Subject:** Maharashtra Women Empowerment Scheme — revised eligibility + benefits.

## Resolution

(i) Approval of the revised Maharashtra Women Empowerment Scheme effective 1 June 2025.

(ii) Scheme components:

### A. Cash Transfer Component (Aaditi Yojana)
- Monthly cash transfer: **INR 1,500 per month**.
- Eligibility: Women aged 21-65, annual household income ≤ INR 2.5 lakh, single or married.
- Verification: Aadhaar + bank account in beneficiary's name.

### B. Skill + Entrepreneurship Support
- Skill training (per Skill Development Mission GR) — fully sponsored.
- Micro-enterprise loan: up to INR 5 lakh with subsidized interest (4% effective rate).

### C. Women's Health
- Free annual health check-up at empanelled hospital.
- Reproductive health services covered under state insurance.

### D. Education for Girl Children
- Scholarship for school-going girls: INR 12,000/year (Class 9-12).
- Scholarship for college-going girls: INR 24,000/year (UG/PG).

(iii) Total allocation FY 2025-26: **INR 16,420 crore** (state's largest single welfare scheme).

(iv) Eligible beneficiaries: estimated 1.18 crore households (verified via SECC + Aadhaar cross-reference).

(v) Implementation: District Women & Child Development Officers report to State WCD Commissioner.

(vi) DBT mandatory — Aadhaar + bank-account verification at registration. For women without independent bank accounts, joint accounts with husband **not** acceptable; women must open individual accounts (Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana available).

(vii) Grievance mechanism: dedicated toll-free helpline + monthly Tehsildar grievance days.
