# DM BEED — FAIR-PRICE SHOP LICENSE RENEWAL ORDER

**Order No.:** DM-BEED/2025/PDS-FPS/0028
**Date:** 8 June 2025

## Background

The Fair-Price Shop (FPS) network in Beed district comprises 488 licensed shops serving approximately 2.84 lakh ration-card-holding households. Per the Public Distribution System (PDS) Reforms scheme, FPS licenses come up for renewal every 3 years.

In FY 2025-26, **132 FPS licenses** in Beed district come due for renewal. The DM hereby orders the renewal procedure + criteria.

## Order

### A. Eligibility for Renewal

(i) Compliance with audit findings of the past 12 months (no irregularities flagged).
(ii) FPS turnover ≥ minimum threshold (varies by category — INR 24 lakh/year for urban, INR 14 lakh for rural).
(iii) Aadhaar-linkage status: Minimum 95% of cardholders linked.
(iv) ePOS device compliance: Active ePOS machine + grain stock reconciliation.
(v) No pending complaints (resolved or under investigation).

### B. Renewal Procedure

(i) Application via Maha-Ration portal between 15 June and 31 July 2025.
(ii) Site verification by the SDM (within 30 days of application).
(iii) Tehsildar recommendation.
(iv) DM final approval.
(v) Renewal certificate issued, valid 3 years.

### C. Special Provisions

(i) **Tribal-area FPS:** Relaxed turnover criteria (75% of standard) per the GR-005-tribal-welfare-fund.

(ii) **Women-owned FPS:** Priority + 5% extra commission on subsidized commodities (per State Women Empowerment GR-011).

(iii) **Stand-alone FPS in fluctuating-demand villages:** Special consideration if turnover falls below threshold; renewal subject to community recommendation.

### D. Non-Renewal Cases

Of the 132 FPS coming due:
- Estimated 110 likely to renew without issue.
- Estimated 18 to be flagged for additional review.
- Estimated 4 likely to be denied renewal (typically due to repeat irregularities).

For non-renewal cases, the cardholders served by those shops will be re-mapped to neighbouring FPS within 30 days.

## Enforcement

District Supply Officer (DSO), Beed, to coordinate. Monthly status reports to DM.
