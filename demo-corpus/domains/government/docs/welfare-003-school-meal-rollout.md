# MID-DAY MEAL SCHEME — BEED DISTRICT Q3 FY 2025-26 ROLLOUT

**Office of DM Beed + District Education Officer**
**Reporting period:** 1 July 2025 - 30 September 2025
**Report date:** 15 October 2025

## Background

Following the revised Mid-Day Meal Scheme rates effective 1 July 2025 (per gr-009-school-meal-scheme), this report documents the Q3 FY 2025-26 rollout in Beed district.

## Coverage

| Tehsil | Schools | Enrolled students | Beneficiaries (Q3) | Coverage % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beed | 286 | 64,200 | 60,840 | 94.8% |

| Patoda | 88 | 22,600 | 21,448 | 94.9% |
| Kaij | 124 | 28,400 | 27,032 | 95.2% |
| Manjlegaon | 184 | 36,200 | 34,640 | 95.7% |
| Wadwani | 78 | 18,800 | 17,932 | 95.4% |

## Nutritional Compliance

- Calorie delivery (Class 1-5): 462 ± 18 cal/meal — within 450-500 target.
- Calorie delivery (Class 6-8): 712 ± 28 cal/meal — within 700-740 target.
- Protein (Class 1-5): 13.2 g — meets 12 g target.
- Protein (Class 6-8): 22.4 g — exceeds 20 g target.

## Quality Audit

- 38 random samples tested at State PHL (Public Health Laboratory).
- All samples passed for microbial + chemical safety.

## Anomalies / Improvements Identified

(i) **2 schools** in Patoda tehsil had supply disruption for 4 days (15-18 Aug) during monsoon flooding. Make-up rations provided.

(ii) **8 schools** had below-95% coverage during Q3 — investigation found these are schools with low enrolment + boarding-meal arrangement (children dispersed to homes).

(iii) **Egg + fruit supply variability** — early-Q3 supplier issues at 4 schools resolved by sub-tenant arrangement.

## Status

**Q3 FY 2025-26 — successfully completed. Coverage 95.2% (well above the 90% benchmark).**
