# OLD AGE PENSION DISBURSEMENT — BEED Q3 FY 2025-26

**Office of DM Beed + Department of Social Welfare**
**Reporting period:** 1 July - 30 September 2025
**Report date:** 8 October 2025

## Scheme

**Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)** + State-supplementary pension.

## Eligibility

- Age ≥ 60 years.
- Below Poverty Line (BPL) category.
- Annual household income ≤ INR 96,000 (urban) / INR 72,000 (rural).
- Resident of Maharashtra ≥ 10 years.

## Per-Beneficiary Monthly Pension (FY 2025-26)

| Category | Monthly pension (INR) |
|---|---|
| Below 80 years | 1,000 |
| 80 years + (enhanced rate) | 1,400 |

- Q3 disbursement = 3 months × per-beneficiary rate × beneficiaries.
- Coverage of eligible 60+ population in district: ~92% (state benchmark 90%).

## Disbursement Method

(i) DBT to beneficiary's individual bank account.
(ii) Monthly disbursement on 5th of each month.
(iii) Aadhaar-linked verification.

## Verification + Audit

- Annual Jeevan Pramaan (life certificate) verification at any pension-disbursing bank or e-mitra centre.
- Quarterly status audit.
- 18 complaints received Q3 (low for the volume); resolved within 21 days average.

## Notes

(i) 142 beneficiaries deceased during Q3 — beneficiary records being closed; survivor benefit transferred to spouse (where applicable) per state pension rules.

(ii) 88 new beneficiaries added during Q3 (newly-eligible turning 60).

(iii) **Caution for the no-Aadhaar cohort:** Of the 26,280 beneficiaries, 142 (0.54%) do not have linked Aadhaar — disbursement to these is via Election Photo ID + manual verification. Mobile Aadhaar enrolment camps in Q3 added 84 more to the linked cohort.
