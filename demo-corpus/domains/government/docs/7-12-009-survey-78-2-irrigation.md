## Irrigation Rights — Detailed

- **Canal supply:** D-14 minor canal, allocated 18,200 m³/year/hectare = 53,872 m³/year total.
- **Tube-well backup:** Own tube well (300 ft depth, installed 2015) — supplements canal during shortage years.
