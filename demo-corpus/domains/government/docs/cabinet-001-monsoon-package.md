# CABINET MEETING MINUTES — MONSOON RELIEF PACKAGE

**Government of Maharashtra**
**Date of meeting:** 12 August 2025
**Venue:** Cabinet Hall, Mantralaya, Mumbai
**Chair:** Hon'ble Chief Minister
**Secretary:** Principal Secretary, GAD (A. R. Kulkarni, IAS)
**Attendance:** All Cabinet Ministers; Chief Secretary (in attendance).

## Agenda Item 1: Monsoon Relief Package for 8 Affected Districts

### Discussion

The Cabinet considered the report from the State Disaster Management Authority (SDMA) on the impact of the 2025 monsoon (10 June – 8 August), which has been the most intense monsoon in Maharashtra in 22 years. Key impact metrics:

- Aggregate rainfall: 220% above the long-period average across 8 districts.
- Affected households (preliminary): 154,800.
- Crop damage estimate: 320,000 hectares (mostly kharif soybean + paddy + bajra).
- Cattle losses: 8,400 head.
- House damage (kachha): 38,200; pucca: 12,400.
- Loss of life: 86 (reported by SDMA + district administrations).

The CM emphasized that immediate cash relief and food/health supply chains must be prioritized; the relief package must reach citizens within 30 days of CR approval.

The Finance Minister noted that the package would draw INR 904 crore from the State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF) with the balance from Government of India NDRF reimbursement (request submitted).

The Agriculture Minister highlighted the need for crop compensation to reach kharif farmers before the next-season financing cycle.

### Decisions

(i) **Approval of the Monsoon Relief Package** with allocation as per the proposed GR (subsequently issued as gr-008-monsoon-relief-2025).

(ii) Aggregate allocation: **INR 904 crore** across 8 districts.

(iii) Components:
   - Cash relief: kachha INR 12,000; pucca INR 25,000.
   - Crop compensation: INR 18,000/ha.
   - Cattle compensation: INR 30,000/cow or buffalo.
   - Emergency food supply via PDS for 3 months.

(iv) **Disbursement timeline:** Tehsildar-level disbursement to commence within 30 days. Cabinet to receive weekly status updates.

(v) **Long-term resilience plan** to be drafted within 6 months covering:
   - Improved flood-warning + early-warning system.
   - Climate-resilient crop diversification + improved insurance.
   - Micro-irrigation revival.

### Action Items

1. Principal Secretary GAD to issue the GR within 72 hours. [Completed — GR dated 15 Aug 2025.]
2. SDMA to coordinate inter-district + inter-departmental disbursement.
3. Disaster Management Department to publish weekly status dashboard.

## Agenda Item 2: Submission to GoI for NDRF Reimbursement

Cabinet approved the submission of a formal NDRF reimbursement request to the Ministry of Home Affairs for the central share of disaster relief.
