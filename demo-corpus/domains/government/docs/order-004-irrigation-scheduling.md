# DM BEED — POST-MONSOON IRRIGATION SCHEDULING ORDER

**Order No.:** DM-BEED/2025/IRR-SCHED/0014
**Date:** 2 October 2025

## Background

The 2025 monsoon (rainfall 220% of long-period average) has dramatically improved water storage in the major reservoirs in Beed District. Per the State Water Resources Department dashboard:

| Reservoir | Storage (Oct 1, 2025) | % capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Manjara | 588 MCM | 89% |
| Bindusara | 412 MCM | 96% |
| Khelghat | 224 MCM | 78% |
| **Total district storage** | **1,224 MCM** | **89% average** |

Given this favourable situation, the rabi (winter) cropping season can be supported with a more generous irrigation allocation than in the past 3 drought-affected years.

## Order

### A. Rabi Irrigation Allocation

For the rabi cropping cycle (15 October 2025 - 28 February 2026):

(i) **Total allocation:** 612 MCM (50% of current district storage).

(ii) **Distribution priority:**
- 1st priority: Drinking water (240 MCM, 39%).
- 2nd priority: Wheat + gram (rabi food security crops) — 256 MCM (42%).
- 3rd priority: Sugarcane (perennial) — 88 MCM (14%).
- 4th priority: Other cash crops (vegetables, oilseeds) — 28 MCM (5%).

### B. Per-Hectare Allocation (rabi)

| Crop | Allocation (m³/ha) |
|---|---|
| Wheat | 4,200 |
| Gram (chickpea) | 2,800 |
| Sugarcane (continued from kharif) | 8,400 |
| Mustard / Oilseeds | 3,200 |
| Vegetables (selected) | 4,800 |

### C. Canal-System Rotation Schedule

Bindusara canal D-12 through D-18 — water releases on Mon/Wed/Fri (alternating).
Manjara canal D-1 through D-8 — water releases on Tue/Thu/Sat.

### D. Cropping-Pattern Compliance

Farmers in canal-irrigation command areas (per their 7/12 extracts) must:
(i) Submit crop-plan to local Talathi by 20 October 2025.
(ii) Stick to the planned crop; deviation requires Talathi approval.
(iii) Pay water-cess as per the schedule.

### E. Penalty for Misuse

Unauthorized water-tapping or use beyond allocation: notice + recovery + possible disconnection.

## Implementation

Sub-Divisional Water Resources Engineer + Tehsildars coordinate. Monthly status to DM.
