# DM BEED — ORDER ON SCHOOL CLOSURE DURING MONSOON FLOODING

**Order No.:** DM-BEED/2025/EDU-CLOSE/0009
**Date:** 14 August 2025

## Background

Given the intense monsoon rainfall over the past 4 days (12-13 Aug 2025) causing widespread flooding in 8 districts of Maharashtra (including Beed), and following the safety advisory from State Education Department + the Disaster Management Authority, the District Magistrate orders the following.

## Order

(i) **All schools (government, government-aided, and private) in Beed District** shall remain closed from **15 August 2025 (Friday) through 19 August 2025 (Tuesday) — both inclusive** — that is, for 5 days.

(ii) **Reason:** Public safety — floodwaters have made several roads + bridges unsafe; bus routes via village roads are unsafe; flood-warning level remains elevated.

(iii) **Schools shall not require teachers to attend** during this period, unless emergency duties (relief work) are assigned via the Education Department or DM.

(iv) **Mid-day meal arrangements:** Schools shall arrange home-delivery of ration packs (5-day equivalent) to students enrolled, using DBT-linked PDS infrastructure. The Education Department + Food and Civil Supplies Department to coordinate.

(v) **Examination + assessment:** Any tests / examinations scheduled for this period are postponed. Revised schedule to be announced by Education Department.

(vi) **Healthcare exception:** Children with medical emergencies should be brought to the nearest health centre; District Health Office is on alert.

## Enforcement + Compliance

District Education Officer (Beed) to issue corresponding circular to all schools by 14 Aug 2025 18:00 IST.

## Review

Order to be reviewed on 19 Aug 2025 based on weather + flood conditions. Schools may resume normal operations from 20 Aug 2025 if conditions permit.
