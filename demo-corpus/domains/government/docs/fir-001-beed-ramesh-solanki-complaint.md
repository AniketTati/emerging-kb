## Brief Facts (as recorded from complainant's statement)

The complainant, Shri Ramesh Solanki, states that:

(i) He had submitted a competitive bid for tender CIDCO/T-2025/NV-RR-014 (Road resurfacing Navi Mumbai Sector 14) through his firm Solanki Constructions, with a quoted price of INR 5.84 crore.

(ii) On 30 April 2025, the L1 announcement declared **ABC Construction Pvt Ltd as L1 at INR 4.28 crore**. The complainant noted that ABC Construction's bid was substantially below the cost-of-execution estimate for the work.

(iii) On 5 May 2025, the complainant filed an RTI application (RTI/CIDCO/2025/0488) asking for the bidder list and ABC Construction's blacklist disclosure.

(iv) The complainant has independently obtained evidence that **ABC Construction Pvt Ltd was blacklisted by PWD Maharashtra in 2022** for sub-standard work (PWD blacklist ref: BL-2022-148). The blacklist was lifted only in March 2024 — within the 5-year exclusion window stipulated in the CIDCO tender's eligibility criteria.

(v) Notwithstanding this, ABC Construction's bid disclosure stated "no blacklist in past 5 years" — a **misrepresentation** that materially affected the tender evaluation.

(vi) The complainant alleges that ABC Construction Pvt Ltd's bid + ineligibility-misrepresentation constitutes:
   - Cheating CIDCO into awarding the work (§420 IPC).
   - Forged document submission (§471 IPC).
   - Criminal conspiracy (§120-B IPC).

(vii) The complainant requests registration of FIR + investigation.
