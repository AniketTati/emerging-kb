# TRIBAL SCHOLARSHIP DISBURSEMENT — BEED Q2 FY 2025-26

**Office of DM Beed + Tribal Welfare Department**
**Reporting period:** 1 July - 30 September 2025
**Report date:** 5 September 2025

## Scheme

Per gr-005-tribal-welfare-fund (Tribal Welfare Fund Allocation FY 2025-26 — Beed share: INR 156 crore total; scholarships are ~30% of this).

## Beneficiary Categories

| Category | Beed Beneficiaries Q2 | Disbursement (INR) |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-Matric Scholarship (Classes 1-10) | 8,420 | 1,684 lakh |
| Post-Matric Scholarship (Classes 11-12) | 1,840 | 552 lakh |
| Graduation Scholarship | 542 | 271 lakh |
| Post-Graduation Scholarship | 88 | 88 lakh |
| Foreign Education Aid (1 beneficiary) | 1 | 14 lakh |
| Hostel + Mess Assistance | 1,420 | 568 lakh |
| **Q2 Total** | **12,311** | **3,177 lakh = INR 31.77 crore** |

## Aggregate Statistics

- Total tribal-listed students enrolled in Beed district: ~14,800.
- Beneficiaries this quarter: 12,311 = 83.2% coverage of tribal-listed students.
- Of beneficiaries with bank accounts: 11,932 (96.9%).
- Beneficiaries with Aadhaar-linkage: 11,684 (94.9%).
- Beneficiaries with mobile-based DBT confirmation: 10,200 (82.8%).

## Disbursement Method

(i) All disbursements via DBT to beneficiary-or-parent bank account.
(ii) Aadhaar verification required at registration.
(iii) Beneficiaries without Aadhaar: alternative authentication via Election Photo Identity Card + voter-roll cross-reference (per Supreme Court Aadhaar judgment + State guidelines).

## Cross-Talk with Land Records

Of the 12,311 beneficiaries this quarter, ~1,840 are from tribal-allotment villages where families hold land per the MLR Code §36A (e.g., Survey No. 22/3 in Tanda Talli — see 7-12-006-survey-22-3). Tribal Welfare Department coordinates with Revenue Department for cross-verification.

## Quality Audit

- 4% sample audit by State Tribal Development Commissioner: no irregularities.
- 0.4% complaints (49 complaints) — all resolved within 30 days.

## Notes

(i) Foreign-education aid (1 beneficiary): A doctoral student at the University of Edinburgh; assistance per the State Tribal Welfare Scheme.

(ii) **Hostel + Mess Assistance** category covers students residing in government tribal hostels — covers tuition + boarding + meals.

(iii) The Beed scholarship coverage (83.2%) is slightly below the state average of 87% — possible due to documentation gaps in remote villages; mobile-camp registration scheduled for Q3 to close the gap.
