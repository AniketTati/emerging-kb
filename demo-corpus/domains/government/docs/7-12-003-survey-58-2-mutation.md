## **उत्तर-निवारण आदेश / Mutation Order — 7/12 Extract (Boundary Correction)**

**Tehsil / Beed | Survey No. 58/2 | Village: Limgaon**
**Mutation No. 2025/0823 | Date: 8 February 2025**

### Subject

Boundary correction for Survey No. 58/2 (4.86 hectares) at Limgaon village. The eastern boundary, as per the 1956 land-revenue map, was 168 metres; subsequent re-survey using Total Station equipment (conducted June 2024) established the actual on-ground boundary at 158 metres — a 10-metre discrepancy that affects ~0.42 ha of the holding.

### Background

The discrepancy was identified during a state-wide land-record digitization exercise. The affected owner is **Smt. Sunanda Pawar (W/o Shankar Pawar)**, who has been in continuous physical possession of the boundary as currently demarcated on the ground (i.e., 158m). She has no objection to the boundary correction.

### Order

(i) The eastern boundary of Survey No. 58/2 is hereby corrected from 168m to 158m, per the Total Station re-survey.

(ii) The 0.42 hectare adjustment is to be reflected as: Survey No. 58/2 reduced to 4.44 hectares; the 0.42 hectare strip transferred to the adjacent Survey No. 58/3 (govt khalsa land — already used by adjacent farmer).

(iii) Updated boundary plans to be filed at the Tehsil office.

### Outcome

Survey No. 58/2 area updated from 4.86 ha to 4.44 ha.
Survey No. 58/3 (govt-owned) increased by 0.42 ha.

### Authorised by

**Tehsildar Sunil Pawar — 8 February 2025**
