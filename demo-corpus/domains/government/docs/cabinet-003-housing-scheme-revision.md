# CABINET MEETING MINUTES — PMAY BEED ALLOCATION REVISION (PRECURSOR TO CORRIGENDUM)

**Date:** 15 August 2024
**Venue:** Cabinet Hall, Mantralaya
**Chair:** Hon'ble CM

## Agenda: Discussion of revised PMAY allocation for Beed district based on re-verified beneficiary count

### Background

The original GR dated 15 May 2024 sanctioned **INR 84 crore** for PMAY-G in Beed district FY 2024-25 based on an estimated 5,200 households. Subsequent re-verification by the DM office (1 June – 12 August 2024) reduced the eligible-household count to **3,840**, identifying:

- 740 households with confirmed pucca housing (no longer eligible).
- 420 households that have migrated out of the district.
- 200 households with Aadhaar-linkage duplicates (consolidated to existing).

### Discussion

The Rural Development Minister presented the re-verification findings. Key debate points:

1. **Should the allocation be revised or maintained at INR 84 crore with reallocation to other deserving districts?**

   Finance Minister noted that the unutilised portion if not re-allocated would need to be returned to the Treasury at year-end. Cabinet preferred revision over keeping a larger envelope.

2. **Treatment of Tranche 1 (INR 33.6 crore) already disbursed.**

   DM Beed has utilised approximately INR 24.88 crore of Tranche 1 against verified beneficiaries; INR 8.72 crore remains unutilised. Cabinet decided to allow carry-over rather than refund (administrative simpler).

3. **Communication to affected stakeholders.**

   The Cabinet emphasized clear communication to: District administration, the 1,360 households previously listed but now removed from eligibility (with reasons), and the Centre (Ministry of Rural Development).

### Decisions

(i) **Approval of allocation revision** from INR 84 crore to **INR 62.20 crore** (calculated as 3,840 × INR 1.62 lakh).

(ii) **Corrigendum to be issued** by GAD within 7 days. (Subsequently issued as gr-001-pmay-beed-corrigendum on 22 Aug 2024.)

(iii) **Tranche 1 carry-over:** The unutilised INR 8.72 crore of Tranche 1 shall be applied to Tranche 2 disbursement against the verified beneficiary list.

(iv) **Notification of 1,360 removed beneficiaries:** District administration to issue formal communication within 30 days, with grievance/appeal process noted.

### Action Items

1. GAD to draft and issue the corrigendum (target: 22 August 2024).
2. DM Beed to publish revised beneficiary list and grievance mechanism.
3. Director Housing (Rural) to track Tranche-2 disbursement against revised list.
