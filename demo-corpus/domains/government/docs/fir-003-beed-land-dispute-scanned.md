## Brief Facts

The complainant, Pravin Lokhande, has stated:

(i) On approximately 28 June 2025, the complainant received a registered legal notice from one **Shri Datta Anandrao Mane** (resident of Sarpanchwadi village, Beed Taluka) claiming that he is the rightful heir of late Sambhaji Lokhande via a maternal-grand-uncle chain, and demanding transfer of Survey No. 44/1 within 30 days.

(ii) Subsequently, on 5 July 2025, the complainant alleges that **Datta Mane along with 3-4 associates** appeared at his residence and at the field on the disputed land. Their conduct included:
   - Verbal threats to "make him forget the land in a way he'll remember."
   - Threats against his family (specifically against his married daughter who lives at Aurangabad).
   - Demand for INR 2 crore as a "settlement" to drop the title claim.

(iii) The complainant has filed a civil suit (PCB-CIV-2025/0188) in Bombay High Court (filed January 2025 by Mane); the criminal allegations (extortion + intimidation) are separate from the civil dispute and warrant criminal investigation.

(iv) The complainant has provided:
   - Copy of registered legal notice from Mane;
   - Witness statements from 3 villagers + 1 neighbour who heard the 5 July incident;
   - Audio recording (allegedly partial recording) of one of the verbal threats — to be technically verified.

## Accused

- **Datta Anandrao Mane** (primary accused).
- 3-4 unidentified associates (descriptive details only).
