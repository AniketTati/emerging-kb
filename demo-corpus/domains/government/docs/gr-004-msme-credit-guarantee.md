# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA — GAD
### GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION

**GR No.:** GAD-MSME-2025/CR-0472/MSME-4
**Date:** 12 April 2025

**Subject:** Maharashtra MSME Credit Guarantee Scheme — extension + subsidy enhancement.

## Resolution

(i) The Maharashtra MSME Credit Guarantee Scheme (MMSCGS) is hereby extended for FY 2025-26 with enhanced features.

(ii) Coverage: Manufacturing + service-sector MSMEs registered under the Udyam portal with annual turnover ≤ INR 50 crore.

(iii) Guarantee cover: **75% of the credit facility, up to a maximum of INR 5 crore per beneficiary.**

(iv) Interest subvention: 5% per annum for first 5 years (up from previous 3%).

(v) Special preferences:
   - Women-led MSMEs: additional 2% interest subvention.
   - SC/ST-led MSMEs: additional 2% interest subvention.
   - Tier-3 + tier-4 cities: additional 1% interest subvention.

(vi) State outlay: **INR 280 crore** for guarantee fee + interest subvention in FY 2025-26.

(vii) Implementing partner: State Industrial and Investment Corporation (SIDBI tie-up).

(viii) Target: cover **18,000 new MSME credit facilities** in FY 2025-26.

(ix) Application via the Maha-MSME portal; turnaround time commitment: 15 working days.
