# CABINET MEETING MINUTES — MID-DAY MEAL SCHEME EXPANSION

**Date:** 20 June 2025
**Chair:** Hon'ble CM

## Agenda: PM POSHAN / Mid-Day Meal Scheme — per-meal rate revision + Bal Vatika inclusion

### Discussion

The Education Minister presented the case for revising per-meal rates, citing:
- Inflation impact since the last revision (2021-22) — food-basket CPI up 32%.
- Nutritional gap among rural pre-primary children (Bal Vatika cohort newly included).
- Industry-coalition recommendations (Akshaya Patra Foundation, ISKCON Food Relief) on minimum cost-per-meal.

Finance Minister sought clarification on the state's FY 2025-26 fiscal envelope — confirmed the INR 1,580 crore allocation fits within the projected revenue receipts.

The Women & Child Development Minister supported the inclusion of Bal Vatika children, noting that early-life nutrition supplementation reduces stunting + wasting by 18-22 percentage points (per NFHS-5 secondary analysis).

### Decisions

(i) **Per-meal rate revision approved:**
   - Bal Vatika (pre-primary, new): INR 9.00/meal.
   - Class 1-5 (primary): INR 6.40 → INR 8.80/meal.
   - Class 6-8 (upper primary): INR 9.20 → INR 12.80/meal.

(ii) **Egg + fruit:** 2× per week for all tiers — state additional cost.

(iii) **Tribal-area schools:** Additional protein supplement (sprouts + milk).

(iv) **State allocation:** INR 1,580 crore for FY 2025-26.

(v) **Effective date:** 1 July 2025.

(vi) **GR to be issued** by 25 June 2025. (Subsequently issued as gr-009-school-meal-scheme.)

### Action Items

1. GAD to issue GR.
2. School Education Department to roll out training to district nodal officers.
3. Akshaya Patra Foundation + other implementing NGOs to be briefed on revised rates.
