# FIRST INFORMATION REPORT (FIR) — MOTOR VEHICLE COLLISION

**Police Station:** Beed Highway Police (NH-211 Beat)
**FIR No.:** 0388/2025
**Date of registration:** 22 September 2025, 04:42 IST (within 1 hour of incident)
**Sections Invoked:** IPC §279 (rash driving), §304-A (negligent homicide), Motor Vehicles Act §134

## Brief Facts

At approximately 03:48 IST on 22 September 2025, a head-on collision occurred between two vehicles on the Beed-Aurangabad National Highway (NH-211), approximately 12 km north of Beed town, at the railway-overbridge approach.

**Vehicle 1:** Heavy Goods Vehicle (HGV) — Maruti Suzuki Eeco light commercial, reg MH-23 AS 1882. Driver: Shri Sanjay Khedkar, age 42. Carrying agricultural produce (sugarcane).

**Vehicle 2:** Two-wheeler (motorcycle) — Hero Splendor, reg MH-23 BD 4521. Rider: Shri Aman Patil, age 24. No pillion.

## Casualties

- **Aman Patil** (motorcycle rider): Sustained critical head + chest injuries; declared deceased on arrival at Beed Civil Hospital at 04:38 IST.
- **Sanjay Khedkar** (HGV driver): Sustained minor cuts + concussion; admitted Beed Civil Hospital for observation; under police custody.

## Sequence of Events (reconstructed)

(i) At approximately 03:45 IST, the HGV driver Sanjay Khedkar attempted to overtake a slow-moving truck on the highway approach to the overbridge.

(ii) During the overtake manoeuvre, the HGV crossed into the opposing lane. At that moment, the motorcycle rider Aman Patil (travelling in the opposite direction at approximately 50-55 km/h) was approaching.

(iii) Head-on collision occurred approximately 240 metres from the overbridge approach. The motorcycle was thrown into the highway shoulder; rider Aman Patil suffered critical injuries.

(iv) Both vehicles were extensively damaged; the HGV came to a stop in the centre of the carriageway, blocking northbound traffic for ~90 minutes during initial response + investigation.

## Investigation

- HGV impounded for forensic examination (mechanical fitness + brake testing).
- Motorcycle examined; rider's helmet was on but ill-fitting.
- Witness statements collected from 4 individuals at the scene + nearby roadside establishments.
- Traffic camera footage (highway CCTV) being retrieved.
- HGV driver's blood alcohol test: negative.
- Mobile phone records of HGV driver: being checked for distracted-driving evidence.

## Sections Invoked

- IPC §279 (rash + negligent driving on public road).
- IPC §304-A (causing death by negligence).
- Motor Vehicles Act §134 (driver's duty to stop + report; satisfied as driver was at scene).
- Motor Vehicles Act §177 (general driving offences).

## Status

- HGV driver arrested, produced before magistrate, granted bail with conditions (per CrPC §436A); subject to investigation.
- Investigation under §157 CrPC commenced.
- Family of deceased + injured being supported by the Tehsildar's office under Motor Accident Claims framework.
