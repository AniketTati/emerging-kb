# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA — GAD
### GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION

**GR No.:** GAD-HOU-2025/CR-0098/HOUSING-1
**Date:** 22 January 2025

**Subject:** MHADA Affordable Housing Mumbai — allocation policy revision.

## Resolution

(i) The Maharashtra Housing and Area Development Authority (MHADA) Affordable Housing Scheme for Mumbai allocation policy is revised effective 1 April 2025.

(ii) Income criteria for FY 2025-26:

| Category | Annual household income | Reserved share |
|---|---|---|
| EWS | up to INR 6 lakh | 35% |
| LIG | INR 6 lakh - INR 12 lakh | 35% |
| MIG-1 | INR 12 lakh - INR 18 lakh | 20% |
| MIG-2 | INR 18 lakh - INR 24 lakh | 10% |

(iii) Geographic distribution: Western Suburbs 40%, Eastern Suburbs 25%, Central Mumbai 20%, South Mumbai 5%, Navi Mumbai 10%.

(iv) Lottery mechanism: monthly draws via the MHADA online portal. Aadhaar + PAN verification mandatory; KYC at allotment.

(v) Maximum tenure for affordable housing: 99 years (long-lease).

(vi) Allocation target FY 2025-26: 4,200 units.

(vii) Affordable housing pricing for FY 2025-26:
   - EWS: INR 24-36 lakh per unit.
   - LIG: INR 42-58 lakh per unit.
   - MIG-1: INR 65-82 lakh per unit.
   - MIG-2: INR 88-115 lakh per unit.

(viii) Maintenance cess: 3% of annual lease rent, paid annually.
