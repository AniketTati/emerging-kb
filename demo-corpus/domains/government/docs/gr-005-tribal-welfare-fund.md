# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA — GAD
### GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION

**GR No.:** GAD-TRI-2025/CR-0388/TRIBAL-2
**Date:** 8 March 2025

**Subject:** Tribal Welfare Fund Allocation FY 2025-26 — district-wise + scheme-wise breakdown.

## Resolution

(i) Approval of state-level tribal welfare allocation for FY 2025-26.

(ii) Aggregate allocation: **INR 4,840 crore** for FY 2025-26, of which:
   - Central share (Ministry of Tribal Affairs): INR 1,560 crore
   - State share: INR 3,280 crore

(iii) District-wise allocation (top 12):

| District | Allocation (INR crore) | Tribal % of population |
|---|---|---|
| Nandurbar | 612 | 67% |
| Gadchiroli | 488 | 54% |
| Yavatmal | 384 | 18% |
| Thane | 348 | 12% |
| Palghar | 312 | 38% |
| Nashik | 288 | 27% |
| Dhule | 244 | 35% |
| Amravati | 220 | 14% |
| Pune | 184 | 6% |
| Beed | 156 | 5% |
| Aurangabad | 144 | 4% |
| Other 24 districts | 1,460 | varies |
| **Total** | **4,840** | — |

(iv) Scheme-wise breakdown:
   - Scholarships + education: INR 1,420 crore
   - Skill development: INR 680 crore
   - Health: INR 588 crore
   - Housing: INR 740 crore
   - Forest Rights Act implementation: INR 412 crore
   - Tribal sub-plan livelihoods: INR 660 crore
   - Cultural preservation: INR 124 crore
   - Administration + monitoring: INR 216 crore

(v) Implementation: District tribal welfare officers report to State Tribal Development Commissioner.

(vi) **CRITICAL:** Aadhaar-linked DBT for all individual benefits. For households without Aadhaar, alternative authentication (voter ID, Election Photo Identity Card) accepted per Supreme Court Aadhaar judgment.

(vii) Audit: quarterly CAG audit + tribal-area community-based monitoring committees.
