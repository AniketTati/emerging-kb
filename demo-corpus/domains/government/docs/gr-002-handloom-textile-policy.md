# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA
## General Administration Department
### GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION

**GR No.:** GAD-IND-2024/CR-2104/IND-12
**Date:** 10 September 2024

**Subject:** Maharashtra Handloom and Textile Industry Policy, 2024-2029.

## Resolution

The Government hereby approves the Maharashtra Handloom and Textile Industry Policy 2024-2029, with the following key provisions:

### 1. Capital subsidy
- Up to **25% capital subsidy** on plant + machinery for new textile-park units in identified textile districts.
- Cap: INR 15 crore per unit.

### 2. Interest subvention
- 6% interest subvention on term loans for working capital.
- Validity: 5 years from commissioning.

### 3. Skill development
- INR 240 crore allocation over 5 years for the Maharashtra Textile Skills Mission.
- Target: train 80,000 weavers + technicians.

### 4. GST reimbursement
- For new textile units in Vidarbha and Marathwada regions: 50% of state-GST reimbursed for 5 years.

### 5. Total package
- Aggregate fiscal outlay over 5 years: **INR 1,820 crore**.

### 6. Designated textile districts
- Solapur, Ichalkaranji, Bhiwandi, Yeola, Nashik, Aurangabad, Nagpur, Amravati.

### 7. Implementation

Department of Industries, Energy and Labour, GoM, is designated as the nodal department. State Industrial Investment Corporation (MIDC) acts as the implementing agency.
