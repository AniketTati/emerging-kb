# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA — GAD
### GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION

**GR No.:** GAD-EDU-2025/CR-1148/EDU-3
**Date:** 25 June 2025

**Subject:** Mid-Day Meal (MDM) Scheme — expansion + per-meal rate revision FY 2025-26.

## Resolution

(i) The Mid-Day Meal Scheme (also known as PM POSHAN at the central level) is hereby expanded for FY 2025-26 with the following revisions.

(ii) **Per-meal rate** (combined central + state, on actual cooking + materials):

| Class | Pre-revised | Revised | Increase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bal Vatika (pre-primary) | — | INR 9.00 | (new) |
| Class 1-5 (primary) | INR 6.40 | INR 8.80 | +INR 2.40 |
| Class 6-8 (upper primary) | INR 9.20 | INR 12.80 | +INR 3.60 |

(iii) Coverage: All government + government-aided primary + upper-primary schools.

(iv) Special provisions:
   - **Egg + fruit:** introduced 2× per week for all tiers (state additional cost).
   - **Tribal-area schools:** additional protein supplement (sprouts + milk).
   - **Quality:** All cooked food to be tested monthly via designated lab.

(v) Nutritional standards:
   - Calories: 450 (Class 1-5), 700 (Class 6-8) per meal.
   - Protein: 12 g (Class 1-5), 20 g (Class 6-8) per meal.

(vi) Total state allocation: **INR 1,580 crore** for FY 2025-26.

(vii) Implementation: District Education Officers, with monthly review by State School Education Department.

(viii) Audit: Quarterly community-based monitoring (parents + village education committee).

(ix) Effective date: 1 July 2025 (subsequent to school re-opening).
