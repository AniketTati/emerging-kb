# CABINET MEETING MINUTES — MSME CREDIT GUARANTEE EXTENSION

**Date:** 8 April 2025
**Venue:** Cabinet Hall, Mantralaya
**Chair:** Hon'ble CM

## Agenda: Extension + Enhancement of Maharashtra MSME Credit Guarantee Scheme (MMSCGS) for FY 2025-26

### Discussion

The Industries Minister presented the proposal. Key points discussed:

- Existing MMSCGS has supported ~14,200 MSME credit facilities since launch (April 2022); aggregate guaranteed coverage INR 1,840 crore.
- Default rate within tolerance: 4.8% (vs the 6% projection).
- Job-creation impact: estimated 64,000 direct jobs.
- Industry feedback: women-led + SC/ST-led MSMEs sought additional interest subvention.

CM emphasized that micro-enterprises (turnover <INR 50 lakh) should not be disadvantaged by the income-cap on guarantee cover.

Finance Minister noted that the state outlay of INR 280 crore is within FY 2025-26 budget envelope; reimbursement-from-Centre under PM Vishwakarma scheme being negotiated.

### Decisions

(i) Approval of MMSCGS extension + enhancements per the proposed GR (subsequently issued as gr-004-msme-credit-guarantee).

(ii) **Guarantee cover:** 75% (up from 65%) of credit facility, ceiling INR 5 crore (was INR 3 crore).

(iii) **Interest subvention:** 5% (was 3%) for first 5 years.

(iv) **Special preferences:** Additional 2% subvention for women-led + SC/ST-led MSMEs; 1% additional for tier-3/4 cities.

(v) State outlay: **INR 280 crore** for FY 2025-26.

(vi) Target: **18,000 new credit facilities** in FY 2025-26.

### Action Items

1. GR to be issued by 15 April 2025.
2. Maha-MSME portal to be updated within 30 days.
3. SIDBI tie-up to be revised — implementing partner role.
4. Quarterly Cabinet status review.
