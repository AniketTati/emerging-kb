# DM BEED — TENDER NOTICE — GOVERNMENT SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION

**Tender Reference:** BEED/T-2025/EDU-012
**Date:** 22 May 2025
**Last date:** 22 June 2025 17:00 IST

## Scope

Construction of 3 government primary schools across Beed taluka villages:
- Village 1 (Wadi Bhosalewadi): 12-classroom block + admin + 2 toilet blocks + 1 kitchen
- Village 2 (Limgaon): 8-classroom block
- Village 3 (Pimpalgaon): 10-classroom block + admin

## Estimated Cost

INR 4.84 crore (combined for 3 sites).

## Specs

- RCC frame construction.
- Per-classroom area: 50 sq m.
- Separate boys + girls toilet blocks (3 toilets each per block).
- Kitchen for mid-day meal preparation (per State School Education guidelines).
- Solar-powered lighting + bore-well water (per energy-efficient construction GR).

## EMD

INR 4.84 lakh.

## Eligibility

- Class-1 contractor.
- 2 similar government-building projects completed in last 5 years.
- Aggregate turnover (last 3 FY) ≥ INR 10 crore.

## Award

L1 (lowest qualifying bid) awarded across all 3 sites or individually if site-specific bidders preferred.

## Timeline

- Award: 30 days.
- Execution: 12 months (target completion before 2026 academic year).

## Funding

Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan + state matching share.
