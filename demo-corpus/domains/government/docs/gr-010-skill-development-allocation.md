# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA — GAD
### GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION

**GR No.:** GAD-SKILL-2025/CR-0892/SKILL-2
**Date:** 30 April 2025

**Subject:** Maharashtra Skill Development Mission — Fund Allocation FY 2025-26.

## Resolution

(i) Approval of fund allocation under the Maharashtra Skill Development Mission for FY 2025-26.

(ii) **Aggregate allocation: INR 480 crore** for FY 2025-26.

(iii) Programme-wise breakdown:

| Programme | Allocation (INR crore) | Target trainees |
|---|---|---|
| Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) state share | 184 | 92,000 |
| Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) | 64 | 42,000 |
| Industry-led short-term skilling | 92 | 38,000 |
| Maharashtra Industry-Academia Skills Network | 56 | 18,000 |
| Skills for special groups (women, SC/ST, persons with disability) | 48 | 24,000 |
| Tribal-area skills missions | 28 | 14,000 |
| Apprenticeship subsidies | 8 | 6,000 |
| **Total** | **480** | **234,000** |

(iv) Sector priorities:
   - Manufacturing + electronics: 28%
   - IT-ITES + tech: 22%
   - Construction + infrastructure: 16%
   - Health + paramedical: 12%
   - Renewable energy: 10%
   - Services + retail + hospitality: 8%
   - Agriculture + agritech: 4%

(v) Tier-3 / tier-4 city focus: 60% of trainee target from tier-3/4 cities.

(vi) Industry partner participation: minimum 240 industry partners (with binding placement MOUs).

(vii) Placement target: 70% of trainees placed within 3 months of certification.

(viii) Outcome-based payments: 50% of cost paid to training partner only upon successful placement-verification.
