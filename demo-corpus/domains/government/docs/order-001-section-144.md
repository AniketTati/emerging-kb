# DISTRICT MAGISTRATE, BEED — ORDER UNDER CrPC §144

**Order No.:** DM-BEED/2025/SEC-144/0018
**Date:** 4 May 2025

## Background

Following the public-order incident at Beed Mandi on 2 May 2025 (FIR-001 + parallel public disturbance), and given that:

(i) There is reasonable apprehension of breach of peace + public order;
(ii) Two community groups have raised the temperature via inflammatory social-media posts;
(iii) The 2 May 2025 tender-fraud allegations + subsequent counter-allegations have created an emotional environment.

Therefore, the undersigned, in exercise of powers under **CrPC §144**, hereby issues this prohibitory order.

## Prohibitions

Effective from 4 May 2025 (00:01 IST) to 13 May 2025 (23:59 IST) within the **Beed City + Beed Mandi area** (precise geographic boundary: NH-211 from Limgaon Bridge to Patoda Junction, and Beed urban + Mandi area):

(i) **Assembly of 5 or more persons** in any public place is prohibited.

(ii) **Carrying of any weapon, lathi, knife, or any other instrument capable of causing injury** is prohibited.

(iii) **Loudspeakers, loud-music events, public processions, or public gatherings** are prohibited without prior written permission from the SDM Beed City.

(iv) **Inflammatory speeches** in any public or private space are prohibited.

(v) **Social-media posts, sharing of unverified images/videos, or content that may incite community tension** are prohibited; any such material may be taken down upon order.

## Exceptions

(i) Funeral processions (with maximum 25 persons + advance notice to SDM).
(ii) Religious gatherings at established places of worship (no procession to non-traditional venues).
(iii) Wedding ceremonies (capped at 100 persons + advance notice).
(iv) Education + healthcare facilities operate normally.
(v) Government, emergency, + essential-service personnel exempt from the gathering prohibition while in performance of duty.

## Enforcement

Maharashtra Police (Beed District) — Superintendent of Police Anant Jadhav, IPS, in coordination with the Sub-Divisional Magistrate Beed City.

Violators subject to action under IPC §188 (disobedience of public-servant order).

## Review

This order shall be reviewed every 3 days for early lifting if circumstances permit.
