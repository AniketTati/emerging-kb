# RTI RESPONSE — FALSE-PREMISE QUERY (NO SUCH CIRCULAR EXISTS)

**RTI Reference:** RTI/GAD/2025/1428
**Filed:** 18 August 2025
**Response date:** 12 September 2025

## Applicant

**Name:** Shri Anand Pendharkar (citizen)

## RTI Application

> "Under §6 of the RTI Act 2005, I request:
>
> Please provide me with the full text + date of issue + signatory officer for **Circular No. GAD/CIR/2024/99999 dated 22 December 2024** allegedly issued by your department on the subject of "Migration policy for state employees".
>
> Additionally, please disclose:
> - Number of state employees who applied for migration under this circular.
> - Number approved.
> - Number rejected with reasons.
> - Geographic distribution of approved migrants."

## Response

### Determination

The Office of the Principal Secretary, GAD, has searched the GAD circular registry, the Maharashtra Government Resolutions portal, and the State Government archives for the period 2024-2025 and reports the following:

> **No circular bearing reference "GAD/CIR/2024/99999" has been issued by the General Administration Department (GAD), Government of Maharashtra, on the subject of "Migration policy for state employees" or any other subject.**
>
> **There is no record of this circular in any of our databases.**

### Verification Steps Undertaken

(i) Search of the GAD circular registry (electronic + physical) — no match.
(ii) Search of the Maharashtra Government Resolutions portal (Aaple Sarkar / Mantralaya issuances) — no match.
(iii) Search of the GAD section in the State Archives — no match.
(iv) Inquiry with related departments (HR + Personnel + Employee Welfare) — no record.

### Reason for Non-Existence

The reference number format "CIR/2024/99999" does not conform to the GAD's circular-numbering convention (GAD circulars in 2024 numbered 0001 - 1247; "99999" exceeds any actual issued number).

### Subsequent Information Requested by Applicant

Items (a), (b), (c), and (d) — number of employees who applied / approved / rejected / geographic distribution — **cannot be provided** because the underlying circular does not exist. There has been no such migration policy, no applications, no approvals, no rejections.

### Possible Explanation for Applicant's Belief

The applicant may have been misled by:
(i) Social-media speculation about a hypothetical employee-migration policy (no formal proposal exists);
(ii) A confusing reference to a separate, unrelated circular GAD/CIR/2024/0249 dated April 2024 (regarding general posting policy — entirely different subject);
(iii) Misinformation or fabrication.

### Statutory Acknowledgement

Per the Right to Information Act 2005, this response is provided within the statutory 30-day window for a question that has no responsive answer because the queried document does not exist. The applicant is welcome to:

- File first appeal under §19 if they believe a different circular exists that should have been disclosed.
- Provide additional context (e.g., source where they encountered this reference) for further investigation.

### Note

If the applicant has information suggesting that this circular does exist (e.g., from a specific newspaper article or government document citing it), they are encouraged to share that reference. GAD is committed to transparency and will re-investigate if new evidence is presented.
