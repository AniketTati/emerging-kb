# RTI RESPONSE — BEED MUTATION ORDERS DISCLOSURE

**RTI Reference:** RTI/DM-BEED/2025/0192
**Filed:** 25 June 2025
**Response date:** 20 July 2025

## Applicant

**Name:** Shri Mehboob Khan (citizen, civic researcher)
**Address:** Beed (full address withheld per applicant request)

## RTI Application

> "I request the following information under §6 of the RTI Act, 2005:
>
> 1. Total number of mutation orders issued by Tehsildars under Beed district during FY 2024-25, broken down by Tehsil.
>
> 2. Of these, how many involved mutations from deceased holder to widow/legal heirs.
>
> 3. Average processing time (days from application to order).
>
> 4. Total number of mutation applications rejected, with reasons (in aggregated form, no individual names)."

## Response

CIDCO ... wait, this is DM Beed responding. Pardon — Office of DM Beed responds:

| Tehsil | Total mutations (FY 2024-25) | Widow/heir mutations | Avg processing (days) | Rejections |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beed | 1,824 | 412 | 38 | 188 |
| Ashti | 624 | 142 | 42 | 84 |
| Patoda | 412 | 92 | 36 | 56 |
| Kaij | 528 | 138 | 44 | 72 |
| Manjlegaon | 612 | 154 | 40 | 88 |
| Wadwani | 384 | 84 | 36 | 52 |
| Ambejogai | 384 | 96 | 38 | 56 |
| Gevrai | 542 | 124 | 41 | 78 |
| Parli | 488 | 118 | 43 | 64 |
| Shirur Kasar | 286 | 64 | 38 | 38 |
| Dharur | 388 | 86 | 39 | 52 |
| **Total** | **6,472** | **1,510** | **40 (avg)** | **828** |

Rejection reasons (aggregated):
- Incomplete documentation: ~58% of rejections.
- Disputed claims (multiple heirs contesting): ~22%.
- Land falls in tribal-allotment area requiring DM approval (process delayed): ~8%.
- Bona-fide errors (procedural): ~12%.

## Notes

(i) Of the 6,472 mutations, approximately 23% (1,510) were widow/heir mutations following decedent's death — consistent with the district's age demographic.

(ii) Processing time of 40 days (avg) is within the statutory 60-day window for routine mutations. The longer-tail cases (>60 days) are typically the disputed ones.

(iii) Per §8(1)(j) of the RTI Act, individual mutation details are not disclosed; only aggregate statistics are provided.

## Right of Appeal

Per §19 of the RTI Act, applicant may appeal within 30 days.
