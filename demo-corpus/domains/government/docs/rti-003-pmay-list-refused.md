# RTI RESPONSE — PARTIAL REFUSAL (PII EXEMPTION)

**RTI Reference:** RTI/DM-BEED/2025/0264
**Filed:** 22 May 2025
**Response date:** 15 June 2025

## Applicant

**Name:** Shri Rohit Jadhav (registered citizen, Beed)

## RTI Application

> "I request:
> 1. The full list of PMAY-G beneficiaries for Beed district FY 2024-25, including:
>    - Beneficiary name
>    - Aadhaar number
>    - Bank account number
>    - Disbursement amount + date
>    - Village + Survey number of the housing site.
>
> 2. Reasons for the allocation reduction from INR 84 crore to INR 62.20 crore (corrigendum dated 22 Aug 2024)."

## Response

The Office of DM Beed has examined the application and responds:

### Item 1 — REFUSED (Partial)

The applicant has requested the **complete beneficiary list with Aadhaar, bank account, and Survey numbers**. This information is **exempt from disclosure** under:

(i) **§8(1)(j) of the RTI Act, 2005** — Information that relates to personal information the disclosure of which has no relationship to any public activity or interest, or which would cause unwarranted invasion of the privacy of the individual. Aadhaar numbers and bank account numbers fall squarely within this exemption.

(ii) **The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** — protects citizen identifiers. Aadhaar disclosure (other than to the citizen themselves or with their explicit consent) is prohibited.

(iii) **The Aadhaar Act 2016** — specific limitations on disclosure of Aadhaar.

### Item 1 — Aggregate disclosure (alternative)

The following aggregate-level information is disclosed instead:

- Total beneficiaries (verified): 3,840 households.
- Total disbursement (FY 2024-25): INR 62.20 crore.
- Average per-household disbursement: ~INR 1.62 lakh.
- Geographic distribution: 14 talukas of Beed district.
- Demographic profile (aggregated): 64% SC/ST/OBC; 36% general/other; 38% female-headed households; 22% with disability or senior citizen primary applicant.

### Item 2 — Reasons for allocation revision

The reduction from INR 84 crore to INR 62.20 crore is documented in the **Corrigendum GR No. GAD-PMAY-2024/CR-1832/POL-7-CORR-1 dated 22 August 2024** (publicly issued, see gr-001-pmay-beed-corrigendum). Key reasons:

(i) Re-verification reduced eligible beneficiaries from 5,200 estimated to 3,840 verified.
(ii) 740 households confirmed to have existing pucca housing (no longer eligible).
(iii) 420 households migrated out of district.
(iv) 200 households had Aadhaar duplicates consolidated.

The corrigendum is publicly available on the Maharashtra Government Resolutions portal.
