# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA — GAD
### GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION

**GR No.:** GAD-WAT-2025/CR-0288/W&I-3
**Date:** 18 February 2025

**Subject:** Water Conservation Programme for Marathwada Region (8 districts) — phase 2 implementation.

## Resolution

(i) Approval of phase-2 water-conservation works across 8 districts of Marathwada — Beed, Latur, Osmanabad, Aurangabad, Jalna, Parbhani, Hingoli, Nanded.

(ii) Aggregate allocation: **INR 1,240 crore** for FY 2025-26.

| District | Allocation (INR crore) |
|---|---|
| Beed | 188 |
| Latur | 156 |
| Osmanabad | 132 |
| Aurangabad | 188 |
| Jalna | 142 |
| Parbhani | 164 |
| Hingoli | 124 |
| Nanded | 146 |
| **Total** | **1,240** |

(iii) Works covered: 1,200 farm ponds, 320 check dams, 480 cement nala bunds, 12 medium-sized stabilization reservoirs.

(iv) Implementation through District Magistrates with on-ground execution by State Water Resources Department + local Gram Panchayats.

(v) Mandatory third-party audit by the State Water Mission Authority — quarterly.

(vi) Beneficiary feedback mechanism via dedicated grievance portal.

(vii) Project completion target: 31 March 2026.

(viii) Tribal-area considerations: 18% of allocation specifically earmarked for tribal blocks.

(ix) Climate-adaptation overlay: works to be designed for projected 2030-2050 rainfall variability.
