# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA

**Mantralaya, Madame Cama Road, Mumbai 400032**

**Date:** 15 May 2024

**Subject:** Allocation of funds under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin) — PMAY-G — for the district of Beed, FY 2024-25.

---

## Preamble

The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin) scheme of the Government of India aims to provide pucca housing units to eligible rural households across the country. The State of Maharashtra implements PMAY-G in coordination with district administrations. Each year the State Cabinet allocates the matching share + supplementary state contribution per district.

The District Magistrate, Beed, submitted a proposal dated 22 March 2024 seeking allocation of **INR 84 crore** for FY 2024-25 to cover an estimated 5,200 eligible households across 14 talukas of the district, identified through the SECC 2011 data with on-ground verification.

After review by the Finance Department and concurrence by the GAD Cabinet Sub-Committee on 8 May 2024, the Government has decided to approve the allocation as follows.

---

## Resolution

The Government hereby resolves:

(i) An allocation of **INR 84,00,00,000 (Indian Rupees Eighty-Four Crore)** is hereby sanctioned for the implementation of PMAY-G in Beed district for FY 2024-25.

(ii) The funds shall be released to the District Magistrate, Beed, in three (3) tranches:
   - Tranche 1: INR 33.6 crore (40%) — by 30 June 2024.
   - Tranche 2: INR 33.6 crore (40%) — by 30 September 2024.
   - Tranche 3: INR 16.8 crore (20%) — by 31 December 2024 (subject to utilization report).

(iii) The benefit shall reach approximately **5,200 eligible households** in Beed district, at an average per-household allocation of approximately INR 1.62 lakh (including the state supplementary).

(iv) The District Magistrate shall ensure:
   - Beneficiary selection per SECC 2011 with grievance-redressal protocol;
   - Geo-tagging of construction sites;
   - Quarterly utilisation certificate to GAD + Finance;
   - Aadhaar-linked direct benefit transfer (DBT).

(v) The Director, Housing (Rural), shall monitor implementation and report monthly to the Principal Secretary, GAD.
