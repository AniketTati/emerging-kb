# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA — GAD
### GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION

**GR No.:** GAD-PEN-2024/CR-2814/PEN-9
**Date:** 20 November 2024

**Subject:** Pension Revision for State Government Employees — Implementation of 8th Pay Commission recommendations.

## Resolution

(i) Implementation of the 8th Pay Commission (8CPC) recommendations for State Government employees, effective 1 January 2025.

(ii) Fitment factor: **2.86** (applied to the pre-revised basic pay).

(iii) Pension revision:
   - Pre-2024 pensioners: revised pension = (pre-revised basic pay × 2.86) → rounded to nearest INR 100.
   - Family pension: 30% of revised basic.
   - Minimum pension: INR 12,500 per month (revised from INR 9,000).
   - Maximum pension: INR 1,42,500 per month.

(iv) Dearness Allowance on revised pension: as per quarterly notifications.

(v) Arrears period: 1 January 2025 - 31 March 2025 → payable in two instalments (April + June 2025).

(vi) Total fiscal impact: ~INR 7,200 crore per annum (state outlay).

(vii) Pensioner verification protocol:
   - Life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan) — annually via Aadhaar or biometric at any pension-disbursing branch.
   - Bank account + Aadhaar linkage mandatory.

(viii) Special category pensioners (war widows, gallantry award holders): additional uplift of 25% per existing notifications.

(ix) Special provision for pre-2006 pensioners: deemed minimum equivalent for purposes of pension parity.
