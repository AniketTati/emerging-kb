# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA — GAD
### GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION

**GR No.:** GAD-REL-2025/CR-1648/REL-7
**Date:** 15 August 2025

**Subject:** Monsoon Relief Package — affected districts FY 2025-26.

## Resolution

(i) The 2025 monsoon has been substantially above normal (220% of long-period average in many western Maharashtra + Marathwada districts), with significant agricultural + property damage.

(ii) Relief package for affected districts:

| District | Allocation (INR crore) | Affected households (est.) |
|---|---|---|
| Pune | 138 | 24,200 |
| Sangli | 124 | 21,800 |
| Kolhapur | 142 | 24,800 |
| Beed | 108 | 18,400 |
| Jalna | 88 | 14,200 |
| Latur | 102 | 17,600 |
| Aurangabad | 116 | 19,200 |
| Osmanabad | 86 | 14,600 |
| **Total** | **904** | **154,800** |

(iii) Relief components:
   - **Cash relief:** INR 12,000 per house damaged (kachha); INR 25,000 per house damaged (pucca).
   - **Crop compensation:** INR 18,000 per hectare for kharif crop loss (paddy, sugarcane, soybean).
   - **Cattle compensation:** INR 30,000 per cow/buffalo lost.
   - **Emergency food supply:** PDS distribution to all flood-affected households for 3 months.

(iv) Disbursement: via DBT to affected households; non-DBT-linked households via village-level disbursement centers.

(v) Verification: by Tehsildars + circle officers within 30 days of receipt of village-level lists.

(vi) Inter-departmental coordination: Disaster Management Authority + Agriculture Department + Revenue Department + Animal Husbandry Department.

(vii) **Monitoring:** Weekly status review by Cabinet Sub-Committee on Disaster Management.

(viii) **Long-term:** Resilience-building plan to be drafted within 6 months covering: improved flood-warning systems, climate-resilient crop diversification, micro-irrigation revival.
