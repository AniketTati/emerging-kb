## RTI Application — Original Query

> "I, Shri Ramesh Solanki, hereby request the following information under §6 of the Right to Information Act, 2005:
>
> 1. The list of all bidders who submitted bids for tender CIDCO/T-2025/NV-RR-014 (Road resurfacing Navi Mumbai Sector 14) — including:
>    - Company name + registered address;
>    - Bid value (financial);
>    - Whether technically qualified or disqualified;
>    - For the L1 (lowest) bidder ABC Construction Pvt Ltd: copy of their blacklist disclosure (if any) per the eligibility criteria.
>
> 2. Specifically: Has ABC Construction Pvt Ltd ever been blacklisted by any State or Central Government department in the last 5 years? If yes, provide details.
>
> 3. The reason for ABC Construction's L1 selection notwithstanding the blacklist criteria."

## Response from Public Information Officer (PIO), CIDCO

(i) **Information sought relates to a live tender** and is therefore subject to §8 + §9 of the RTI Act. CIDCO has nevertheless decided to disclose the following:

(ii) **List of bidders** for tender CIDCO/T-2025/NV-RR-014:

| Bidder | Bid (INR) |
|---|---|
| ABC Construction Pvt Ltd | 4.28 crore |
| Sterling Infra Pvt Ltd | 5.14 crore |
| Patel Roads Maharashtra | 5.28 crore |
| Konkan Infra Pvt Ltd | 5.42 crore |
| Maha Roads Pvt Ltd | 5.58 crore |
| Pune Civic Works | 5.84 crore |
| Bharat Highways | 6.12 crore |

(iii) **Regarding ABC Construction Pvt Ltd's blacklist history:**

> The applicant's specific assertion regarding a 2022 PWD Maharashtra blacklist of ABC Construction Pvt Ltd has been brought to CIDCO's attention through this RTI. CIDCO is **investigating** this matter immediately.
>
> CIDCO has placed the L1 award announcement on hold pending the investigation.

(iv) **Reason for L1 selection:**

The original L1 selection was based on the bidder's self-declared eligibility statement (which represented that no blacklist exists within the past 5 years). If the applicant's assertion regarding the 2022 PWD blacklist is verified, this will be deemed a misrepresentation under the tender terms, and disqualification of the L1 bid would follow.

(v) **Update timeline:** CIDCO commits to communicate the outcome of the investigation to the applicant within 30 days. Subsequent decisions on the tender will be transparent + documented.

## Right of Appeal

Per §19 of the RTI Act, the applicant may file a first appeal with the First Appellate Authority (FAA), CIDCO, within 30 days if not satisfied with this response.

## Subsequent Outcome (record-only addition)

The investigation triggered by this RTI led to the disqualification of ABC Construction Pvt Ltd from the tender (per tender-001-cidco-bid-decision dated 12 June 2025). The work was re-awarded to L2 Sterling Infra Pvt Ltd at INR 5.14 crore. This RTI applicant's contribution to transparency was formally acknowledged.
