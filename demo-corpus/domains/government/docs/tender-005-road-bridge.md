# DM BEED — TENDER NOTICE — ROAD BRIDGE RECONSTRUCTION

**Tender Reference:** BEED/T-2025/BRG-003
**Date:** 4 September 2025
**Last date:** 4 October 2025 17:00 IST

## Scope

Reconstruction of the road bridge across the Manjara River on the Beed-Aurangabad highway. The original 1986-built bridge was severely damaged in the 2025 monsoon (flash floods in August 2025) and has been declared structurally unsafe.

- Bridge length: 220m.
- Carriageway: 12m (4-lane).
- Foundation: Pile-driven (depth to bedrock ~14m).
- Superstructure: pre-stressed concrete box girders.
- Approach roads: 480m on each side (240m × 2).

## Estimated Cost

INR 28.4 crore (excluding GST).

## EMD

INR 28.4 lakh.

## Eligibility

- Class-1A contractor.
- Minimum 3 bridge-construction projects completed in last 7 years.
- At least one bridge ≥200m length.
- Aggregate turnover (last 3 FY) ≥ INR 80 crore.

## Funding

- 60% from State Disaster Response Fund (per gr-008-monsoon-relief-2025).
- 40% from State PWD budget.

## Special Requirements

- Climate-resilience certification (per state water-resources GR-003-water-conservation-marathwada).
- Independent third-party quality assurance (TPQA) mandatory.
- Worker safety protocols (PPE, harness, fall protection) per MIDC guidelines.

## Timeline

- Award: 30 days.
- Execution: 18 months.
