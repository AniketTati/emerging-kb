# 7/12 Extract — Survey No. 44/1 (DISPUTED — Under Legal Proceedings)

**Village:** Pangri | **Tehsil:** Beed
**Date of extract:** 22 June 2025

> ⚠ **DISPUTED HOLDING — see FIR-003-beed-land-dispute-scanned + ongoing civil proceedings at Bombay HC.**

## Land Details

- **Area:** 6.84 hectares
- **Class:** Bagayat (well-irrigated) — high-value agricultural land
- **Use:** Cotton (kharif), Wheat (rabi)
- **Land revenue:** INR 1,820/year

## Disputed Ownership Claim

Two parties claim ownership of Survey No. 44/1:

### Claimant A: Shri Pravin Sambhaji Lokhande

- Holds an officially-registered 7/12 since 2003.
- Inherited from his uncle Sambhaji Lokhande (died 2002, no children).
- Possession of land claimed since 2003.

### Claimant B: Shri Datta Anandrao Mane

- Claims he was the rightful heir via a maternal-grand-uncle chain (his maternal grandfather, Hari Mane, was Sambhaji Lokhande's first cousin).
- Holds a will dated 2001 allegedly executed by Sambhaji Lokhande in his favour. Authenticity of will under dispute.

## Current Legal Status

- **Civil suit:** PCB-CIV-2025/0188 filed by Datta Mane in Bombay High Court, January 2025.
- **Police complaint:** FIR-003 (see fir-003-beed-land-dispute-scanned) filed against Datta Mane by Pravin Lokhande in July 2025, alleging extortion and threats.
- **Status quo order:** Bombay HC granted status quo order on 12 March 2025 — neither party to alienate, alter, or develop the land pending decision.

## Possession + Cultivation (Current)

- Pravin Lokhande continues to cultivate the land in good faith pending resolution.
- Tehsildar advised both parties of status-quo order; police presence verified during cropping cycles.

## Special Entries

(i) This 7/12 extract is issued as a current record of registered ownership (Pravin Lokhande) without prejudice to the ongoing legal proceedings.

(ii) Any prospective buyer or financier must be aware of the dispute; bank financing currently held in abeyance.
