# OFFICE OF THE DISTRICT MAGISTRATE, BEED
## TENDER NOTICE — WATER SUPPLY PIPELINE PROJECT

**Tender Reference:** BEED/T-2025/WS-008
**Date of issue:** 30 April 2025
**Last date for bid submission:** 4 June 2025 17:00 IST

## 1. Scope

Construction of a 24-km gravity-fed water-supply pipeline from the Bindusara Reservoir to 4 villages in Beed taluka (estimated 32,000 beneficiaries).

## 2. Estimated Cost

INR 18.6 crore (excluding GST).

## 3. Scope Components

- 24 km of 250mm DI pipeline (gravity-fed).
- 4 village reservoirs (concrete tanks, 100 KL each).
- 12 distribution branch lines (50mm HDPE).
- Pumping station upgrades at Bindusara intake.
- Electromechanical commissioning + 12-month O&M.

## 4. EMD

INR 18.6 lakh (1% of estimated cost).

## 5. Eligibility

- Class-1 contractor.
- Minimum 2 similar water-supply works completed in last 5 years.
- Aggregate turnover (last 3 FY) ≥ INR 40 crore.
- Valid PWD + JJM (Jal Jeevan Mission) registration.

## 6. Award

Two-bid system. L1 (lowest qualifying bid) to be awarded.

## 7. Award Timeline

- Award: within 30 days of bid opening.
- Execution: 18 months from work commencement.

## 8. Funding

50% from Jal Jeevan Mission (Central), 50% from state water resources budget.
