## Special Entries — Tribal Land Restrictions

(i) **Sale restriction:** Per MLR Code §36A, tribal-allotted land may not be sold to non-tribal persons without explicit prior Government permission. Any sale to a non-tribal in violation of this is void ab initio.

(ii) **Lease restriction:** Lease to non-tribal limited to 3 years; renewal requires DM approval.

(iii) **Mortgage:** May be mortgaged only to Government-recognized financial institutions (e.g., District Cooperative Bank, NABARD-affiliated lenders).

(iv) **Cultivation:** Owner-cultivator; no tenants.
