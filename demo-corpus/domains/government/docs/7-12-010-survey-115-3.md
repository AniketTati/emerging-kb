## Special Entries

(i) **Land reform origin:** This holding is one of approximately 4,800 tenancy-to-ownership conversions in Beed district under the 1961-62 ceiling exercise.

(ii) **Sale restriction:** Per the 1948 Tenancy Act §43-B, sale to non-cultivator (including absentee landlord patterns) is prohibited. Sale to fellow cultivator permitted with Tehsildar approval.

(iii) **Subdivision restriction:** Subdivision below 0.40 hectares is not permitted under the Maharashtra Prevention of Fragmentation and Consolidation of Holdings Act, 1947.

(iv) No outstanding loans or encumbrances.

(v) Family lives on the land; owner-cultivator.
