# CIDCO — TENDER NOTICE — AFFORDABLE HOUSING BLOCK CONSTRUCTION

**Tender Reference:** CIDCO/T-2025/AH-024
**Date:** 8 July 2025
**Last date for bid submission:** 14 August 2025 17:00 IST

## Scope

Construction of an affordable-housing tower in CIDCO Navi Mumbai sector 22 — Block H — comprising:
- 1 tower (G+12), 224 units total.
- Unit types: EWS (24 units of 280 sq ft), LIG (96 units of 420 sq ft), MIG-1 (84 units of 560 sq ft), MIG-2 (20 units of 720 sq ft).
- Amenities: lifts (4), parking (2-level basement), STP, water harvesting, solar lighting in common areas, community hall.

## Estimated Cost

INR 38.4 crore (excluding GST).

## EMD

INR 38.4 lakh.

## Eligibility

- Class-1A contractor.
- Minimum 5 similar high-rise housing projects (G+10 or above) completed in last 7 years.
- Aggregate turnover (last 3 FY) ≥ INR 250 crore.
- No blacklist in past 5 years (Maharashtra or any State/Central body).

## Award

L1 (lowest qualifying bid) to be awarded.

## Timeline

- Award: 30 days.
- Execution: 24 months from work commencement.

## Compliance

- RERA registration mandatory.
- All sub-contractors to be on CIDCO approved-list.
- Workers' welfare provisions per State Labour Department guidelines.
