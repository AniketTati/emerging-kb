# CABINET MEETING MINUTES — WOMEN EMPOWERMENT SCHEME APPROVAL

**Date:** 14 May 2025
**Chair:** Hon'ble CM

## Agenda: Maharashtra Women Empowerment Scheme — revised eligibility + scope

### Discussion

The Women & Child Development Minister presented the proposal. Key points:

- Existing scheme reached 84 lakh women in FY 2024-25; cabinet-approved budget INR 14,800 crore.
- Industry-coalition feedback (FICCI Ladies Organisation, MAVIM) on need for:
  - **Higher income ceiling** (existing INR 2 lakh → INR 2.5 lakh).
  - **Skill+entrepreneurship integration** (existing scheme purely cash-transfer).
  - **Standalone bank accounts** mandatory (existing scheme accepted joint accounts).

CM emphasized that the scheme must be free from political-discretion abuse — strict Aadhaar + eligibility-criteria adherence.

The Finance Minister noted that the proposed budget of INR 16,420 crore (up ~11%) was achievable given FY 2025-26 revenue projections.

### Decisions

(i) **Income ceiling** raised from INR 2 lakh to **INR 2.5 lakh** annual household income.

(ii) **Monthly cash transfer:** Continued at INR 1,500/month (per the prevailing scheme rate).

(iii) **Skill + entrepreneurship overlay:** Skill training fully sponsored; micro-enterprise loan up to INR 5 lakh at 4% effective rate.

(iv) **Health:** Free annual check-up at empaneled hospital; reproductive-health services covered.

(v) **Girl-child education:** Class 9-12 INR 12,000/yr; UG/PG INR 24,000/yr scholarship.

(vi) **Account requirement:** Women must hold **individual bank accounts** (PMJDY or otherwise). Joint accounts with husband NOT acceptable.

(vii) **Total budget:** INR 16,420 crore for FY 2025-26 — largest single welfare scheme.

(viii) **Effective date:** 1 June 2025.

(ix) **GR to be issued** by 20 May 2025. (Subsequently issued as gr-011-women-empowerment-scheme.)

### Action Items

1. GAD to issue GR.
2. State WCD to coordinate with PMJDY centres to onboard women without bank accounts.
3. Grievance helpline to be activated by 1 June 2025.
