# GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA
## General Administration Department
### CORRIGENDUM TO GOVERNMENT RESOLUTION (शुद्धिपत्र)

**Mantralaya, Mumbai 400032**

**Corrigendum No.:** GAD-PMAY-2024/CR-1832/POL-7-CORR-1
**Date:** 22 August 2024

**Subject:** Revision of allocation under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin) for Beed district, FY 2024-25.

**Reference:** Government Resolution No. GAD-PMAY-2024/CR-1832/POL-7 dated 15 May 2024.

---

## Background

Subsequent to the issue of the original Government Resolution dated 15 May 2024 sanctioning an allocation of INR 84 crore for PMAY-G implementation in Beed district, the following developments have necessitated a revision:

(i) Re-verification of the SECC 2011 + on-ground verification data conducted by the Office of the District Magistrate, Beed, in the period 1 June 2024 - 12 August 2024, has reduced the eligible-household count from the original estimate of **5,200** to a verified figure of **3,840 households**.

(ii) The verification exercise identified:
   - 740 households whose ownership of existing pucca housing was subsequently confirmed (no longer eligible);
   - 420 households that had migrated out of the district (no longer eligible);
   - 200 households whose Aadhaar-linkage workflow exposed duplicate listings (consolidated to existing entries).

(iii) The Finance Department, on 18 August 2024, advised that the original allocation should be revised downward consistent with the verified beneficiary count.

(iv) The Cabinet Sub-Committee on Welfare Schemes (meeting of 21 August 2024) has endorsed the revised allocation.

---

## Corrigendum

In supersession of the allocation announced in the original GR dated 15 May 2024 (which sanctioned INR 84 crore for 5,200 estimated households), the Government hereby orders:

(i) The revised allocation under PMAY-G for Beed district for FY 2024-25 is hereby fixed at **INR 62,20,00,000 (Indian Rupees Sixty-Two Crore Twenty Lakh)**.

(ii) This revised allocation shall cover the verified **3,840 eligible households**, at an average per-household amount of approximately INR 1.62 lakh (unchanged from the original GR).

(iii) The tranche release schedule is correspondingly revised as follows:

| Tranche | Original (INR crore) | Revised (INR crore) | Date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tranche 1 | 33.6 | 24.88 | by 30 June 2024 — already released |
| Tranche 2 | 33.6 | 24.88 | by 30 September 2024 |
| Tranche 3 | 16.8 | 12.44 | by 31 December 2024 (subject to UC) |
| **Total** | **84.00** | **62.20** | — |

(iv) Tranche 1 (INR 33.6 crore) was released to the District Magistrate, Beed, on 28 June 2024, before this revision. The District Magistrate is directed to:
   - Carry over the un-utilised portion of Tranche 1 (~INR 8.72 crore) into Tranche 2 disbursement against the revised beneficiary list, OR
   - Refund the excess to the State Treasury, at the discretion of the District Magistrate after consultation with the Finance Department.

(v) All other provisions of the original GR remain unchanged.

---

## Effect

This Corrigendum is effective from the date of issue (22 August 2024) and is to be read along with the original GR dated 15 May 2024.

**The corrigendum supersedes the allocation amount stated in the original GR.** The current operative allocation is **INR 62.20 crore (not INR 84 crore)**.
