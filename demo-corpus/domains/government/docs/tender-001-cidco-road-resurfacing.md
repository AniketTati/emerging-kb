# CITY AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION OF MAHARASHTRA LIMITED (CIDCO)
## TENDER NOTICE — ROAD RESURFACING WORK

**Tender Reference:** CIDCO/T-2025/NV-RR-014
**Date of issue:** 20 March 2025
**Last date for bid submission:** 25 April 2025 17:00 IST
**Pre-bid meeting:** 5 April 2025 11:00 IST, CIDCO HQ Nariman Point

---

## 1. Scope of Work

Resurfacing of arterial road network in **Navi Mumbai Sector 14** comprising:

- Length: 8.4 km of arterial roads.
- Surface area: ~84,000 sq m.
- Specifications: 50mm DBM (Dense Bituminous Macadam) base + 40mm BC (Bituminous Concrete) wearing course.
- Includes: drainage rehabilitation, road markings, lane reflectors, junction reconfigurations.

## 2. Estimated Cost

INR 5.20 crore (excluding GST).

## 3. Earnest Money Deposit (EMD)

INR 5.20 lakh (1% of estimated cost), to be deposited via DD payable to CIDCO Ltd.

## 4. Eligibility

- Class-1 contractor (Maharashtra PWD or equivalent).
- Minimum 3 similar-scope works completed in last 5 years.
- Aggregate turnover (last 3 financial years) ≥ INR 12 crore.
- Valid GST registration + PAN.
- No blacklist by any State/Central Government department in last 5 years.

## 5. Bid Submission

- Two-bid system: Technical Bid + Financial Bid.
- Online submission via Maharashtra eTendering portal.
- Hard-copy submission also accepted at CIDCO HQ Reception by 17:00 on the last date.

## 6. Evaluation Criteria

- Technical evaluation: pass/fail (eligibility verification).
- Financial evaluation: **Lowest qualifying bid (L1) to be awarded the work.**

## 7. Pre-bid Queries

Queries to be submitted via email to procurement@cidco.example by 1 April 2025.

## 8. Bid Validity

180 days from the date of bid opening.

## 9. Award + Execution Timeline

- Award: within 30 days of bid opening.
- Execution: 90 days from work commencement order.
