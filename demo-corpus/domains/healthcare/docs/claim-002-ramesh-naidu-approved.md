## Claim Reference

- **Claim ID:** SH-CLM-2025-0428193
- **Original decision:** rejected on 10 June 2025 (see claim-001-ramesh-naidu-rejected).
- **Appeal filed:** 24 June 2025 by Mr. Ramesh Naidu's family through Manipal Hospital TPA desk.

## Documents Reviewed on Appeal

1. Emergency Department admission notes (28-May-2025) confirming STEMI presentation.
2. Cath lab + PCI procedure notes (Dr. P. Suresh).
3. Manipal billing team's certification that emergency pre-auth attempt was made at 17:20 on 28-May (system glitch documented) and re-submitted on 29-May morning.
4. Policy holder's clarification on tobacco use — patient confirms occasional smoking was disclosed on policy application but accept that quantification (25-pack-years) was not provided in detail. Adjudicator found this disclosure adequate per IRDAI guidelines on disclosure standards.

## Decision

The Claims Review Committee, after careful review of the appeal documentation, has determined that:

1. The admission qualifies as a **bona-fide medical emergency** (STEMI with active myocardial infarction) — pre-authorization rules permit retroactive submission within 24 hours for emergency cases.
2. **Pre-existing condition exclusion is NOT applicable** — tobacco history was disclosed in spirit on policy application; pre-existing-condition criteria require formally diagnosed conditions, not lifestyle risk factors.
3. The treatment was medically necessary, with appropriate clinical protocol (primary PCI for STEMI within evidence-based timeframe).
