## Reason for Rejection

> **"This claim is rejected on grounds of pre-existing condition exclusion under Section 4(b) of the policy.
>
> Specifically: Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) related to underlying diabetes mellitus (15+ years) and longstanding hypertension. Both conditions and their consequences (including CKD progression and the need for renal replacement therapy) are excluded from coverage for the first 36 months of the policy.
>
> The current policy began on 1-Apr-2023, providing 30 months of cover at the time of admission (2-Oct-2025) — under the 36-month threshold for pre-existing condition waiver."**

## Adjudicator's Note

Patient's medical history clearly documents long-standing diabetes (since 2010) and hypertension (since 2011) — both well in excess of the policy inception date. CKD stage 5 with the need for renal replacement therapy is a foreseeable, longstanding-disease-related complication.
