### 2. AV Fistula Creation

For management of renal anemia.

## Risks Discussed

- **Dialysis:** Hypotension during/after, line infections (line-related sepsis), muscle cramps, fatigue, increased cardiovascular risk over time.
- **AV fistula creation:** Infection, non-maturation requiring revision, distal ischemia (rare), bleeding.
- **EPO:** Hypertension, headache, very rarely thrombosis.

## Discussion of Alternatives

- Peritoneal dialysis (CAPD): discussed; not feasible given patient's living conditions.
- Renal transplantation: discussed as a longer-term option; eligibility workup to begin once stable on dialysis.
- Conservative management: discussed; not chosen given relatively young age and active life.

- I understand the importance of medication compliance, fluid restriction, and dietary modifications.
