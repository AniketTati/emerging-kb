## Reason for Rejection

> **"The claim is rejected at this stage pending submission of:
> (a) Pre-authorization request — your provider did not submit pre-auth before the procedure; emergency admission status to be clarified.
> (b) Smoking-status disclosure — clinical notes mention 25-pack-year tobacco history; this may be deemed a pre-existing risk factor not declared at policy inception.
> (c) Hospital itemized bill cross-referenced against IRDAI-mandated MoU schedule."**

## Adjudicator's Note

The patient was admitted via Emergency with documented STEMI; emergency pre-authorization can typically be requested within 24 hours of admission per IRDAI rules. **No pre-auth was submitted** for this admission until the discharge billing on 30 May.

Pre-existing condition exclusion is being evaluated; tobacco use is documented in clinical notes but was not declared on the policy application from 2024.
