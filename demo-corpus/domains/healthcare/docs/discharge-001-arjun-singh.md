## Reason for Admission

Newly-diagnosed atrial fibrillation with uncontrolled hypertension, admitted for telemetry monitoring + medication initiation.

## Hospital Course

**Day 1 (15-Sep):** Admission to telemetry. Started on Telmisartan 40 mg + Chlorthalidone 12.5 mg combination. Apixaban 5 mg BID initiated. Apixaban dose confirmed appropriate (no renal dose reduction needed). Patient remained in AF with rate 88-112 bpm.

**Day 2 (16-Sep):** Labs reviewed (CBC + lipid + thyroid + HbA1c). Thyroid function normal (excluded as AF cause). Lipid panel showed persistent dyslipidemia despite atorvastatin 10 mg — up-titrated to atorvastatin 20 mg ON. HbA1c 7.8% — endo opinion sought; metformin continued, sitagliptin 50 mg OD added.

**Day 3 (17-Sep):** Echocardiogram performed (see imaging-001-arjun-singh-echo):
- LV ejection fraction 58% (preserved).
- Mild LA enlargement.
- No structural valve disease.
- No thrombus visualized.

Bisoprolol 2.5 mg OD started for rate control. Heart rate began trending to 75-85 bpm.

**Day 4 (18-Sep):** Patient stable. Brief education sessions on AF, anticoagulation, and BP self-monitoring. BP improved to 136/84 mmHg.

**Day 5 (20-Sep):** Discharge-fit. BP 132/82 mmHg, HR 72 bpm (regular periods documented for the first time — possible spontaneous conversion). Discharged with discharge medications (see below).
