# DGMS INSPECTION REPORT — Noamundi Iron-Ore Block

**Issued by:** Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS)
**Inspector:** A. K. Singh, Inspector of Mines (Dhanbad Region)
**Report ID:** TSM-DGMS-2025-NOA-007
**Date of inspection:** 22-25 April 2025
**Mine:** Noamundi Iron-Ore Block (Lease ML-WS-12/2018; operator: Tata Steel Mining Operations)
**Triggering event:** Reportable Lost-Time Injury, 9 April 2025 (TSM-NOA-2025-INC-014; hydraulic hose failure on contractor drilling rig).
**Statutory authority:** Mines Act, 1952, §23; Mines Rules, 1955.

---

## 1. Background

This inspection was triggered by the reportable LTI on 9 April 2025 at the Noamundi block (incident-003-noamundi-vendor-equipment). The DGMS Inspector visited the site between 22 and 25 April 2025. The visit included:

- Walk-through inspection of the Phase-3 drilling area.
- Review of operator (Tata Steel Mining) records: shift logs, daily safety briefings, recent inspection records.
- Review of contractor (Mahalaxmi Drilling) records: rig maintenance logs, hose-replacement records, safety-training certificates.
- Interviews with: Naresh Soren (Safety Officer, Tata Steel), Manish Deshmukh (Chief Mining Engineer, Tata Steel), Vinod Kumar (Field Supervisor, Mahalaxmi Drilling).
- Physical inspection of the failed hose + the replacement rig.

## 2. Findings

### 2.1 Direct cause of incident

**Confirmed:** Failure of hydraulic hose at swaged-fitting junction. The hose itself was within service life; the fitting showed evidence of improper crimping with the wire-braid layer protruding. This is consistent with a non-OEM replacement or a damaged OEM fitting that was re-crimped improperly during a maintenance event in June 2024.

### 2.2 Contractor maintenance records — DEFICIENT

The contractor (Mahalaxmi Drilling) **cannot produce documentation** for the June 2024 hose replacement. The maintenance logbook for the rig (DM45 ROC, reg MGS-DR-2023-094) has entries for replacements but **no supplier invoices, batch numbers, or OEM authentication certificates attached for several 2024 replacement events**.

This is a **violation of Mines Rule 7(b)** which requires maintenance records to be auditable and traceable to OEM specifications for safety-critical equipment.

### 2.3 Pre-start safety briefing — DEFICIENT

The pre-start briefing on 9 April 2025 (per Mahalaxmi Drilling's daily briefing log) covered general drilling safety but **did not explicitly cover hydraulic-system response procedures**. Industry standard practice is to brief on hydraulic-line-failure response at least weekly for active drilling operations.

This is **inadequate** under DGMS guidance circular DG/SAFETY/2022/14, which specifies daily briefings on the most relevant hazard for the current operation.

### 2.4 Operator (Tata Steel Mining) — partial compliance

- ✅ Reporting timeliness: Tata Steel reported the incident to DGMS within the statutory 24-hour window.
- ✅ Immediate response: First-aid + evacuation handled within published mine emergency-response procedures.
- ❌ Vendor management: Tata Steel's sub-contracting agreement with Mahalaxmi Drilling does NOT mandate OEM-only spares with auditable provenance for safety-critical equipment. This is a notable gap.
- ❌ Contractor oversight: Tata Steel's own audits of contractor maintenance practices have apparently not flagged the gaps noted in §2.2.

## 3. Statutory Findings and Directions

Per the Mines Act §23 and Mines Rules 1955:

1. **DGMS NOTICE OF DEFICIENCY (Notice No. DGMS/DHN/2025/0473)** issued to:
   - **Mahalaxmi Drilling & Geo-Services Pvt Ltd** — maintenance-record deficiencies (Rule 7(b)) and inadequate pre-start safety briefings.
   - **Tata Steel Mining Operations** — vendor-management deficiencies in safety-critical equipment procurement.

2. **Compliance period:** 60 days from receipt of this notice (by 24 June 2025).

3. **Re-inspection:** A DGMS re-inspection is scheduled for **last week of July 2025** to verify compliance with the directions below.

## 4. Directions

### To Mahalaxmi Drilling & Geo-Services:

(i) Reconstruct full hose-replacement records for the past 36 months, including supplier invoices, OEM batch numbers, and crimping-tool calibration records.
(ii) Replace any hose / fitting on safety-critical hydraulic systems lacking documented OEM provenance — within 30 days.
(iii) Submit revised pre-start safety briefing template covering hydraulic-system-failure response — within 14 days.
(iv) Train all drilling-crew personnel on the revised briefing — completion by 30 days; submit training-attendance records to DGMS.
(v) Apply face-shield + flame-retardant outer layer as standard PPE on hydraulic-rig operations.

### To Tata Steel Mining Operations:

(i) Amend the sub-contracting agreement (dated 12 March 2024) with Mahalaxmi Drilling to mandate OEM-only spares with auditable provenance for safety-critical equipment — submit amended agreement copy to DGMS within 60 days.
(ii) Establish a vendor-audit protocol with at least quarterly on-site audits of contractor maintenance practices.
(iii) Provide DGMS with a copy of the operator-side post-incident report (incident-003-noamundi-vendor-equipment) — ✅ already received during this inspection.
(iv) Re-train Tata Steel duty-supervisor cadre on contractor-equipment-failure response.

## 5. Penalties

No statutory penalty imposed at this stage. **DGMS reserves the right to impose penalty under §23A of the Mines Act if compliance is not demonstrated by the re-inspection date.**

## 6. Acknowledgement

Both parties (Tata Steel Mining and Mahalaxmi Drilling) have acknowledged receipt of this report and the deficiency notice; both have indicated commitment to compliance.
