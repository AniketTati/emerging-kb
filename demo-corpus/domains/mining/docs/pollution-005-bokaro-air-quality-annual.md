# ANNUAL AIR-QUALITY COMPLIANCE REPORT — BOKARO COAL BLOCK (CY 2024)

**Operator:** Tata Steel Mining Operations
**Site:** Bokaro Coal Block (lease ML-JH-14/2020)
**Reporting period:** Calendar Year 2024 (1 January 2024 - 31 December 2024)
**Submission:** 28 February 2025

## 1. Monitoring Network

8 in-lease + 5 ambient stations (per the EC for Bokaro coal block). Hourly sampling, daily aggregation, monthly + quarterly + annual reporting.

## 3. Methane Emissions (NEW reporting requirement, post-Oct 2025 incident)

Following the methane explosion incident at Bench 6 (incident-007-bokaro-methane-explosion, 2 Oct 2025), the DGMS and JSPCB have requested **annual methane emission reporting** starting CY 2025.

For CY 2024 (this report period, pre-incident), the following retrospective summary is provided:

- No formal methane monitoring at bench-face level during CY 2024.
- Mine-water + dewatering analysis suggests methane emissions of ~12,000-18,000 m³/yr (estimated from coal seam analysis; not measured).
- For CY 2025 onwards: continuous methane monitoring system to be deployed at all bench faces; annual methane balance to be reported.

## 4. Compliance

**Compliance: ✅ All NAAQS parameters within statutory limits for CY 2024.**

⚠ However, the methane events of October 2025 (post-reporting-period) have triggered a re-classification of the Bokaro seam from Gassy Category I to Category II. Subsequent quarterly reports will include enhanced methane reporting.
