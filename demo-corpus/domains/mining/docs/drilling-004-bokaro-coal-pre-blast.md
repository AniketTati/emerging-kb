# DRILLING REPORT — BOKARO COAL BLOCK (Pre-Blast Bore-Hole Survey)

**Operator:** Tata Steel Mining Operations
**Block:** Bokaro Coal Block (lease ML-JH-14/2020)
**Drilling type:** Pre-blast bore-hole survey + gas-monitoring sampling
**Report date:** 8 September 2025
**Drilled by:** In-house Tata Steel drilling crew, equipment GR-7 reverse-circulation rig

## 1. Scope

Pre-blast survey of Bench 4 (eastern face) for the **bench cut planned for the week of 22 September 2025**. Survey targeted:
- Geotechnical characterization of the overburden + interburden material;
- Pre-blast gas-monitoring boreholes (methane testing — required by DGMS guidance for the seam classified as gassy);
- Verification of seam thickness and dip prior to drilling-pattern design.

## 2. Boreholes

| Hole | Depth (m) | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| GR-001 | 42 | OB sandstone + interbedded shale; gas tested negative |
| GR-002 | 38 | OB sandstone; gas negative |
| GR-003 | 44 | OB sandstone; **gas detected (methane 0.8 % LEL)** |
| GR-004 | 41 | OB sandstone; gas detected (methane 1.2 % LEL) |
| GR-005 | 45 | OB sandstone; gas detected (methane 1.8 % LEL — flagged) |
| GR-006 | 43 | OB sandstone; gas negative |

## 3. Gas Monitoring — Key Finding

Holes GR-003, GR-004, and GR-005 (clustered on the SE corner of Bench 4) showed elevated methane gas readings: **0.8 % – 1.8 % LEL**. While these are below the statutory action thresholds (5 % LEL = lower explosive limit), they are **above background** and warrant proceeding with additional caution.

**Recommendation:** The bench-cut programme should incorporate continuous gas monitoring with handheld detectors at each drill-hole location prior to ignition. Additional drill-bench ventilation may be required.

## 4. Subsequent Events (added retrospectively)

> ⚠ **The 2 October 2025 methane explosion at Bokaro (incident-007-bokaro-methane-explosion)** occurred at a different bench (Bench 6, NW corner) — separate from the area covered by this pre-blast survey. However, the gas-monitoring findings in this report (Bench 4 SE corner elevated readings) **should have served as a corpus-wide signal that the Bokaro seam was more gassy than initially classified**. Post-incident review (October 2025) flagged the gap in cross-bench risk-extrapolation.

## 5. Recommendations

1. Bench 4 SE corner — continuous gas monitoring during all drilling and blasting operations.
2. Re-classify the Bokaro seam from "Gassy Category I" to **"Gassy Category II"** under DGMS classification.
3. Mandatory pre-shift gas surveys across all bench faces, not just newly-opened ones.
