# SAFETY INCIDENT — ELECTROCUTION NEAR-MISS

**Incident ID:** TSM-BKR-2025-INC-005
**Date / time:** 11 June 2025, 21:34 IST
**Location:** Bokaro Coal Block, electrical sub-station SS-2
**Severity:** **Near-Miss** (no injury sustained)

## 1. Description

An electrician was performing routine maintenance on a 3.3 kV switchgear panel in sub-station SS-2 when his tool slipped and momentarily contacted an exposed bus-bar. The arc flash energy was sufficient to cause superficial scorch marks on his glove but did not penetrate the PPE. The electrician was not injured but the incident triggered immediate sub-station shutdown.

## 2. Personnel

- Electrician: Vinay Mahato, age 45, Tata Steel direct employee.

## 3. Immediate Cause

- The 3.3 kV switchgear had been isolated from the upstream feeder but the **bus-bar was not earthed** prior to commencing maintenance work.

## 4. Root Cause

(i) **Earthing procedure not followed** — lockout-tagout was applied for the upstream isolation, but the additional step of applying earthing clamps to the bus-bar was skipped.
(ii) **Work permit deficiency** — the electrical work permit (issued by the electrical supervisor) did not specifically itemize the bus-bar earthing step.
(iii) **Time pressure** — the technician was reportedly under pressure to complete the work before the shift change; haste may have contributed.

## 5. Actions Taken

- Bus-bar earthing now mandatory in all electrical work permit templates (revised template effective 15 Jun 2025).
- All electrical technicians re-trained on the dead/earthed/isolated procedure (3-day refresher, 16-18 Jun 2025).
- Sub-station SS-2 audit conducted by external auditor (BSAS) — completed 25 Jun 2025; minor additional findings on labeling.

## 6. Status

Closed (3 Jul 2025). No DGMS notification (no injury); internal LTIF tracking only.
