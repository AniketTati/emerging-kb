# ANNUAL WATER DISCHARGE COMPLIANCE REPORT — PARSA COAL BLOCK

**Submission date:** 31 March 2025

> *Note: Although the title says "Jharkhand State Pollution Control Board" in the parties field, Parsa is in Chhattisgarh — this report is submitted to **Chhattisgarh Environment Conservation Board (CECB)**, not JSPCB.*

## 2. Discharge Quality

| Parameter | Annual mean | Pre-EC limit (mg/L) | Compliance |
|---|---|---|---|
| pH | 7.4 | 6-9 | ✅ |

| COD | 64 | 250 | ✅ |

| Iron | 0.6 | 3.0 | ✅ |

**Overall water-quality compliance: ✅ All parameters within limits.**

## 3. Zero-Discharge Status

Annual net discharge to surface water: ~300,000 KL — this is treated discharge to a controlled outlet for the local stream, not a contaminant release. The "zero-discharge" EC requirement refers to zero discharge of *contaminated* water; the treated discharge complies with that requirement.

## 4. Sedimentation Pond Status

The pond has been desilted twice during FY 2024-25 (per the standard 6-month cycle). Sediment volume removed: ~14,200 m³. Sediment was used for backfilling the inactive bench.

## 5. Compliance

**Compliance: ✅ Full compliance with all EC water-management conditions.**
