## 1. Scope

Initial exploration drilling programme for the Noamundi iron-ore block expansion zone (Block-B extension). Twelve (12) diamond-core holes drilled over the period **15 April – 30 July 2024**, total drilled length **2,418 metres**. Holes targeted the Banded Hematite-Quartzite (BHQ) ferruginous unit. Core recovery: 92.4 %.

## 2. Geology Summary

The Block-B extension overlies the Iron Ore Group of the Singhbhum craton. Stratigraphically the target unit is the Banded Hematite-Quartzite (BHQ) of the Tomka-Daitari belt, with the iron formation typically forming N-S striking lenses 60-180 m thick.

Drilling intersected the BHQ ferruginous unit in 11 of 12 holes; the 12th hole (BH-12) terminated in barren phyllite at 132 m without intersecting the target.

## 5. Limitations & Recommendations

1. The Inferred resource estimate is preliminary; further infill drilling (target 20 additional holes at 60 m spacing) is recommended to upgrade to the Indicated category.

2. Hole BH-12 (barren) suggests the BHQ unit may be lens-truncated on the eastern flank. A follow-up traverse of the east boundary is warranted.

3. The mean grade estimate of **58.2 % Fe** has a confidence interval of ±2.4 % given the limited sample density. Further drilling will materially tighten this.

4. SiO₂ content (12.4 %) is on the higher side; beneficiation testwork is recommended.
