# DRILLING REPORT — PARSA COAL BLOCK

**Operator:** Adani Enterprises Mining Division
**Block:** Parsa Coal Block (Hasdeo Arand coalfield, Chhattisgarh)
**Lease ref:** ML-CG-08/2019 (vintage Sep 2019)
**Report date:** 8 November 2024
**Prepared by:** CMPDI (Central Mine Planning and Design Institute) — Senior Geologist team

## 1. Scope

Exploration drilling for the Parsa coal block expansion area, **18 holes drilled** between 12 February 2024 and 30 October 2024 (cumulative depth: 5,212 m). Holes targeted seams Sm-IIIA, Sm-IIIB, and Sm-IV of the Lower Gondwana sequence.

## 2. Coal Seam Summary

| Seam | Mean thickness (m) | Cumulative tonnage (Mt) | Mean ash % | Mean calorific value (kcal/kg) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sm-IIIA | 4.6 | 88 | 22.4 | 4,920 |
| Sm-IIIB | 6.8 | 142 | 24.2 | 4,780 |
| Sm-IV | 3.8 | 64 | 28.6 | 4,420 |
| **Total** | — | **294 Mt** | — | — |

## 3. Drill-Hole Statistics

- Total holes: 18 (all successfully intersected at least one target seam).
- Mean hole depth: 290 m.
- Core recovery in coal seams: 88-94 %.
- 4 boreholes converted to permanent groundwater monitoring wells.

## 4. Resource Categorisation

- **Indicated:** 218 Mt across all 3 seams.
- **Inferred:** 76 Mt (peripheral / under-sampled zones).
- **Total:** 294 Mt.

## 5. Mining Implications

- Multi-seam open-cast operation feasible. Recommended stripping ratio: 2.4 BCM/ton overburden.
- Sm-IIIB is the highest-tonnage seam and should be prioritized.
- Sm-IV's higher ash content (28.6 %) suggests blending with Sm-IIIA at downstream stage to meet customer specs.

## 6. Recommendations

- Initiate mine-plan revision to incorporate the additional Indicated tonnage.
- Re-submit environmental clearance amendment if cumulative production target exceeds the current EC's 18 MTPA cap (see env-clearance-001-parsa-corrigendum).
