Aligned to the EC sanctioned 45 MTPA (per env-clearance-002-noamundi).

## 2. Mining Method

Open-cast operation. Three principal benches:
- Upper bench (Bench-1, Bench-2): waste removal + topsoil preservation.
- Middle benches (Bench-3, Bench-4, Bench-5): ore production.
- Lower benches (Bench-6+): high-grade central core (developed for years 3-7).

Total resource (per drilling-001-v3): 214 Mt.

- Indicated: 56 Mt — incorporated in years 6-15.
- Inferred: 26 Mt — peripheral; subject to upgrading via Phase-4 drilling.

## 5. Equipment Plan

- Excavators: 14× 200-ton class hydraulic shovels (existing fleet + 4 new).
- Haul trucks: 38× 100-ton CAT 777G + 6× 150-ton CAT 793F (for high-grade core).
- Drilling: 6× rotary blast-hole drills + 4× ultra-high-pressure surface miners.
- Crushers: 2× primary jaw + 4× secondary cone (existing).
- Conveyors: 12 km new conveyor for waste handling (under construction, target end-2026).

## 6. Waste Management

- Total OB to be removed: ~545 MBCM over 5 years.
- Backfill into mined-out area: 60-70% of waste.
- External dump capacity: planned for 200 MBCM peak storage.

> [DIAGRAM 5: Waste dump layout + reclamation plan — see PDF p. 42]

## 7. Manpower

Total planned employment: 4,800 (direct + contractor combined).
Local employment commitment: 80% unskilled, 60% skilled.

## 8. Safety Program

- Daily pre-shift safety briefing.
- Quarterly third-party audit (BSAS).
- Annual emergency drill.
- Continuous geotechnical monitoring (radar slope monitoring system being installed).

## 9. Environmental Compliance

Per env-clearance-002-noamundi conditions, with the additional commitments:
- Greenbelt: 25 % of lease area developed by end of plan period.
- Water reuse: 100% within lease.
- Air-quality: real-time PM monitoring at 12 stations.

## 10. Closure Plan

Closure plan submitted separately (ML-WS-12/2018-CP-2025). Key elements:
- Backfilling: minimum 80% of mined-out volume.
- Replanting + ecological restoration over 8 years post-closure.
- Hand-over of restored land to Govt of Jharkhand under standard protocol.
