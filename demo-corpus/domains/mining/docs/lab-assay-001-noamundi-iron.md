# IRON-ORE ASSAY REPORT — NOAMUNDI (32 core samples)

**Operator:** Tata Steel Mining Operations
**Block:** Noamundi Iron-Ore Block, Block-B extension
**Sample set:** 32 core samples (selected intervals from Phase-1, Phase-2, and Phase-3 boreholes; see drilling-001-v3 §3 for borehole list)
**Assay laboratory:** Tata Steel R&D, Jamshedpur (NABL-accredited)
**Report date:** 15 June 2025

## 1. Methodology

- ICP-OES for major elements (Fe, Mn, Ti, Al).
- ICP-MS for trace elements.
- XRF for cross-validation (3 samples per batch).
- Wet chemistry (titration) for Fe verification on the high-grade samples.

## 2. Major Element Summary (32 samples)

| Element | Mean (%) | Min | Max | Std Dev |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fe** | **63.2** | 57.8 | 65.4 | 1.8 |
| SiO₂ | 8.4 | 5.6 | 14.2 | 2.0 |
| Al₂O₃ | 1.4 | 0.8 | 2.4 | 0.4 |
| P | 0.062 | 0.038 | 0.094 | 0.012 |
| S | 0.012 | 0.004 | 0.028 | 0.006 |
| Mn | 0.32 | 0.18 | 0.54 | 0.08 |
| TiO₂ | 0.18 | 0.10 | 0.32 | 0.06 |
| CaO | 0.06 | 0.02 | 0.12 | 0.02 |
| MgO | 0.04 | 0.02 | 0.10 | 0.02 |
| LOI | 1.8 | 0.8 | 3.2 | 0.5 |

The mean Fe of **63.2 %** corroborates the v3 drilling-report estimate (62.8 % from the broader drilling dataset). The 32 core samples are deliberately selected from the high-grade central core; the global resource mean (62.8 %) is properly the weighted average across the entire deposit.

|---|---|

No deleterious-element values exceed limits for direct shipping ore.

## 4. Beneficiation Implications

The mean SiO₂ of 8.4% suggests that for premium pellet feed, a moderate beneficiation step (jigging + classification) will be needed to lift Fe content to >65%. The current direct-shipping ore (DSO) profile is suitable for blast-furnace feed without beneficiation.
