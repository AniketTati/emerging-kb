## **खनिज पट्टा-विलेख / MINING LEASE DEED**

**[Stamp paper, Government of Madhya Pradesh, Indian Rupees Two Hundred (Rs. 200/-)]**

**अनुदान-दिनांक / Date of Grant:** 15 August 1972 (15 अगस्त 1972)
**पट्टेदार / Lessor:** Government of Madhya Pradesh (at that time; subsequently Chhattisgarh on its formation in 2000)
**पट्टा-प्राप्तकर्ता / Lessee:** **Tata Iron and Steel Company Limited** (वर्तमान में Tata Steel Limited / now Tata Steel Limited)
**अवधि / Term:** 30 years from date of grant.

---

## **1. खनिज क्षेत्र / Lease Area**

Bailadila Iron-Ore Range — **Block 14, parts of Blocks 11 and 12.**
स्थान / Location: Dantewada District, then Madhya Pradesh (now Chhattisgarh, post-2000 reorganization).
कुल क्षेत्र / Total Area: **2,840 हेक्टेयर / 2,840 hectares** (per 1972 survey; later GIS surveys put this at 2,816 hectares — discrepancy noted).

## **2. खनिज प्रकार / Mineral**

लौह अयस्क / **Iron ore** — particularly the high-grade hematite ("blue dust") deposits characteristic of the Bailadila series.

## **3. रॉयल्टी / Royalty**

[1972 rate, since superseded multiple times by Central notifications]
On run-of-mine ore: **Rs. 4 per tonne** for grades >55% Fe; Rs. 2.50 for grades 45-55% Fe; Rs. 1.50 for grades below.

## **4. वार्षिक भू-भाड़ा / Annual Surface Rent**

**Rs. 8 प्रति एकर प्रति वर्ष / Rs. 8 per acre per annum** for forest land; non-forest at Rs. 5 per acre.

## **5. शर्तें / Conditions**

(i) उत्पादन के 2 वर्ष के भीतर रेल कनेक्शन / Rail connectivity to Bailadila within 2 years of lease commencement. [OCR confidence 0.7]

(ii) [Section partially illegible — interpreted as] Mandatory submission of half-yearly returns to the Director of Mines, Bhopal.

(iii) "जनजातीय कल्याण योगदान" / **Tribal Welfare Contribution** — clause inserted [in handwriting in margin]: "लीज़ी contributory fund maintained at not less than 0.5% of net profit per annum." [margin note OCR confidence 0.5]

(iv) सुरक्षा अधिनियम 1952 का पालन / Compliance with the Mines Act, 1952.

(v) [Section illegible — appears to reference forest clearance under whatever was the pre-1980 framework]

## **6. नवीकरण / Renewal**

One renewal of 20 years subject to compliance review. **Renewal granted in 2002** for an additional 20 years; expires 14 August 2022.

> *(Subsequent further renewal / consolidation since 2022 not documented in this scan; the lease as consolidated post-2022 sits under a separate file in Tata Steel Bailadila operations records.)*

## **7. State Recall Clause**

State Government retains right to recall on 12 months' notice.

## **Subsequent Amendments (rubber-stamped)**

(i) **State Reorganization 2000:** Lease transferred from MP to Chhattisgarh effective 1 November 2000. Stamped 14 February 2001.

(ii) **Royalty Revision 2007:** Royalty rate revised to ad-valorem 10% of sale value per MMDR Notification. Stamped 22 April 2007.
