# ENVIRONMENTAL CLEARANCE — PARSA COAL BLOCK — **CORRIGENDUM**

**Issued by:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India
**File reference:** F.No.J-11015/127/2021-IA.II(M) — Corrigendum
**Date:** 20 September 2024

**Subject:** Amendment to the Environmental Clearance dated 14 April 2022 for Parsa Coal Block

---

## 1. Background

Adani Enterprises Mining Division has applied for an enhancement of the production capacity sanctioned in the EC dated 14 April 2022.

The application is based on:
(i) Re-assessment of resource potential (per drilling-002 dated 8 Nov 2024 + supplementary geological mapping);
(ii) Demand projections for thermal coal in Eastern + Central India for the period 2025-2035;
(iii) Existing infrastructure capacity (rail loading + transport) which is sized for higher throughput than 12 MTPA;
(iv) Public hearing held on **18 July 2024** at Block Office, Bhanpuri (Sarguja); minutes recorded.

Following due process — including the public hearing, EIA review by the SEIAA Chhattisgarh, and re-assessment of the carrying capacity of the Hasdeo Arand area by Wildlife Institute of India — MoEFCC has approved the production-cap enhancement.

## 2. Amendment

The Environmental Clearance dated 14 April 2022, **§2 — Production Capacity Sanctioned**, is hereby amended.

The line:
> *"Maximum production capacity: 12 MTPA (twelve million tonnes per annum) of run-of-mine coal."*

is **substituted with**:

> ✅ **"Maximum production capacity: 18 MTPA (eighteen million tonnes per annum) of run-of-mine coal."**

Consequential amendment to the line:
> *"Annual overburden removal: ≤32 MBCM/yr at peak."*

substituted with:

> **"Annual overburden removal: ≤48 MBCM/yr at peak."**

## 3. Supplementary Conditions

In connection with the production-cap enhancement:

(i) **Wildlife corridor protection — strengthened:** Annual third-party audit by Wildlife Institute of India (vs the original biennial audit).

(ii) **Forest restoration fund — supplemented:** Additional INR 8 crore (over the original INR 12 crore = total INR 20 crore) deposited.

(iii) **Tribal welfare — supplemented:** Increased contribution to **INR 7 crore per annum** to the District Mineral Foundation (Sarguja) — earmarked for tribal-area development.

(iv) **Air-quality monitoring stations — increased:** From 6 to 10 stations within the lease + from 4 to 7 ambient stations.

(v) **Mine-water management:** Maximum extraction from local sources increased from 1,200 KLD to 1,800 KLD. Zero-discharge mandate retained.

(vi) **Quarterly compliance reports** — frequency unchanged, but reports now include detailed throughput metrics + compliance-with-cap verification.

(vii) **Trigger for review:** If any of the supplementary conditions are not met within 18 months of this corrigendum, this corrigendum may be **revoked** + the original 12 MTPA cap restored, subject to MoEFCC discretion + due process.

## 4. All Other Conditions

All other conditions of the original EC dated 14 April 2022 remain in force unchanged.

## 5. Validity

The amended EC (original + this corrigendum) is valid for the term of the mining lease (until 2049), subject to compliance.
