## 3. Material Change — Mean Grade Upgraded

The Phase-2 holes consistently returned **higher Fe grades** than the Phase-1 sampling suggested. Combining the full 19-hole intercept dataset:

| Item | v1 (12 holes) | **v2 (20 holes)** | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total tonnage (Inferred + Indicated) | 142 Mt | **186 Mt** | +44 Mt |
| **Mean grade (Fe)** | 58.2 % | **61.4 %** | **+3.2 %** |
| Mean SiO₂ | 12.4 % | 9.8 % | -2.6 % |
| Mean Al₂O₃ | 1.8 % | 1.6 % | -0.2 % |
| Mean P | 0.082 % | 0.072 % | -0.010 % |
| Resource depth range | 120-200 m | 110-205 m | broadened |

**Observation:** The grade upgrade reflects two factors: (a) the Phase-2 holes targeted the inferred high-grade central core (not just the perimeter sampled in Phase-1), and (b) the Phase-1 holes inadvertently included some marginal ore in the intercepts. Re-sampling of Phase-1 cores (selective re-assay of intervals adjacent to the originally-logged ones) corroborates the Phase-2 finding — true central-core Fe content is closer to **62 %**.

## 4. Updated Resource Classification

With the additional holes:
- **Indicated:** 124 Mt @ 61.4 % Fe (mean) — the central high-grade zone.
- **Inferred:** 62 Mt @ 58.6 % Fe — perimeter/peripheral zones.
- **Combined:** 186 Mt.

The east-boundary lens truncation noted in v1 §5 (around BH-12) is confirmed; the deposit terminates at approximately 642,460 E.

## 5. Recommendations

1. Proceed with the Indicated Resource for inclusion in the mine-plan submission.
2. Complete final 12 holes (Phase 3) at 30 m spacing in the central high-grade zone to upgrade to Measured category — recommended for the final mine-plan submission.
3. Beneficiation flowsheet study should target the lower-SiO₂ profile of the high-grade core.
