# WAGE SETTLEMENT — ADANI MINING ↔ COAL MINE WORKERS UNION (CHHATTISGARH)

**Period:** 1 June 2025 - 31 May 2028 (3-year settlement)
**Parties:** Adani Enterprises Mining Division (Management) and Coal Mine Workers Union (CMWU — Sarguja Chapter)
**Coverage:** ~3,200 unionized workers at Parsa Coal Block.
**Signed:** 15 May 2025

## 1. Wage Structure

### Year 1 (FY 2025-26):
- Basic pay: 9% increase across all grades.
- DA: Quarterly adjusted per CPI.
- HRA: 14% of basic.
- Performance-linked bonus: 1.5-2.0 month equivalent.

### Year 2 (FY 2026-27):
- Basic pay: 7% increase.
- Bonus floor raised to 1.8 month.

### Year 3 (FY 2027-28):
- Basic pay: 6.5% increase.
- Long-service bonus: introduced for >10 years of service.

## 2. Benefits

### A. Health
- Family floater health insurance: INR 10 lakh sum insured.
- Free annual health check-up at empaneled hospital.
- Special focus on respiratory health screening (annual chest X-ray + spirometry).

### B. Safety
- Enhanced PPE on Bench-3 area (post incident-009-parsa-dust-exceedance).
- Mandatory respirable-dust personal sampler for all drillers.
- Heat-stress allowance INR 100/shift during April-June.

### C. Leave
- Annual: 28 days.
- Personal emergency: 5 days.
- Maternity: 26 weeks; Paternity: 15 days.

### D. Special Conditions

(i) **Forest-tribal community engagement clause:** Adani commits to 70% local employment in unskilled positions; CMWU commits to facilitate community engagement on lease boundary issues.

(ii) **Wildlife corridor protection:** Union members participate in the annual third-party wildlife audit team.

(iii) **Tribal scholarship fund:** Adani contributes INR 1.2 crore/year to a tribal-area education fund (over and above the EC-mandated CSR commitment).

## 3. Workplace Safety Committee

Equal representation: 3 Adani + 3 CMWU. Monthly meetings. Authority to:
- Halt unsafe work in coordination with site safety officer.
- Investigate near-misses and incidents.
- Recommend changes to procedures + PPE.
- Escalate to Group Safety Committee + DGMS if unresolved.

## 4. Dispute Resolution

Bipartite → Tripartite (with State Labour Commissioner) → Industrial Tribunal.
