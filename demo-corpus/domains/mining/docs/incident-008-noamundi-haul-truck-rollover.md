# SAFETY INCIDENT — HAUL-TRUCK ROLLOVER

**Incident ID:** TSM-NOA-2025-INC-017
**Date / time:** 28 May 2025, 04:14 IST (pre-dawn shift)
**Location:** Noamundi Iron-Ore Block, haul road segment H-3 (between Bench-4 and primary crusher)
**Severity:** Reportable injury (driver minor injury — Lost-Time Injury LTI)

## 1. Description

A 100-ton haul truck (CAT 777G, Tata Steel registration TSM-HT-103) **rolled onto its side** while negotiating the H-3 haul road curve at approximately 04:14 IST. The driver sustained a fractured wrist and bruising but was wearing his seatbelt and was protected by the truck's ROPS cab.

## 2. Personnel

- Driver: Ashok Hembrom, age 38, Tata Steel direct employee.
- No passengers; no nearby personnel.

## 3. Damage

- CAT 777G truck: Significant damage to RHS cab, mirror assembly, tipping platform hinge. **Repair estimate: INR 22 lakh**, ~21-day downtime.
- Haul road shoulder: Minor erosion + load (~80 tons of iron ore spilled).

## 4. Immediate Cause

- Driver fatigue at end of 8-hour pre-dawn shift.
- Loaded truck (carrying ~85 tons) entered the haul-road curve at 28 km/h — at the upper end of the safe-speed envelope (recommended ≤25 km/h for loaded condition on this curve).

## 5. Contributory Factors

(i) Haul-road curve angle and super-elevation marginal for loaded condition (the road geometry was designed before the current generation of 100-ton trucks; redesign was on the medium-term capex plan but not yet executed).
(ii) Pre-dawn shift fatigue — known factor in mining transportation incidents.
(iii) Driver's last full break was 4.5 hours prior to incident.

## 6. Actions Taken

- Driver provided medical care + family compensation activated (LTI compensation per Workmen's Compensation Act).
- Haul-road curve at H-3 redesigned (super-elevation increased; speed limit reduced to 18 km/h for loaded trucks) — completed 12 Jun 2025.
- Driver-fatigue monitoring system (driver-attention camera + 4-hour mandatory break) being installed across all haul trucks — rollout completed 30 Jun 2025.
- Pre-dawn shift rotation policy reviewed (shift-handover at 04:00 instead of 04:00 alternation reduces fatigue overlap).

## 7. Status

Closed (15 Jul 2025). DGMS notified within 24 hours (LTI requirement). No further DGMS action.
