# MINING LEASE DEED — RAJPURA-DARIBA ZINC-LEAD PROSPECT

**Lease ref:** ML-RJ-04/2022 (Prospecting + Mining Composite Lease)
**Granted by:** Government of Rajasthan, Department of Mines & Geology
**Granted to:** Adani Enterprises Mining Division
**Effective date:** 30 November 2022 — term of **25 years** (composite prospecting + mining)

## 1. Lease Area
- **Block:** Rajpura-Dariba zinc-lead prospect.
- **Total area:** 488 hectares.
- **Located in:** Rajsamand District, Rajasthan.

## 2. Mineral Authorized
Zinc, lead, silver, copper (volcanogenic massive-sulphide deposit).

## 3. Royalty Schedule
| Mineral | Rate |
|---|---|
| Zinc concentrate | 8% of LME-linked sale price |
| Lead concentrate | 7% of LME-linked sale price |
| Silver | 5% of LME-linked sale price |
| Copper concentrate | 4% of LME-linked sale price |

## 4. Production Cap (per current EC)
Prospecting phase + 5 MTPA mining (env-clearance-003 grants this).

## 5. Annual Surface Rent
INR 6 lakh per annum (semi-arid land).

## 6. Special Conditions

(i) Composite lease: first 36 months are prospecting-only; mining commences upon submission of approved mine plan.
(ii) **Water-resource compliance:** This is a water-scarce region. Mandatory annual water audit + commitment to use ≤80% of allocated quota.
(iii) Underground mining preferred (as endorsed by drilling-005 §4) — open-cast prohibited.

## 7. Renewal
One renewal of 20 years subject to compliance.
