# ENVIRONMENTAL CLEARANCE — PARSA COAL BLOCK (ORIGINAL)

**Issued by:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India
**File reference:** F.No.J-11015/127/2021-IA.II(M)
**Date:** 14 April 2022

**Granted to:** Adani Enterprises Mining Division

---

## 1. Project Description

Parsa Coal Block, Hasdeo Arand coalfield, Sarguja District, Chhattisgarh.

**Project type:** Open-cast coal mining + associated coal-handling infrastructure.
**Mining lease area:** 1,898 hectares.
**Lease ref:** ML-CG-08/2019.

## 2. Production Capacity Sanctioned

**Maximum production capacity:** **12 MTPA (twelve million tonnes per annum)** of run-of-mine coal.

This includes:
- Annual coal production: ≤12 MTPA.
- Annual overburden removal: ≤32 MBCM/yr at peak.

## 3. Project Lifespan

Project mine life: 26 years from commencement of production (estimated end-of-life: ~2049).

## 4. Conditions (Specific to this EC)

### A. General

(i) Compliance with the Forest (Conservation) Act 1980, the Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972, and the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act 1981 + Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act 1974.

(ii) Quarterly compliance reports to MoEFCC + Chhattisgarh State Environment Impact Assessment Authority (SEIAA) + Chhattisgarh Environment Conservation Board (CECB).

### B. Specific Conditions

(i) **Production cap as in §2 (12 MTPA)**.

(ii) **Forest restoration fund:** INR 12 crore deposited with the State Forest Department (Chhattisgarh) for off-site afforestation (acreage equivalent).

(iii) **Wildlife corridor:** Mining operations restricted to the western 1,628 hectares of the lease; the eastern 270 hectares (including the Hasdeo Arand wildlife corridor buffer) are off-limits.

(iv) **Tribal welfare:** INR 4 crore per annum to be deposited in the District Mineral Foundation (Sarguja) earmarked for tribal-area development.

(v) **Dust + air-quality:** PM10, PM2.5, SOx, NOx monitoring at minimum 6 stations within the lease boundary + 4 ambient stations at the lease periphery. Compliance with the National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS).

(vi) **Water:** Maximum extraction from local sources: 1,200 KLD. Discharge: zero discharge with full recycling. Settling ponds + sedimentation tanks sized for monsoon peak flows.

(vii) **Mine drainage:** Adequate dewatering capacity + treatment before any discharge.

(viii) **Noise:** Daytime peak noise ≤75 dB(A) at lease boundary; nighttime peak ≤70 dB(A). Blasting operations only between 09:00 and 17:00.

(ix) **Biodiversity:** Biodiversity Management Plan to be submitted within 18 months of project commencement.

### C. Reporting

Annual environmental compliance report (CER) to MoEFCC, CECB, and District Magistrate. Six-monthly progress reports.

## 5. Validity

This EC is valid for the term of the mining lease (until 2049) subject to compliance with the conditions above.

## 6. Right of Amendment

This EC may be amended via Corrigendum issued by MoEFCC upon application by the lessee for production-cap revisions, capacity expansions, or condition modifications — subject to public hearing + environmental impact reassessment.
