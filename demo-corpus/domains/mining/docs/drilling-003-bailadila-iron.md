# DRILLING REPORT — BAILADILA IRON-ORE BLOCK 14

**Operator:** Tata Steel Mining Operations (joint study with NMDC for shared corridor)
**Block:** Bailadila Iron-Ore Range, Block 14 (Dantewada district, Chhattisgarh)
**Report date:** 15 March 2025
**Prepared by:** CMPDI — Geologist team led by Dr. Ranjan Mishra

## 1. Scope

Exploratory drilling on the southern flank of Block 14 over the period **November 2024 - February 2025**, total **14 holes**, cumulative drilled length **3,168 m**. Targeted the high-grade ferruginous hematite (locally called "blue dust") horizons of the Bailadila Iron Ore Series.

## 2. Resource Profile

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Indicated tonnage | 162 Mt |
| Mean Fe grade | 65.4 % |
| Mean Al₂O₃ | 1.2 % |
| Mean SiO₂ | 6.8 % |
| Mean P | 0.054 % |

The mean Fe grade of **65.4 %** at Bailadila is materially higher than the Noamundi v3 estimate (62.8 %) — a function of the Bailadila series being among the highest-grade iron deposits in India.

## 3. Mining Approach

Open-cast operation feasible. Recommended bench height: 12 m. The "blue dust" mineralization allows for direct-shipping ore (DSO) without beneficiation, subject to lump-to-fine ratio targeting.

## 4. Constraints

- Forest clearance for the southern flank is **partial** — the western 38 hectares are in a notified forest zone requiring separate clearance under the Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980.
- Tribal land considerations — local consultation under PESA (Panchayats Extension to Scheduled Areas) Act ongoing.
