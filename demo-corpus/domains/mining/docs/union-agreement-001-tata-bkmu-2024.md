# WAGE SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT — TATA STEEL MINING ↔ BHARATIYA KHANIKAMGAR UNION

**Period covered:** FY 2024-25 + FY 2025-26 + FY 2026-27 (3-year wage settlement)
**Effective date:** 1 September 2024
**Parties:** Tata Steel Mining Operations (Management) and Bharatiya Khanikamgar Union (BKMU) — Jharia Chapter

**Negotiations:** Held during the period June-August 2024; final terms agreed 28 August 2024; agreement signed 30 August 2024.

---

## 1. Coverage

This settlement covers all unionized workers (approximately 6,400 employees across the Noamundi, Bokaro, and Jharia Tata Steel operations) for the 3-year period commencing 1 September 2024 and ending 31 August 2027.

## 2. Wage Increases

### Year 1 (FY 2024-25):
- Basic pay: 8.5% increase across all grades.
- Dearness allowance: As per the agreed Q1-2024 baseline, with quarterly adjustments per the Industrial Workers' CPI.
- House Rent Allowance: 12% of basic pay (was 10%).

### Year 2 (FY 2025-26):
- Basic pay: 7.5% increase.
- Performance-linked annual bonus: floor of 1.5 month's basic, ceiling of 2.5 months (was 1-2 months).

### Year 3 (FY 2026-27):
- Basic pay: 7.0% increase.
- Long-service bonus: introduced for employees with >15 years of service — INR 25,000 lump-sum per year of service above 15.

## 3. Other Benefits

### A. Health & Welfare

- Health insurance sum increased from INR 8 lakh family floater → INR 12 lakh.
- Group term life insurance increased from INR 25 lakh → INR 40 lakh.
- Dependent education allowance: INR 12,000/year per dependent child (up to 2).

### B. Pension + Provident Fund

- Provident Fund contribution: 12% (statutory) — no change.
- Defined-benefit pension scheme contributions enhanced by 1.5%.

### C. Safety & Working Conditions

- Mandatory annual safety + first-aid training (paid leave during training).
- Enhanced PPE — full body harnesses, dust masks (FFP3 grade), face shields where applicable.
- Heat-stress allowance: INR 80/shift during summer months (Apr-Jul) at all surface operations.
- Underground worker night-shift premium: 25% (was 20%).

### D. Leave

- Annual leave: 28 days (no change).
- Personal-emergency leave: 5 days (was 3 days).
- Maternity leave per statute (26 weeks); paternity leave introduced (15 days, paid).

### E. Workplace Dispute Resolution

- Bipartite grievance committee at each mine.
- Mediation procedure before arbitration.
- Maximum dispute-resolution timeline: 60 days from filing.

### F. Special Issues (specific to Jharia)

In light of the 30 July 2025 fatal roof collapse at Jharia (incident-005), supplementary safety provisions were agreed via a side letter dated 12 August 2025:
- Enhanced family welfare for fatal-incident next-of-kin.
- Independent safety audit committee with union representation.
- Dedicated rescue team funded by the company.

## 4. Compliance

Both parties commit to:
- No strike action during the agreement period.
- Management commits to no retrenchment beyond normal attrition + retirement.
- Joint communication during operational changes.

## 5. Dispute Resolution if Terms Disputed

Disagreements about interpretation referred to:
1. Bipartite committee (Tata Steel + BKMU).
2. If unresolved, to a tripartite committee with State Labour Commissioner.
3. If still unresolved, to industrial tribunal.
