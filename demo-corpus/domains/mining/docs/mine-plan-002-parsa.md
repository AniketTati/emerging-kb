# MINE PLAN — PARSA COAL BLOCK (18 MTPA, post-corrigendum)

**Plan validity:** 5-year plan (FY 2024-25 through FY 2028-29)

## 2. Mining Method

Open-cast with combined dragline + truck-shovel operation. Three seams (Sm-IIIA, Sm-IIIB, Sm-IV) extracted in sequence with cross-bench transfers.

## 3. Resource

Per drilling-002-parsa-coal: Total 294 Mt across all three seams. This 5-year plan extracts approximately 84 Mt (29% of total resource).

## 4. Equipment

- Draglines: 2× Marion 8050.
- Hydraulic shovels: 12× 350-ton class.
- Haul trucks: 50× 220-ton CAT 793F.
- Drill rigs: 8× rotary blast-hole drills.
- Surface miners: 6× Wirtgen 4200 SM (for selective extraction).

## 5. Coal Handling

- Two primary crushers (existing).
- Coal-handling plant + rail-loading capacity: 18 MTPA matched.

## 6. Manpower

Total: 3,800. Local employment: 85% unskilled, 55-70% skilled (per lease commitments).

## 7. Environmental + Wildlife

- Wildlife corridor protection enhanced per corrigendum (annual WII audit).
- Air-quality + water management per the EC + corrigendum.

## 8. Closure Plan

Submitted separately. Includes biodiversity restoration + tribal welfare.
