## 4. Notes on the Mean-Grade Revision Across Versions

A user of this report should be aware that the **mean Fe grade has been revised upward across each version** of this study:
- v1 (Aug 2024) reported **58.2 %**
- v2 (Feb 2025) reported **61.4 %**
- v3 (this report, Jul 2025) reports **62.8 %**

This progression reflects accumulating sample density and the increased weighting toward the high-grade central core as drilling concentrated there. **The v3 estimate is the operative number** for the mine-plan submission and economic modelling.

The v1 number (58.2 %) is now considered an under-estimate; the v2 number (61.4 %) is an intermediate estimate. **All economic and operational decisions should reference v3 (62.8 %).**

## 5. Mine-Plan Implications

- The deposit is significantly richer than initially modelled. Strip ratio improves correspondingly.
- The Phase-3 high-grade core (BH-21 through BH-32) supports a planned bench cut profile with selective mining of the central core during years 1-7 of operations.
- Beneficiation studies (Tata R&D, Jamshedpur, June 2025) confirm the lower-SiO₂ profile is amenable to direct shipping with minimal upgrading.
