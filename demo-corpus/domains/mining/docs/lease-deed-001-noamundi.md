# MINING LEASE DEED — NOAMUNDI IRON-ORE BLOCK

**Lease ref:** ML-WS-12/2018
**Granted by:** Government of Jharkhand, Department of Mines & Geology
**Granted to:** Tata Steel Mining Operations (a unit of Tata Steel Ltd)
**Effective date:** 15 June 2018 — for a term of **30 years**, expiring 14 June 2048

---

## 1. Lease Area

- **Block:** Noamundi Iron-Ore Block, including Block-A and Block-B (extension area for which v3 drilling has been completed).
- **Total area:** 1,148 hectares.
- **Located in:** West Singhbhum District, Jharkhand.
- **Schedule of boundaries:** Per Schedule-A (survey map attached, not reproduced here). Northern boundary follows National Highway NH-220; eastern boundary the Noamundi forest reserve; southern boundary the railway alignment to Jamshedpur; western boundary as per Jharkhand state demarcation 2017.

## 2. Mineral Authorized

Iron ore (hematite + magnetite + BHQ) and associated minerals.

## 3. Royalty Schedule

| Mineral | Royalty Rate |
|---|---|
| Iron ore, lumps (>10 mm) | 15 % of sale value or such other rate as the Central Government may notify under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act 1957 |
| Iron ore, fines (<10 mm) | 10 % of sale value |
| Iron ore, slimes (<1 mm) | 5 % of sale value |

## 4. Surface Rent

- Forested area: INR 2,000 per hectare per annum.
- Non-forested area: INR 1,200 per hectare per annum.
- Annual surface rent: ~INR 18 lakh (paid annually).

## 5. Production Cap

Subject to the operative Environmental Clearance (currently env-clearance-002-noamundi, allowing up to **45 MTPA** post-amendment from the original 30 MTPA).

## 6. Conditions

(i) Annual Submission of Mining Plan to the Indian Bureau of Mines (IBM); approval by DGMS for safety aspects.
(ii) Submission of monthly returns of production, royalty paid, and persons employed to the state Department of Mines.
(iii) Mandatory environmental and social compliance per the MoEFCC EC conditions.
(iv) **Surrender clause:** State retains right to require surrender of mining rights with 12 months' notice in case of national interest.
(v) **Local employment commitment:** ≥80% of unskilled positions to be filled from local Jharkhand candidates; ≥60% of skilled positions same.
(vi) Annual CSR commitment: ≥2% of net profits per the Companies Act 2013 (Tata Steel commits to additional 1.5% under voluntary policy).

## 7. Renewal

Renewable for one further term of 20 years subject to satisfactory compliance review at end of initial 30-year term.

## 8. Royalty Payment Method

Royalty paid to State Government of Jharkhand quarterly within 30 days of end of quarter.
