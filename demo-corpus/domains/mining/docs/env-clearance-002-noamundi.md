# ENVIRONMENTAL CLEARANCE — NOAMUNDI IRON-ORE BLOCK (EXPANSION)

**Issued by:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India
**File reference:** F.No.J-11015/049/2024-IA.II(M)
**Date:** 5 July 2024

**Granted to:** Tata Steel Mining Operations
**Project type:** Iron-ore mining expansion (Block-B extension)

## 1. Production Capacity

**Maximum production capacity:** **45 MTPA** of iron ore (run-of-mine), up from the previous 30 MTPA — a 50% enhancement.

## 2. Lifespan

Approved expansion mine life: 24 years from commencement (estimated end-of-life ~2048).

## 3. Conditions

(i) Production cap: 45 MTPA.
(ii) Annual overburden removal: ≤105 MBCM/yr at peak.
(iii) Forest restoration: INR 15 crore (additional to baseline restoration fund).
(iv) Air-quality monitoring: 8 ambient stations + 12 in-lease stations.
(v) Water: 2,400 KLD max extraction; zero-discharge mandatory.
(vi) Noise: per the National Ambient Noise Standards.
(vii) Tribal welfare: INR 5 crore/yr to District Mineral Foundation (West Singhbhum).
(viii) Wildlife: No incursion into the Noamundi Reserve Forest buffer (a 200-m buffer from the lease boundary is mandatory).
(ix) Biodiversity Management Plan to be submitted within 12 months of expansion commencement.

## 4. Compliance

- Quarterly compliance reports to MoEFCC, SEIAA Jharkhand, Jharkhand State Pollution Control Board (JSPCB).
- Annual third-party environmental audit.
- Public hearing held: 14 May 2024 at Noamundi Block Office; minutes recorded.

## 5. Validity

This EC is valid for the term of the mining lease (ML-WS-12/2018, until 2048) subject to compliance.
