# SAFETY INCIDENT — CONTROLLED-BLAST MISFIRE

**Incident ID:** AEM-RAJ-2025-INC-002
**Date / time:** 14 August 2025, 13:18 IST
**Location:** Rajpura-Dariba Zinc Prospect — Stope-7 (underground)
**Severity:** Near-Miss (no injury, no property damage)

## 1. Description

During a controlled production blast at the Stope-7 underground working face, **one of fourteen (14) blast holes failed to detonate** when the firing sequence was initiated. The remaining 13 holes detonated as planned.

The failed (un-detonated) hole was identified during the post-blast 15-minute waiting period via visual inspection from the safe-distance observation point. The face was immediately barricaded; all personnel withdrew to a 100-m safe distance.

The unexploded charge was safely disposed of using a secondary detonator placed by the blast supervisor at 14:46 IST, after the prescribed waiting + risk-assessment period.

## 2. Personnel

No personnel were exposed or injured. Standard 100-m exclusion zone was maintained throughout.

## 3. Cause

Investigation by the blast supervisor (with on-site consultation from the explosive supplier — Solar Industries) determined the misfire was due to a faulty electric detonator (manufacturer's batch defect, since acknowledged by Solar Industries).

## 4. Actions Taken

- Faulty batch (Solar Industries batch 2025/SE/0427) — all unused detonators from this batch returned to supplier for replacement (compensation arranged via standard supplier warranty).
- Blast supervisor's incident report submitted to DGMS within 24 hours per Mines Act §23.
- Blast procedure review with blast crew (refresher 16 Aug 2025).

## 5. Status

Closed (20 Aug 2025). DGMS notified; no further action required by DGMS. Solar Industries has issued a fleet-wide recall of the affected batch (12-page recall notice circulated 23 Aug 2025).
