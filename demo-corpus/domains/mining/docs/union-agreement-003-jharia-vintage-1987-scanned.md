## **वेतन समझौता ज्ञापन / WAGE SETTLEMENT MEMORANDUM**

**Date / दिनांक:** 4 December 1987 (4 दिसंबर 1987)

**Parties:**
**Tata Iron and Steel Company Limited** (now Tata Steel Ltd) — referred to as "the Management"
तथा / and
**Khanikamgar Sangh, Jharia** (हिंदी translit: "Khanikamgar Sangh, Jharia") — referred to as "the Union"

**Coverage:** All workers at the Jharia Coal Block (operational sections; the underground-fire-affected sections are excluded). Approximately 12,400 workers at that time (1987 census).

---

## **1. वैधता-अवधि / Validity Period**

This Agreement shall be in force for **3 years** from 1 January 1988 to 31 December 1990.

## **2. वेतन वृद्धि / Wage Increases**

### Year 1 (1988):
- मूल वेतन वृद्धि / **Basic pay increase: 18%** (reflecting the post-Pay-Commission environment of that era).

### Year 2 (1989):
- मूल वेतन वृद्धि / Basic pay increase: 15%.

### Year 3 (1990):
- मूल वेतन वृद्धि / Basic pay increase: 12%.

## **3. महंगाई-भत्ता / Dearness Allowance**

Adjusted quarterly per the All-India Consumer Price Index (CPI).

## **4. कोयला आवंटन / Coal Allocation**

Each worker is entitled to **400 kg of coal per month** at the deeply discounted rate of Rs. 0.50 per kg (vs the prevailing market rate of approximately Rs. 4-6 per kg at that time). This is in addition to wages.

## **5. आवास सुविधा / Housing**

Tata Steel commits to providing pucca housing units for **75% of workers** within the planning horizon (1990). For those not yet provided housing, monthly HRA of Rs. 280 per worker.

## **6. स्वास्थ्य सेवा / Health Services**

- Free medical treatment at TMH Jamshedpur for self + dependents.
- Tuberculosis-screening program (era-appropriate priority).
- Respiratory-disease compensation per the Coal Mines Pneumoconiosis Scheme.

## **7. शिक्षा-भत्ता / Education Allowance**

- Per child per annum: **Rs. 600** (for up to 3 children).
- Subsidized primary school + secondary school operated by Tata Education Trust.

## **8. सुरक्षा / Safety**

[Section partially illegible — OCR confidence 0.5]
[Interpreted text]: Safety committee with equal representation; weekly safety meetings; provision of personal protective equipment per the then-current Mines Rules.

## **9. विवाद समाधान / Dispute Resolution**

बातचीत → लेबर कमिश्नर → औद्योगिक ट्रिब्यूनल / Negotiation → Labour Commissioner → Industrial Tribunal.

## **10. कार्य-घंटे / Working Hours**

8-hour shift × 3 shifts per day. Overtime at 1.5× basic for first 4 hours; 2× thereafter.

## **11. आराम के दिन / Rest Days**

One weekly rest day. 28 days annual leave (after 1 year of service).
