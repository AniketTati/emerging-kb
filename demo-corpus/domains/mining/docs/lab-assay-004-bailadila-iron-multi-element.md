# MULTI-ELEMENT ASSAY — BAILADILA IRON-ORE BLOCK 14

**Operator:** Tata Steel Mining Operations (joint study with NMDC)
**Block:** Bailadila Iron-Ore Block 14
**Report date:** 25 September 2025
**Lab:** Tata Steel R&D, Jamshedpur (NABL-accredited)

## 1. Sample Set

14 composite samples (one per borehole; see drilling-003 for hole numbers).

## 2. Major Elements (%)

| Element | Mean | Min | Max | Std Dev |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fe | 65.4 | 62.8 | 68.4 | 1.8 |
| SiO₂ | 6.8 | 4.2 | 11.6 | 2.4 |
| Al₂O₃ | 1.2 | 0.6 | 2.1 | 0.4 |
| P | 0.054 | 0.034 | 0.084 | 0.014 |
| S | 0.014 | 0.004 | 0.028 | 0.007 |
| Mn | 0.18 | 0.12 | 0.34 | 0.06 |
| TiO₂ | 0.22 | 0.14 | 0.38 | 0.06 |
| LOI | 1.4 | 0.6 | 2.8 | 0.6 |

The Bailadila mean Fe (65.4%) is **materially higher than Noamundi (63.2 % per lab-assay-001)** — consistent with the Bailadila series being India's highest-grade iron ore range.

## 3. Trace Elements

| Element | Range (ppm) |
|---|---|
| V | 84 - 142 |
| Cr | 14 - 38 |
| Ni | 22 - 48 |
| Cu | 4 - 18 |
| Pb | 8 - 24 |
| Zn | 10 - 32 |

Notably higher vanadium (V) content vs Noamundi — characteristic of the Bailadila ore series.

## 4. Beneficiation Implications

The Bailadila ore is suitable for direct-shipping with minimal upgrading. The lower SiO₂ + Al₂O₃ vs Noamundi means lower slag-forming penalty in blast-furnace operation.
