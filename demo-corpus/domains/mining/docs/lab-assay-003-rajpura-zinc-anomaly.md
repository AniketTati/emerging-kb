# ⚠ ZINC ASSAY — RAJPURA-DARIBA PROSPECT (ANOMALOUS HIGH-GRADE SAMPLE)

**Operator:** Adani Enterprises Mining Division
**Block:** Rajpura-Dariba Zinc-Lead Prospect
**Report date:** 11 August 2025
**Lab:** CMPDI Laboratory, Ranchi (NABL-accredited)

## ⚠ Headline Finding

**Sample BH-RA-17 returned Zn grade of 28.4 %** — anomalous relative to the deposit mean of 5.4% Zn (the average across 22 holes; per drilling-005). This is **5.3 times the deposit average** and warrants:

(i) Re-sampling for confirmation;
(ii) Geological mapping for possible new vein or contamination;
(iii) Follow-up drilling around BH-RA-17.

## 2. QA/QC for BH-RA-17

To rule out contamination or analytical error:
- **Re-assayed** at 3 different sub-intervals from the same core box — all returned Zn 26-29 % (consistent).
- **Cross-validated** by external lab (Hindustan Zinc Ltd's Pantnagar lab) — confirmed Zn 27.8 %.
- **Core inspection:** The original core sample shows visible sphalerite mineralization (the zinc-bearing mineral) with massive textures rather than the disseminated style seen in adjacent holes.

**Conclusion:** The anomaly is **real, not a sampling or analytical artifact**.

## 3. Geological Interpretation (preliminary)

The BH-RA-17 hole appears to have intersected a **previously-unmapped high-grade vein** within the broader Rajpura-Dariba deposit. The vein is approximately 4.2 m wide (based on the core interval), strikes N15°E, and dips 70° to the east.

If this vein extends through the immediately-surrounding area, the **deposit tonnage and grade estimates would be materially upgraded**. Recommended action: drill 4 additional holes within a 50-m radius of BH-RA-17 to map the vein extent.

## 4. Economic Implication

If even a modest extent of the high-grade vein is confirmed, the deposit's economics shift substantially:
- At 28% Zn, the cost-recovery threshold drops by ~70% vs the deposit average.
- Silver byproduct also dramatically enhanced.
- Project NPV could double if a 2-3 Mt high-grade zone is delineated.

## 5. Next Steps

(i) Plan 4 follow-up holes around BH-RA-17 (target Q4 2025 drilling).
(ii) Reserve estimate to remain conservative (use deposit average) until follow-up results.
(iii) Notify Adani Enterprises board + technical committee of the anomaly.
