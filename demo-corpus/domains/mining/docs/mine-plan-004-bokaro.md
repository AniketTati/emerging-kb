# MINE PLAN — BOKARO COAL BLOCK (22 MTPA open-cast)

> ⚠ **DIAGRAM-HEAVY** — bench layouts + dump schematics in original PDF.

**Operator:** Tata Steel Mining Operations
**Block:** Bokaro Coal Block (lease ML-JH-14/2020)
**Plan validity:** 5-year plan (FY 2024-25 through FY 2028-29)

## 2. Mining Method

Open-cast, dragline-supported with truck-shovel for bench advancement. Two principal seams: Sm-IIB (4.2 m thick) and Sm-IIIA (5.4 m thick) — both within Bench-3 through Bench-8 elevation range.

## 3. Special Operational Conditions (added October 2025)

Following the methane explosion at Bench 6 (incident-007-bokaro-methane-explosion, 2 October 2025), the following operational conditions are now in effect:

(i) **Seam re-classified** from Gassy Category I → Category II.
(ii) **Continuous methane monitoring** at all bench faces (24×7 monitoring stations + personal sensors).
(iii) **Pre-shift gas surveys** mandatory across all bench faces, not just newly-opened ones.
(iv) **Wet drilling mandatory** on Bench-4 SE corner and Bench-6 NW corner (the historical gas-rich pockets).
(v) **Bench-6 operations suspended** pending completion of BSAS investigation + DGMS clearance. Resumption expected Q1 2026.

These conditions are subject to amendment via formal plan revision; an interim safety memorandum dated 8 October 2025 supplements the original plan.
