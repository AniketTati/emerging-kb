# MINE PLAN — RAJPURA-DARIBA ZINC PROSPECT (5 MTPA underground)

> ⚠ **DIAGRAM-HEAVY** — underground stope diagrams + level plans in original PDF.

**Operator:** Adani Enterprises Mining Division
**Block:** Rajpura-Dariba (lease ML-RJ-04/2022)
**Plan validity:** 5-year plan (FY 2024-25 through FY 2028-29)
**Method:** Sub-Level Open Stoping (SLOS) underground mining

## 1. Production Plan

| Year | Ore (Mt) | Cumulative Zn produced (kt metal) |
|---|---|---|
| 2024-25 | 2.0 (ramp-up) | 96 |
| 2025-26 | 3.5 | 264 |
| 2026-27 | 5.0 | 504 |
| 2027-28 | 5.0 | 744 |
| 2028-29 | 5.0 | 984 |

## 2. Mining Method

Sub-Level Open Stoping (SLOS) — proven method for steeply-dipping vein-style deposits like Rajpura-Dariba. Each stope is 50 × 25 × 75 m (length × width × height); extraction from upper to lower levels with paste-fill of mined-out stopes.

## 3. Special Considerations — High-Grade Anomaly

The plan as filed (April 2024) was based on the deposit average grade of ~5.4% Zn. **The August 2025 discovery of the high-grade BH-RA-17 vein (lab-assay-003-rajpura-zinc-anomaly, 28.4% Zn)** has prompted a re-evaluation. A revised mine plan incorporating the anomalous high-grade zone is in preparation (target submission Q1 2026).

## 4. Equipment

- LHD (load-haul-dump) units: 4× CAT R1700.
- Underground trucks: 6× CAT AD60.
- Long-hole drills: 4× Sandvik DL410-7.
- Surface concentrator: 5 MTPA throughput (under construction; target commissioning Q2 2025).

## 5. Concentrator

Conventional sulfide flotation flowsheet — zinc concentrate + lead-silver concentrate. Recovery targets:
- Zinc: 88% recovery, 52% Zn grade in concentrate.
- Lead: 84% recovery, 64% Pb grade in concentrate.
- Silver: 78% recovery.

## 6. Manpower

Total: 1,200 (smaller operation than Noamundi/Parsa).

## 7. Tailings + Water

- Tailings dam per BIS IS-13075. Total storage capacity: 35 Mt.
- Water: 800 KLD max extraction; zero-discharge.
- Annual water audit (per the EC).

## 8. Special Risks

(i) Underground operation — enhanced ventilation + ground support engineering.
(ii) Water-scarce region — strict adherence to water-quota.
(iii) High-grade anomaly investigation — additional drilling Q4 2025.
