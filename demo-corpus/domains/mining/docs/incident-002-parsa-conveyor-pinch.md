# SAFETY INCIDENT — CONVEYOR PINCH-POINT NEAR-MISS

**Incident ID:** AEM-PRS-2025-INC-003
**Date / time:** 18 February 2025, 09:46 IST
**Location:** Parsa Coal Block, coal-haulage conveyor CV-2 (transfer house)
**Severity:** **Near-Miss** (no injury sustained)

## 1. Description

A conveyor operator was performing a routine belt-tension adjustment near the head pulley of conveyor CV-2 when the loose end of his hi-vis jacket sleeve was pulled into the tail-pulley pinch point. The jacket sleeve tore and the operator was pulled approximately 30 cm before the conveyor's safety lanyard (the local emergency-stop pull-cord along the conveyor walkway) was activated by a passing co-worker.

The operator was not injured but reported some bruising and was visibly shaken.

## 2. Personnel

- Operator: Rajesh Kerketta, age 31, Adani Mining direct employee.
- Co-worker who triggered E-stop: Manoj Tigga, age 28, Adani Mining direct employee.

## 3. Immediate Cause

- Operator's loose jacket sleeve unrestrained near a pinch point.
- Belt-tension adjustment performed while the conveyor was running (in violation of lockout-tagout procedure).

## 4. Root Cause

(i) **Lockout-tagout (LOTO) procedure not applied** — belt-tension adjustments are explicitly required to be performed only after locking out drive motors and applying tags.
(ii) **PPE non-compliance:** Loose jacket inadequately fastened. Site standard specifies snug-fit work jackets without loose sleeves near rotating equipment.
(iii) **Supervisor oversight gap:** No supervisor was present to verify LOTO compliance before the work began.

## 5. Corrective Actions

- All conveyor-maintenance crew re-trained on LOTO procedure (mandatory refresher, 20-22 Feb 2025).
- LOTO compliance log introduced for all conveyor maintenance — supervisor sign-off required.
- Site standard amended: snug-fit work jackets mandatory near all rotating equipment.

## 6. Status

Closed (22 Feb 2025). No DGMS notification triggered (no injury sustained); internal LTIF tracking only.
