# SAFETY INCIDENT — FALL FROM HEIGHT

**Incident ID:** TSM-NOA-2024-INC-008
**Date / time:** 4 December 2024, 14:08 IST
**Location:** Noamundi Iron-Ore Block, Bench-3 (Block-A old section)
**Severity:** Lost-Time Injury (LTI)
**Reported by:** Naresh Soren, Safety Officer

## 1. Description

A maintenance technician fell ~3.2 m from the upper inspection platform of haul-truck No. 47 (Belaz BelAZ-7555, 130-ton dump truck) onto the maintenance bay floor while performing a hydraulic-line inspection. Fall arrested partially by overhead toolbox rack.

## 2. Injuries

- Technician: Bhuvan Murmu, age 34, Tata Steel direct employee (Maintenance dept, Noamundi).
- Diagnosis: Fractured right radius + ulna (mid-shaft, Grade III). Contusions to right thigh + flank. No head injury.
- Hospital admission: TMH Jamshedpur (Tata Main Hospital); inpatient 4 days; surgical fixation of forearm.
- Estimated time off work: 45 days (full duty); 14 days (light duty).

## 3. Immediate Cause

The fall occurred when the technician stepped on a wet section of the upper inspection platform; lack of secondary fall protection (no harness anchor used).

## 4. Root Cause

- **PPE compliance:** Fall-arrest harness was available but not worn — technician was wearing only a regular safety belt without harness anchor.
- **Wet platform:** Earlier hydraulic-line drain operation left residual oil + water on the upper deck.
- **Pre-task risk assessment:** Not formally conducted for this routine inspection task.

## 5. Actions Taken

- Bench-3 maintenance bay closed for 24 hours pending cleanup + barrier installation.
- All maintenance technicians re-trained on harness + anchor-point use (1-day refresher, 6-9 Dec 2024).
- Bay flooring updated with anti-slip coating (completed 12 Dec 2024).
- Toolbox-task risk assessment process introduced for routine maintenance tasks.

## 6. Cost Impact

- Medical: INR 1.8 lakh (covered by employee health insurance).
- Equipment downtime: 1 day haul-truck unavailable.
- Investigation + remediation: INR 1.2 lakh.

## 7. Status

Closed. Compliance verified via internal audit 22 December 2024. DGMS notified as Lost-Time Injury; no further action required.
