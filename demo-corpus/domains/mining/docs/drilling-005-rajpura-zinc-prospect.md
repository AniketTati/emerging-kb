# DRILLING REPORT — RAJPURA-DARIBA ZINC PROSPECT

**Operator:** Adani Enterprises Mining Division
**Block:** Rajpura-Dariba Zinc-Lead Prospect (Rajsamand district, Rajasthan)
**Report date:** 22 May 2025
**Prepared by:** CMPDI, Geologist team

## 1. Scope

Exploratory drilling on the Rajpura prospect — **22 holes** drilled between January and May 2025, total drilled length **4,860 m**. Holes targeted the Proterozoic Rajpura-Dariba volcanogenic massive-sulphide (VMS) zinc-lead-silver mineralization.

## 2. Resource Profile

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Indicated tonnage | 28 Mt |
| Mean Zn grade | 5.4 % |
| Mean Pb grade | 1.8 % |
| Mean Ag content | 86 g/tonne |
| Mean Cu grade | 0.4 % |

## 3. Highlights

- Multi-element mineralization with Zn dominant.
- 4 holes returned **anomalously high Zn grades (>15 %)** — these were further investigated and one sample (BH-RA-17) showed an extreme **28.4 % Zn outlier**, possibly representing a previously-unsampled high-grade vein (see also lab-assay-003).
- Vertical extent of mineralization: 80-180 m below surface, dipping ~65° to the NW.

## 4. Mining Approach

Underground stope mining recommended given the dip + grade profile. Sub-level open stoping (SLOS) is the preferred method.

## 5. Recommendations

- Initiate scoping-level feasibility study for a 1.5-2 MTPA underground operation.
- Further drilling needed at the anomalous high-grade zone (BH-RA-17 vicinity) — possible expansion of the deposit.
