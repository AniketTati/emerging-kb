# MINE PLAN — JHARIA COAL BLOCK (SEALED-SECTION REVIVAL)

> ⚠ **DIAGRAM-HEAVY** — underground level diagrams + room-and-pillar layouts in original PDF.

**Operator:** Tata Steel Mining Operations
**Block:** Jharia Coal Block — sealed-off section (Block South-East 4)
**Plan validity:** 5-year plan, contingent on revival approval

> **Note:** This is a **revival proposal** for the abandoned 1986 section. It is **NOT** the currently-operating Jharia section (where the fatal roof collapse occurred — see incident-005-jharia-fatal-roof-collapse). The sealed-section revival is a long-term option pending all clearances + safety reviews.

## 1. Background

The Sm-IIA seam in the sealed-off SE-4 section of Jharia was abandoned in 1986 due to underground fire (now controlled — subsidence stable since 2018, per drilling-006-jharia-coal-revival).

This plan proposes a phased revival:
- Phase 1 (Y1-2): Atmospheric assessment + ventilation system installation.
- Phase 2 (Y3-4): Limited production from the southern 80 hectares (per drilling-006 §3 — northern 60 hectares too thermally degraded for economic revival).
- Phase 3 (Y5+): Continued operation subject to safety + economic review.

## 3. Critical Safety Considerations

Per drilling-006 §6, the sealed section MUST be approached with:
- Extensive pre-revival gas surveying.
- Re-classified as Gassy Category III.
- Substantially elevated PPE + monitoring.
- Multiple emergency exits.
- Dedicated rescue team on standby.

## 4. Atmospheric Risks

- Methane build-up: potentially severe in sealed sections.
- Residual heat from the 1986 fire: monitoring shows quiescence but periodic verification required.
- Oxygen depletion in some pockets: pre-entry sampling mandatory.
- Carbon monoxide (residual from past combustion): air sampling mandatory.

## 5. Phase 1 Plan (Y1-Y2)

(i) Pilot boreholes for atmospheric sampling (12 holes).
(ii) Inflatable atmosphere-containment seals + controlled air ingress.
(iii) New ventilation shaft (Shaft-3) construction.
(iv) Roof + floor stability re-confirmation via cross-bores.
(v) Ground-water inflow modelling + pumping system design.
(vi) Detailed safety case submission to DGMS.

## 6. Pre-revival Approvals Required

(i) DGMS revival approval (process: 2-3 years estimated).
(ii) MoEFCC EC amendment.
(iii) State Government (Jharkhand) consent.
(iv) Wildlife clearance (if applicable, given the proximity to wildlife corridor — to be assessed).
(v) Tribal council consultation.

## 7. Decision Framework

If at any point the safety case or commercial case fails:
- Phase 1: revival project shelved; sealed section maintained.
- Phase 2: pilot suspended; safety review.
- Phase 3: full operation halted; transition to closure.

## 8. CSR + Community

- Local employment commitment: 90% (sealed-section work involves substantial historical-community engagement).
- Training programs for Jharia-area residents.
- Community development fund: INR 12 crore over 5 years.
