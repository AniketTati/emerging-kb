# ENVIRONMENTAL CLEARANCE — RAJPURA-DARIBA ZINC PROSPECT

**Issued by:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), Government of India
**File reference:** F.No.J-11015/088/2023-IA.II(N)
**Date:** 12 June 2023

**Granted to:** Adani Enterprises Mining Division
**Project type:** Zinc-lead underground mining + associated facilities

## 1. Production Capacity (initial phase)

- Prospecting phase: full prospecting authorized for first 36 months.
- Initial mining cap: **5 MTPA** of zinc-lead ore (run-of-mine).
- Concentrator capacity: matched to mining throughput.

## 2. Lifespan

Mining permitted for 22 years from commencement (subject to renewal of lease).

## 3. Conditions

(i) Production cap as in §1.
(ii) **Water-scarce region:** Maximum water extraction 800 KLD; zero-discharge mandatory.
(iii) Mandatory annual water-audit (third-party).
(iv) Underground mining only — open-cast prohibited (per drilling-005 §4 finding).
(v) Air-quality + dust suppression in concentrator + ore-handling areas.
(vi) Tailings management: Engineered tailings dam meeting BIS code IS-13075 (specifications for tailings storage facilities); annual third-party stability audit.
(vii) Forest clearance: Not required (lease is on barren mineral-rich land; no forest area).
(viii) Local employment: ≥75% local employment in unskilled positions; ≥50% in skilled positions.

## 4. Compliance

Quarterly compliance reports to MoEFCC, Rajasthan State Pollution Control Board, SEIAA Rajasthan.

## 5. Validity

This EC is valid until expiry of the mining lease (ML-RJ-04/2022) — i.e. 2047, subject to compliance.
