# ⚠ SAFETY INCIDENT — METHANE GAS EXPLOSION

**Incident ID:** TSM-BKR-2025-INC-METHANE-001
**Date / time:** **2 October 2025, 17:12 IST**
**Location:** Bokaro Coal Block, **Bench 6 (NW corner)**
**Severity:** Reportable injury (4 injuries, no fatalities) + significant property damage
**Reported by:** Safety Officer, Bokaro Operations

---

## 1. Description

A methane gas explosion occurred at the NW corner of Bench 6 (Bokaro Coal Block) at approximately 17:12 IST on 2 October 2025. The blast occurred during ongoing drilling operations preparing for the next bench cut.

Four workers sustained injuries, none life-threatening. Significant property damage to: one drilling rig (extensively damaged), the overhead lighting array, and the temporary site cabin (partially collapsed roof + interior fire).

## 2. Casualties

| Name | Role | Injuries | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hemanth Mahato | Drill operator | 2nd-degree burns to face + hands; minor shrapnel | Admitted Bokaro General Hospital, 4 days |
| Anand Tudu | Drill helper | Concussion + lacerations | Admitted Bokaro General; discharged Day 2 |
| Sukhdev Soren | Supervisor | Eardrum perforation; minor burns | Treated as outpatient |
| Nilesh Mahanta | Contractor (rig owner's mechanic) | Minor lacerations, smoke inhalation | Treated on-site + outpatient observation |

## 3. Property Damage (estimated cost ~INR 84 lakh)

- Drilling rig (Atlas Copco DM30) — extensively damaged; declared total loss.
- Overhead lighting array on Bench 6 — entirely destroyed.
- Site cabin (temporary, prefab) — roof collapse + interior fire; rebuild required.
- Surrounding bench-edge berm — partial damage.

## 4. Immediate Cause (preliminary)

Ignition of a methane-air mixture by either:
- Drilling friction sparking, or
- Electrical arc from rig hydraulic-control panel (initial inspection suggests the latter — the electrical panel shows blast-damage radiating outward).

The methane gas accumulation was caused by **gas seepage from the Sm-IIIA seam exposed by the prior bench cut**, combined with **inadequate ventilation** of the bench face (the Bench 6 NW corner is a pocket geometry that traps gas).

## 5. Root Cause (under investigation)

**Critical finding:** A pre-blast bore-hole gas-monitoring report from September 2025 (drilling-004-bokaro-coal-pre-blast) had flagged elevated methane readings (0.8-1.8 % LEL) at the **Bench 4 SE corner**. The report explicitly recommended:

> *"The Bokaro seam should be re-classified from 'Gassy Category I' to 'Gassy Category II' under DGMS classification. Mandatory pre-shift gas surveys across all bench faces, not just newly-opened ones."*

This recommendation was **NOT IMPLEMENTED** at Bench 6 prior to the explosion. The Bench 6 face had no continuous gas monitoring in place; pre-shift gas surveys were performed only at the active drilling locations, not across the broader face.

**Contributory factors:**

(i) **Seam re-classification not actioned** between Sep 2025 (when flagged) and Oct 2025 (incident). The 26-day window was insufficient for the operational change to propagate.

(ii) **Pocket-geometry ventilation** — Bench 6 NW corner has a topographic depression that allows methane to accumulate; this was not identified in the standard ventilation modelling.

(iii) **PPE coverage** — Workers had standard PPE but no portable methane sensors. Standard practice on Gassy Category II faces requires personal methane sensors.

(iv) **Maintenance gap** — The electrical panel on the rig had a service record gap of 4 months; routine inspection schedules had been deferred.

## 6. Statutory Notifications

- DGMS notified at 17:42 IST (within 30 minutes of incident).
- District Magistrate, Bokaro notified.
- Bokaro General Hospital notified to expect patients.

## 7. Immediate Actions

(i) Bench 6 operations suspended pending investigation.
(ii) All Bokaro bench faces immediately equipped with continuous methane monitoring (achieved within 24 hours via mobile gas-detector deployment).
(iii) Sectoral seam reclassification to **Gassy Category II** formally requested from DGMS (notified 4 Oct 2025).
(iv) All workers issued personal methane sensors (delivery completed 8 Oct 2025).
(v) Independent investigation by Bharat Safety Audit Services commissioned (3 Oct 2025).

## 8. Investigation Timeline

- 3 Oct 2025: BSAS investigation initiated.
- 16 Oct 2025: Preliminary site assessment complete.
- Target: 30-day preliminary report (by 1 Nov 2025).
- Target: Full 90-day report (by 31 Dec 2025).
