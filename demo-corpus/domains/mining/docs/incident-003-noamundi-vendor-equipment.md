# SAFETY INCIDENT REPORT — Tata Steel Mining (Noamundi)

**Incident ID:** TSM-NOA-2025-INC-014
**Date / time of incident:** 9 April 2025, 11:24 IST
**Block / location:** Noamundi Iron-Ore Block — Phase-3 drilling area, near hole BH-26.
**Severity classification:** Reportable injury (Lost-Time Injury — LTI).
**Reported by:** Naresh Soren, Safety Officer
**Investigated by:** Naresh Soren + Manish Deshmukh (Chief Mining Engineer)
**Cross-reference:** DGMS inspection report TSM-DGMS-2025-NOA-007 (see incident-003-dgms-inspection-report).

---

## 1. Brief Description

A hydraulic-line failure on a contractor-operated drilling rig (Atlas Copco DM45 ROC) released pressurized hydraulic oil at approximately 11:24 IST, striking a contractor employee (driller's helper) in the upper torso and face. The employee sustained second-degree burns and a lacerated forearm. He was evacuated to Apollo Hospitals Pune and admitted; treated as inpatient for 3 days; expected to recover fully.

## 2. Equipment Involved

- **Drilling rig:** Atlas Copco DM45 ROC, registration **MGS-DR-2023-094**.
- **Owner / operator:** **Mahalaxmi Drilling & Geo-Services Pvt Ltd** (contractor; engaged via Tata Steel Mining sub-contracting agreement, dated 12 March 2024).
- **Rig last maintenance check:** 28 February 2025 (per contractor's logbook).
- **Hose age:** 18 months (per contractor's records).

## 3. Personnel Involved

- **Injured:** Mukesh Singh, Driller's Helper (employee of Mahalaxmi Drilling, not direct Tata Steel employee).
- **Driller-in-charge:** Vinod Kumar (Field Supervisor, Mahalaxmi Drilling).
- **Tata Steel duty supervisor:** R. Anand (no injury).
- **First responder:** Tata Steel Safety Officer N. Soren, on-site within 6 minutes of incident.

## 4. Sequence of Events

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 11:18 | Crew restarted rig after 20-minute meal break. Pressure-up to ~140 bar. |
| 11:21 | Driller noted slight pressure fluctuation on main panel; logged as a transient. No shutdown. |
| 11:24 | **High-pressure hydraulic line (between main pump and rotary head) ruptured.** Oil released as fine mist + jet impinging on the driller's helper, who was repositioning the cuttings sample box ~2 m from the rig. |
| 11:25 | Driller hit emergency stop; pressure released. |
| 11:27 | First aid administered; calls to safety + medical (Apollo Pune ambulance). |
| 11:48 | Ambulance arrived; patient evacuated. |

## 5. Immediate Cause

Rupture of a hydraulic hose at the swaged-fitting junction. Visual inspection of the failed hose post-incident shows:
- The hose itself shows no obvious wear; rated for 350 bar working pressure (operating 140 bar = well within rating).
- The fitting (swaged crimp) shows uneven crimping with one wire-braid layer protruding. This is consistent with a non-OEM or improperly-crimped replacement, possibly during a prior contractor-maintenance event.

## 6. Root Cause (preliminary)

A. **Inadequate quality control on hose-fitting replacement** by the contractor (Mahalaxmi Drilling). Replacement hose dated June 2024; original Atlas Copco OEM-spec record is missing from the rig's maintenance log.

B. **Lack of hose-replacement traceability** in the contractor's records. Contractor cannot produce the supplier invoice for the June 2024 replacement, nor confirm whether the hose was OEM or a generic equivalent.

C. The pressure fluctuation at 11:21 was a precursor signal that was logged but not acted upon. Crew procedure should have triggered a hard-stop and inspection at any pressure anomaly.

## 7. Contributory Factors

- **Contractor's onboard maintenance logbook gaps:** Multiple maintenance events in 2024 reference replacement parts without invoice attachments.
- **Safety briefing:** The day's pre-start briefing did NOT explicitly cover hose-failure response (per contractor's briefing log).
- **PPE adequacy:** Standard issue safety glasses + jacket; insufficient for high-pressure-oil mist exposure (face shield + flame-retardant outer layer would have been more protective).

## 8. Recommendations / Actions

**Immediate (within 7 days):**
1. ✅ Drilling rig DM45 ROC removed from service; alternative rig (Atlas Copco DM30) mobilised by contractor — completed 14 Apr 2025.
2. ✅ All hydraulic hoses on Mahalaxmi-operated rigs at Noamundi to be re-inspected by Atlas Copco-certified technician — completed 18 Apr 2025; 4 hoses flagged for replacement.
3. ✅ Contractor to produce OEM invoices for all hose replacements over the past 24 months — outstanding as of report date.

**Short-term (within 30 days):**
1. Re-train contractor crew on hydraulic-system maintenance and pre-start checks. ✅ Done 25 Apr 2025.
2. Upgrade PPE for all drilling-crew personnel — face shields + FRC outer-layer mandatory.

**Medium-term:**
1. Tata Steel Mining to issue a contractual compliance audit of Mahalaxmi Drilling — formal letter sent 30 Apr 2025; on-site audit planned for July 2025.
2. Update sub-contracting agreement (dated 12 Mar 2024) to require OEM-only hose replacements with auditable provenance.
3. **DGMS notified per Mines Act §23 and Mines Rules 1955** — DGMS inspection report follows (see incident-003-dgms-inspection-report).

## 9. Cost Impact

- Medical: ~INR 2.4 lakh (covered under contractor's workmen-compensation policy).
- Equipment downtime: 5 days of Phase-3 drilling delayed. Estimated cost: INR 18 lakh in delay-equivalent.
- Investigation + remediation: INR 4 lakh.
- **Total estimated cost: ~INR 24 lakh.**
