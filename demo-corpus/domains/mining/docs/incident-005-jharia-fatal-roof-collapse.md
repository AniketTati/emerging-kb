# ⚠ FATAL SAFETY INCIDENT REPORT — JHARIA UNDERGROUND ROOF COLLAPSE

**Incident ID:** TSM-JHR-2025-INC-FATAL-001
**Date / time:** **30 July 2025, 06:42 IST**
**Location:** Jharia Coal Block — currently-operating section (NOT the sealed-off revival area), Sub-level 4, sub-block South-3
**Severity:** **FATAL — 3 fatalities + 2 hospitalizations**
**Reported by:** Mine Manager, Jharia (immediate); investigation lead: Naresh Soren (Tata Steel Safety) + Manish Deshmukh (Chief Mining Engineer)
**Statutory:** Notified to DGMS within 30 minutes per Mines Act §23

---

## 1. Description

A section of the immediate roof above a sub-level working face (approximately 18 m long × 4 m wide × 0.8-1.2 m thick) collapsed at 06:42 IST on 30 July 2025, during the early-morning shift. The collapse buried five (5) workers who were operating a continuous miner at the face.

Two workers were rescued alive within 2 hours (with crush injuries, see §3); three workers did not survive.

This is the **most severe single-event incident at any Tata Steel Mining operation in the past 8 years**. The full investigation is conducted jointly with DGMS, with a preliminary report to follow within 30 days.

## 3. Immediate Cause (preliminary)

Catastrophic failure of the immediate roof along a previously-unidentified fault plane. The fault appears to have been triggered or aggravated by:
- Overburden water-table loading (the prior week's monsoon rainfall was 220% above normal),
- Reduced roof-bolt density in this sub-block (the area had been previously identified as competent and bolt density was below the corpus norm),
- Possible micro-seismic activity (regional seismic data being reviewed).

A preliminary inspection of the post-collapse face suggests that the fault was undetected during routine geotechnical mapping. The fault plane has a high-angle dip (~75° from horizontal) and was masked by superficial intact roof above the bolted zone.

## 4. Root Cause (under investigation)

The full root-cause analysis is being conducted jointly with DGMS and an independent panel. Preliminary working hypotheses:

(i) **Geotechnical mapping gap** — the fault plane was not identified during the most recent (Q2 2025) sub-block geotechnical survey. Possible due to:
   - Limited core sampling in the immediate roof zone;
   - Insufficient cross-section spacing;
   - Time pressure to advance the development heading.

(ii) **Roof support inadequacy** — bolt density in this sub-block was below the corpus norm (1.0 bolt/m² vs the standard 1.4 bolt/m²). The original assessment for this zone marked it as "competent rock — reduced bolt density acceptable." This assessment is now under review.

(iii) **Hydrological loading** — the unusual monsoon rainfall in July 2025 (220% of normal) may have accelerated water-table loading on the roof strata. Pre-monsoon hydrological modelling did not foresee this.

(iv) **Pre-shift inspection gap** — the routine pre-shift roof inspection was performed at 06:00 (40 minutes before the incident) and reported no abnormalities. Either the failure was too rapid to be detected by visual inspection, or the inspection protocol needs revision.

## 5. Immediate Actions Taken

(i) **Mine operations suspended** for the entire Jharia working section, effective 30 July 2025 09:00 IST. Other sections of Jharia (the sealed-off section under revival assessment, and adjacent unrelated workings) are NOT affected.

(ii) **DGMS Inspection initiated** — DGMS Inspector A. K. Singh on site from 31 July 2025 onwards.

(iii) **Family welfare** — three deceased workers' families have been provided immediate ex-gratia payments of INR 25 lakh each; two injured workers' families provided interim relief of INR 5 lakh each. Long-term pension scheme + employment-for-dependent provisions activated.

(iv) **External experts engaged** — an independent geotechnical expert panel chaired by Prof. R. Misra (IIT Dhanbad) constituted to conduct a parallel assessment.

(v) **Resumption protocol drafted** — no resumption of underground operations in the affected section until: (a) DGMS clearance, (b) independent panel sign-off, (c) Tata Steel internal safety review committee approval.
