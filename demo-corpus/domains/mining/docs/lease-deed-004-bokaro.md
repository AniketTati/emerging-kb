# MINING LEASE DEED — BOKARO COAL BLOCK

**Lease ref:** ML-JH-14/2020
**Granted by:** Government of Jharkhand, Department of Mines & Geology
**Granted to:** Tata Steel Mining Operations
**Effective date:** 8 March 2020 — term of **20 years**, expiring 7 March 2040

## 1. Lease Area
- **Block:** Bokaro Coal Block (West Bokaro coalfield), Bench-1 through Bench-8.
- **Total area:** 824 hectares.
- **Located in:** Bokaro District, Jharkhand.

## 2. Mineral Authorized
Non-coking thermal coal — Sm-IIB and Sm-IIIA seams.

## 3. Royalty
14% of sale value or such other rate as notified under MMDR Act.

## 4. Production Cap
22 MTPA per the operative environmental clearance (separately granted).

## 5. Annual Surface Rent
INR 14 lakh per annum.

## 6. Special Conditions

(i) **Gas classification:** Bench 4 and Bench 6 are classified as **Gassy Category II** (revised from initial Category I in October 2025 following the gas-monitoring findings from drilling-004 + the methane incident at Bench 6).
(ii) Mandatory continuous methane monitoring during all drilling and blasting operations.
(iii) Quarterly third-party safety audit (currently Bharat Safety Audit Services — BSAS).
(iv) Special "wet drilling" requirement on Bench 6 to suppress dust + gas release.

## 7. Renewal
One renewal of 10 years subject to compliance.
