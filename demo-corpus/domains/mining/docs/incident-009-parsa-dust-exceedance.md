# SAFETY INCIDENT — RESPIRABLE DUST EXCEEDANCE (Occupational Health)

**Incident ID:** AEM-PRS-2025-INC-007
**Date / time:** 22 June 2025 (8-hour shift exceedance event)
**Location:** Parsa Coal Block — Bench 3 drilling area
**Severity:** Occupational health concern (no acute injury); chronic-exposure mitigation triggered

## 1. Description

Routine occupational-health monitoring (in line with Mines Rules 1955 §40A and DGMS guidance) detected respirable dust concentration exceeding the action threshold at the Bench 3 drilling face during the day shift on 22 June 2025.

**Measured concentration:** 7.4 mg/m³ (8-hour TWA)
**Statutory limit:** 3.0 mg/m³ (8-hour TWA) — per DGMS Coal Mines Air-Quality Standard
**Exceedance factor:** 2.5×

## 2. Affected Personnel

Eight (8) workers in the immediate Bench 3 drilling area were exposed during the shift. All eight subsequently underwent:
- Spirometry baseline (clinically normal in all 8).
- Chest X-ray (no abnormality in 6; 2 referred for further evaluation — Adani is funding the additional medical workup).
- Inclusion in the long-term occupational-health surveillance roster.

## 3. Immediate Cause

(i) **Dust suppression system failure** — the water-spray system on the drilling rig (RIG-PRS-002) had a clogged nozzle bank; ~40% of nozzles were not delivering water during the shift.
(ii) **Dust monitoring system delay** — the personal-air-sampler data is downloaded at end-of-shift, not real-time; the exceedance was not detected during the shift, only post-shift.

## 4. Root Cause

(i) **Maintenance gap** — dust-suppression water-spray nozzles require weekly cleaning + bi-monthly nozzle replacement. The maintenance log for RIG-PRS-002 shows the last nozzle service was 9 weeks prior (vs the required 4-week interval).
(ii) **Real-time monitoring absence** — DGMS now recommends real-time respirable-dust monitoring (vs end-of-shift TWA). Parsa does not yet have this technology deployed.

## 5. Corrective Actions

- All drilling rigs at Parsa subjected to dust-suppression system check + nozzle replacement (completed 25 Jun 2025).
- Dust-suppression maintenance schedule revised — weekly mandatory.
- Real-time respirable-dust monitoring system procurement initiated (target deployment Q4 2025).
- Long-term occupational-health surveillance for the 8 exposed workers — annual chest imaging + spirometry for 10 years.

## 6. Statutory Notification

DGMS notified per Mines Rules 1955 §40A within 7 days (notification date: 26 Jun 2025). DGMS review in progress.

## 7. Status

Open — pending DGMS review + completion of real-time monitoring deployment.
