### ⚠ Notable: PM₁₀ Exceedance

In-lease PM₁₀ mean (108 µg/m³) **exceeded the NAAQS limit (100 µg/m³)** during Q2. Drilling operations + haul-road traffic during the dry pre-monsoon weeks of May 2025 were the principal sources.

**Mitigation actions taken during Q2:**
- Increased water-tanker frequency on haul roads (from 6 to 10 passes per shift).
- Dust-suppression spray system on drilling rigs serviced.
- Mandatory wet drilling on Phase-3 boreholes.

**Mitigation actions planned for Q3:**
- Continued enhanced water-tanker frequency.
- Drilling-rig wet-drilling extended to all boreholes.
- New dust-suppression chemical foam additive trial (3-month pilot).
