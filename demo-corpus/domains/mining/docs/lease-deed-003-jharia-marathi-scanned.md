# जमीन-कलम-कार्यानुयन — कोल खान कलम-पट्टा / MINING LEASE DEED

(English translation provided below each Marathi/Hindi section)

---

**जमीन-कलम-संख्या / Lease Reference Number:** ML-CO-JH/1989/0042
**[Stamp paper, Government of Bihar, INR 200/-]**

**तपशील:** बिहार राज्य सरकार, खनिज विभाग / Issued by: Government of Bihar (then), Department of Mines

**अनुरूप:** टाटा आयरन एंड स्टील कंपनी लि. (वर्तमान टाटा स्टील लि.) / Granted to: Tata Iron and Steel Company Ltd (now Tata Steel Ltd)

**अवधि / Term:** **30 [तीस] वर्षाची / thirty years**, dated effective from 12 April 1989.

**विस्तृत क्षेत्र / Area:** Jharia Coalfield, Block-North-7 — admeasuring **412 हेक्टेयर / 412 hectares** approximately. (Note: by present-day GIS surveys this is actually 408.4 hectares; the 1989 measurement has marginal error.)

---

## **1. खनिज प्रकार / Mineral Type**

कोयला, थर्मल विविधता / **Coal, thermal grade (non-coking).**

## **2. रॉयल्टी / Royalty**

मे रन-ऑफ-माइन कोयला / On run-of-mine coal: **Rs. 12 per tonne** [per the rates applicable in 1989 — subsequently revised by Central Government notifications].

## **3. वार्षिक भू-भाड़ा / Annual Surface Rent**

forest land: रू 25 प्रति एकर प्रति वर्ष / Rs. 25 per acre per annum.
non-forest land: रू 15 प्रति एकर प्रति वर्ष / Rs. 15 per acre per annum.

## **4. ठेके की शर्तें / Conditions**

(i) पाँच वर्ष में मिनिंग प्लान जमा कराना अनिवार्य / Mining Plan to be submitted within 5 years.
(ii) [OCR confidence: 0.4 — possibly: "Each calendar year submit production return to Mines Department, Patna"]
(iii) सुरक्षा अधिनियम 1952 का पालन / Compliance with Mines Act 1952.
(iv) [Margin note in handwriting, partially illegible]: *"... required pump capacity 200 cu.m/hr ... drainage from sealed sec..." [OCR confidence 0.3]*

## **5. राज्य पुनर्ग्रहण क्लॉज / State Recall Clause**

राज्य सरकार को सूचना देकर वापस ले सकती है / Government of Bihar retains right to recall on 12 months' notice.

## **6. नवीकरण / Renewal**

एक नवीकरण 20 वर्षों के लिए / One renewal of 20 years subject to compliance.
