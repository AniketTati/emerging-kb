# MINING LEASE DEED — PARSA COAL BLOCK

**Lease ref:** ML-CG-08/2019
**Granted by:** Government of Chhattisgarh, Department of Mines & Geology
**Granted to:** Adani Enterprises Mining Division (a division of Adani Enterprises Ltd)
**Effective date:** 21 September 2019 — term of **30 years**, expiring 20 September 2049

---

## 1. Lease Area

- **Block:** Parsa Coal Block, including the Parsa Kente Collieries (PKC) expansion area.
- **Total area:** 1,898 hectares.
- **Located in:** Sarguja District (Hasdeo Arand coalfield region), Chhattisgarh.

## 2. Mineral Authorized

Non-coking thermal coal (Lower Gondwana seams Sm-IIIA, Sm-IIIB, Sm-IV — see drilling-002 for tonnage estimates).

## 3. Royalty Schedule

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Royalty on coal | 14 % of dispatched-coal sale value (as per MMDR Act current rate) |
| Tax on coal cess | Per Coal Mines (Cess) Rules |
| DMF + NMET contributions | 30 % + 2 % of royalty (paid into District Mineral Foundation + National Mineral Exploration Trust) |

## 4. Production Cap

Subject to operative Environmental Clearance — currently **18 MTPA** post-corrigendum (see env-clearance-001-parsa-corrigendum, dated 20 September 2024).

## 5. Annual Surface Rent

INR 28 lakh per annum (forest area: ~62% of lease).

## 6. Statutory Conditions

(i) Forest clearance under Forest (Conservation) Act 1980 — granted concurrently with the lease.
(ii) Wildlife sanctuary buffer compliance — Hasdeo Arand wildlife corridor crosses the eastern margin of the lease; mining operations restricted to the western 1,628 hectares.
(iii) Quarterly safety + environment compliance reports to DGMS, MoEFCC, and JSPCB (Jharkhand State Pollution Control Board has no jurisdiction; CG counterpart is Chhattisgarh Environment Conservation Board — CECB).

> *Note: The reference to JSPCB elsewhere in this corpus refers to Tata Steel's Jharkhand operations; Adani's CG operations report to CECB.*

## 7. Local-Employment Commitment

- Unskilled: ≥85% local employment (Sarguja district + adjacent).
- Skilled: ≥55% local employment, scaling to ≥70% over 5 years.

## 8. Special Conditions

(i) **Mandatory establishment of forest restoration fund** — INR 12 crore deposited with the State Forest Department for off-site afforestation.
(ii) **Tribal welfare commitment** — INR 4 crore per annum to the District Mineral Foundation specifically earmarked for tribal-area development (Sarguja has significant Adivasi population).
(iii) **Wildlife corridor protection** — annual third-party audit; submitted to MoEFCC.

## 9. Renewal

One renewal of 20 years subject to satisfactory compliance.
