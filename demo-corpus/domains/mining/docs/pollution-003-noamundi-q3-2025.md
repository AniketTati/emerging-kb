# QUARTERLY EMISSIONS MONITORING REPORT — NOAMUNDI Q3 2025 (post-mitigation)

**Reporting period:** 1 July 2025 - 30 September 2025
**Submitted:** 15 October 2025

## 1. Air-Quality Summary

| Parameter | In-lease mean | Ambient mean | NAAQS limit | Compliance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PM₁₀ (µg/m³) | **86** | **72** | 100 | ✅ (recovered from Q2) |
| PM₂.₅ (µg/m³) | 41 | 30 | 60 | ✅ |
| SO₂ (µg/m³) | 21 | 11 | 80 | ✅ |
| NO₂ (µg/m³) | 36 | 21 | 80 | ✅ |

### Recovery from Q2 PM₁₀ Exceedance

The Q2 exceedance (in-lease PM₁₀ 108 µg/m³) has been **fully mitigated**. Q3 PM₁₀ readings (in-lease 86 µg/m³, ambient 72 µg/m³) are well within the NAAQS limit and improved from the Q1 baseline.

Mitigation interventions that materially contributed:
1. Enhanced water-tanker frequency on haul roads (sustained from Q2).
2. Wet-drilling on all boreholes (no exceptions).
3. New dust-suppression foam additive (pilot extended; effective).

JSPCB lifted the enhanced compliance monitoring on 5 October 2025.

## 2. Monsoon Impact (July-Sept 2025)

The Q3 reporting period coincides with the monsoon season; natural dust suppression substantially helped the PM recovery. However, the mitigation actions are designed to maintain compliance through the next dry period (Q4 + Q1 2026).

## 3. Water + Discharge

- Total water extracted: 198,420 KL (lower extraction due to monsoon).
- Treated discharge: 0 KL (zero-discharge maintained).
- **Mine-water management note:** Monsoon-period drainage runoff was successfully managed via sedimentation ponds + recycling.

## 4. Solid Waste

- Overburden generated: 26.2 MBCM.
- Overburden backfilled: 22.4 MBCM (86%).

## 5. Compliance

**Overall compliance: ✅ All parameters within statutory limits.** Compliance recovered from the Q2 PM₁₀ exceedance.
